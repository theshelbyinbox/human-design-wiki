# Planets

Every activation in a BodyGraph was put there by a planet. Thirteen celestial bodies are used, each one twice, which gives the twenty-six activations that the whole of a chart is built from. Type, Authority, Profile, Definition and Incarnation Cross are all derived from those positions.

The gate says what the theme is. The line says how it is expressed. The planet says which part of a life that theme belongs to.

## The Sun and the Earth

The heaviest imprint in any chart, always in exact opposition, and the source of Profile and the Incarnation Cross.

- [[Sun]] Personality Expression and Life Force
- [[Earth]] Grounding and Balance

## The Nodes

Not planets but positions, where the path of the Moon crosses the ecliptic. They divide a life into two halves at the Uranus opposition.

- [[North Node]] Future Direction and Environment
- [[South Node]] Past Direction and Environment

## The Personal Planets

Fast movers. These are the placements where a chart becomes individual rather than generational.

- [[Moon]] Driving Force
- [[Mercury]] Communication and Thinking
- [[Venus]] Values and Sociology
- [[Mars]] Immaturity and Energy Dynamics

## The Slow Planets

Slow enough that everyone born within a long window shares the placement. These describe a generation as much as a person, and they form background rather than foreground.

- [[Jupiter]] Law and Protection
- [[Saturn]] Discipline, the Judge, Restraint
- [[Uranus]] Unusualness, Chaos and Order, Science
- [[Neptune]] Illusion, Art, Spirituality
- [[Pluto]] Truth, Transformation, Psychology
