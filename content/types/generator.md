# Generator

The Generator is the most common energy type in [[Human Design System]]. A Generator is anyone born with a defined [[Sacral Center]], and the Sacral is the body's prime motor, the source of the life force itself. Ra Uru Hu put the consequence bluntly: "life on this Earth is created by Generators. It is their work."

"You are the energy beings... You are the life force on the planet... The disarray, the chaos that is the byproduct of it. The incredible frustration that fills this plane." - Ra Uru Hu

What separates the pure Generator from the [[Manifesting Generator]] is not the Sacral but the [[Throat Center]]. There are four motor centers: the Sacral, the [[Root Center]], the [[Heart Center]] and the [[Solar Plexus Center]]. When any of them reaches the Throat, directly or through a chain of definition, the being can initiate manifestation. A pure Generator has no motor connected to the Throat. The generative power is there in full but has no exit of its own, which is why a Generator's manifestation runs through other people and circumstances.

## Population

Sources genuinely disagree, and none of these figures are census data. Counting Generators and Manifesting Generators together, Ra Uru Hu variously said they "represent approximately two-thirds of the human population, just over two-thirds of humanity", "about 67 percent of the human population", and "about 70% of humanity". The Definitive Book of Human Design puts almost 70 percent of the world's population in the category of being guided by defined Sacral Centers; contemporary teaching material gives "about 66 to 70% of the population combined"; and an aura-type teaching document derived from a Ra.TV presentation gives a precise 66.30 percent. The honest summary is a band, not a number.

Counted separately, Ra was specific that "One third of humanity are Manifesting Generators", and contemporary teaching sources give the Generator share as around 37 or 38 percent. A different division cuts across the type split and matters as much: approximately 51 percent of all human beings have a defined Solar Plexus, so that "half of all generators are emotional and half of all generators are not emotional."

## The Sacral as a Motor

The Sacral Center corresponds to the ovaries in a woman and the testes in a man, and its themes are work, reproduction, sexuality and perseverance. Ra described it as a spiral vortex rather than a simple battery: gates crossing inside it, the 9 to the 5, the 29 to the 42, the 14 to the 3, the 27 to the 59, with the 34 coming in too, all of it spinning like a cosmic washing machine. Out of that churn comes the frequency we recognise as life.

Three things distinguish this motor from the other three. Its complexity is second only to the Throat. It is the only center that generates the life force rather than pressure, willpower or feeling. And it is the only center that communicates its own moment-to-moment availability out loud, through its own guttural sounds.

The motor also has a fixed frequency. Ra said "the Sacral Center is a motor, but it doesn't have any gears. The gears are in the Throat", and that Throat and Sacral together are the basic binary of life. Because the frequency is fixed, every Generator enters a thing at one frequency and stays there until that stage is complete, and then arrives at a state of stuckness. Read correctly, stuckness is the sign that metamorphosis is coming. Read through the not-self, the same sensation is frustration and the urge to quit. "It's not metamorphic; it's simply a motor," Ra said. "It's very, very powerful. It's the kind of motor that doesn't give up, that doesn't stop, and that keeps on going."

Ra also described the Sacral in terms of availability: nine gates, nine themes of availability, split between availability for work and availability for sex. Whatever channel defines the Sacral sets the criteria the response answers by. Someone with the 9-52 responds positively only to what they can focus and concentrate on, in a weekend away as much as in a job, and the mind never knows why.

## Sacral Response

The Sacral is the only center with a voice. Ra, a Manifestor with a completely open Sacral, pointed out that his own Ego does not speak; he could not ask it anything. A Generator can. The guttural sounds come out of a guidance system that existed long before the capacity for speech evolved.

That is why the sound precedes the words. Response is mechanical and so pure that it bypasses awareness altogether. It is neither a moral judgement nor an intellectual statement: "The Sacral Center's not intelligent," Ra said. What it says is smaller and more useful. "It's saying: this is not a good thing to use our energy for, or this is a great thing to use our energy in."

The response is coloured by what the Sacral is connected to, because "the way that it responds is determined by what hooks it up." Linked to the [[Splenic Center]], the yes or no reports whether something is healthy or secure in the now. Linked to the Solar Plexus, the emotional system conditions the answer and operates in a wave. Where the definition is individual, the sound itself changes into a droning build up rather than a clean rush to one end or the other.

The common counterfeit is a yes formed in the head and delivered by the Throat. It sounds like agreement and is not response. The sound is the tell, which is why the classic practice is to ask a Generator yes or no questions and listen to what arrives before the sentence does.

Responding is not passivity. It is impossible for a Generator to do nothing, because they are constantly busy generating. Retreating from a dog's growl, smiling when a bird sings, humming along with a tune: all of it is response. "Generators never have to wait long. There's always something to respond to."

## Aura: Open and Enveloping

Auras form a sphere around the body stretching out twice the length of the arms, they pass through walls, floors and ceilings, and where two meet, conditioning takes place. The Generator aura does not merely stay open; it reaches around whoever steps into it. Ra called it an auric bear hug.

> Generators are the key to life on this planet. They literally generate the life force of the planet, and as such, they have the most penetrating, in that sense, of auric fields. A Generator will envelop anyone that steps into its aura. This openness in the Generator is also a vulnerability; that is, in being so open and enveloping, you take deeply into yourself those that you allow into your aura. - Ra Uru Hu

What the aura does to others is physical. Non-sacral beings standing in the field feel the buzz and briefly borrow energy that is not theirs to sustain. A format energy carried in a defined Sacral conditions whole rooms: with a 9-52 present in a classroom, everybody is more focused and concentrated. The Generator rarely notices.

The aura also does the Generator's negotiating. Ra's advice was to let the aura do the talking, because it carries an energy that says, in effect, "you better initiate with me because I'm here to respond." What the Generator takes in, though, they amplify and put back out. Put enough frustrated Generators together, Ra said, and "the patina of frustration is so thick it's uncomfortable." This is one reason non-sacral beings are told to sleep in their own aura.

## Strategy: Respond

The Generator [[Strategy]] is to respond. Nothing is initiated from the mind's agenda; the environment provides the cue and the Sacral provides the answer. For this type it means the right work, the right people and the right life are not sought but recognised when they arrive. Ra's formulation was that a Sacral being comes into the world to find the right work, and "it's only once you've found the right work that you get your life." The mechanic itself is covered in [[Respond]].

## Authority

Generators with a defined Solar Plexus carry [[Emotional Authority]]. For them the response is not a decision but information gathered toward one made over time, because "There is no truth in the now." Ra's instruction to Emotional Generators was to be hard to get and make the other party wait. It is not rejection, it is respect, and it has a practical dividend: the other party values you, and they make you a better offer.

Generators without a defined Solar Plexus carry [[Sacral Authority]] and are correct in the moment or not at all. There is no wave to wait inside and no accompanying explanation. They get the answer and never get the reason.

## Signature: Satisfaction

The [[Signature]] of the Generator is satisfaction. "Generators are here to find satisfaction. You only find satisfaction through the correct use of your energy." Satisfaction is not a mood; it is the readout of a motor used the way it was built to be used. Ra described the end of a correct day as the exhaustion of joy. A satisfied Generator in a room will draw a crowd, because the field they broadcast changes with them.

## Not-Self Theme: Frustration

The not-self theme is frustration, and it is chemical before it is psychological: the center is not doing what it exists to do.

The spiral has a shape. Conditioned to go out and make it happen, the Generator initiates. Because the motor was never engaged by a response, the energy gets stuck, the mind compensates by pushing harder, the work does not finish, and the Generator concludes the thing is not for them and walks away. "They're the great quitters," Ra said of initiating Generators, and the last thing to occur to them is to respond. The same spiral appears at the top of a completed level: the Generator feels the stuckness and pushes for the transition instead of letting it arrive. The first thing that happens when a Generator plays Manifestor is that they get smacked, and somebody gets to control them.

What breaks the spiral is not insight, because the mind is not on the Generator's side. It is the mechanical return to response, which is what the question and response episode is for: someone asks a long series of yes or no questions, forcing the Sacral back into work it has not done in decades.

## Energy, Sleep and Burnout

The Sacral requires that its available energy be exhausted each day. Generators are meant to burn the day down and go to bed when they are done, then wake replenished. "The essence of being a Generator is that if you're using your energy properly you reach a point of exhaustion each day that's perfect for you." Ra suggested a Generator raised entirely correctly would need very little sleep, perhaps extended naps two or three times a day.

Where this goes wrong is not overwork but misdirected work. If the motor is forced to do what it did not respond to, its generative capacity becomes degenerative and burnout results; a misused Sacral atrophies prematurely, leaving the person frustrated and unhealthy. "Most Generators, when they get into bed at night, their motor has not been used properly that day." That is the origin of the sleeplessness, and Ra held that most sleeping problems belong to Generators, far beyond their share of the population.

## Work, Mastery and Repetition

Generators are the only type designed to master something, and mastery goes level by level, each stage completed before the next. The plateaus between levels are not obstacles but the shape of a fixed-frequency motor.

This is why repetition suits Generators as it suits no one else. Sustained work in something they love is nourishment rather than endurance, which is also how they become exploited: employers learn quickly that a Generator will work until they drop for the thing they love, and will keep working long after they have stopped loving it.

Ra drew a sharp line about motive: "no Generator ever works for money. They work for the pleasure of working." The money is secondary, a byproduct of how well the energy is used.

## The Generator Child

"I have a deep concern for Generator children. I'm more interested in them quite frankly, than I am in you," Ra told an audience of adults. A child raised according to its nature never has to be deconditioned.

The conditioning begins with the sounds. Generator children answer with the Sacral voice and are told it is not polite, that they should use words, that they should not make noises at the table. "It's repressed. It's been repressed by the fact that your parents want a Manifestor. They don't want a grunter." And more broadly: "They didn't ask you, they told you."

A Generator raised by Generators absorbs the same instruction to go out and make it happen, learns to initiate from the mind, and loses contact with the mechanism. What is left, Ra said, is like a beautiful car left in a garage for twenty or thirty or forty years: "The Generator response mechanism is deeply repressed. It's not like it's just sitting there waiting to be activated."

The corrective is small and consistent. Ask rather than tell. Give them choices, so the child can run each option past the Sacral. Give the energy an outlet. And do not impose a bedtime: forcing a Generator child to sleep before the motor has run down is deeply unhealthy, and is met with resistance.

## Generators in Relationship

Ra held that Generators are the only type designed to be personally honest, because the Sacral answers without adornment. Ask a Generator whether they love you and the answer is a sound. There is nothing to interpret and nothing to argue with. "You can't reason with a grunt."

The difficulty is what the aura advertises. Others feel the available power of a defined Sacral and, in Ra's blunt framing, recognise slave material. When a conditioned Generator starts answering from the Sacral, the people who relied on the automatic yes do not like it, and some of them leave. That is a normal cost of correctness. Availability is the mechanic of intimacy too: a Generator is only genuinely available within their own pattern, and a life of saying yes outside it is evidence of deep conditioning.

One pairing dominates human life. Generators at roughly 70 percent and [[Projector]] types at roughly 20 percent represent about 90 percent of the species between them, an enveloping aura and a focused one in constant contact.

## Deconditioning for a Generator

Deconditioning runs seven years, with a real change in the body and its chemistry at around three and a half, the point at which a Generator is more who they are than what they are not.

The obstacle is the mind. "Generator minds are the toughest minds to break," Ra said, because the Generator mind has held the most authority of any type, and it will fight for the full seven years. Reasoning with it does not work. "Your mind must become your ally," and the way to recruit it is to give it something it likes: research, and the question and response experiment, through which it slowly hands authority back to the Sacral.

Past the halfway point comes a wall of fear. Honouring the Sacral means arriving at a place where you do not know what is going to happen to you, and the mind's last gasp is to invent the most implausible fears to make that unbearable. The anxiety has to be processed, sometimes for months. What waits beyond it is not the destruction of the not-self. "My Not-Self is my shadow. It's not gone. I don't want it to be gone. I need it."

## Pure Generator and Manifesting Generator

Ra was firm that there are only four kinds of beings and that "Generators are Generators are Generators": pure or manifesting, the strategy is identical and decisions are made only in response. The difference is mechanical. Pure Generators have no motor to the Throat and move level by level. Manifesting Generators have a motor-to-throat connection that gives them speed, and Ra treated that capacity as a liability rather than an advantage: "the greatest handicap you have is the manifesting capacity, because it's the thing that's locked you into living out the manifesting propaganda." It is what makes them, in his reading, the most deeply conditioned of all beings.
