# How the Five Types Work Together

Type is almost always taught one type at a time, but no type operates alone. The five [[Energy Types]] describe a division of labor, and most of what a person actually experiences of their own type is what happens when their aura meets somebody else's. Auras form a sphere around the body reaching out roughly twice the length of the arms, and they pass through walls, floors and ceilings. Wherever two of them meet, they influence each other and [[Conditioning]] takes place. This article is about those meetings.

## The Chain the System Describes

One account of the cooperation runs as a chain. Manifestors feel a change coming before anyone else does, and they can act on it because they are already at ease with not belonging and not quite fitting in. What a Manifestor activates first is not the guide and not the mirror but the [[Manifesting Generator]], who feels a sacral yes to the Manifestor's energy and knows immediately what to do with it. That is the Manifestor impulse with real sacral fuel underneath it, which is why the Manifesting Generator is described as the bridge. From there [[Generator]] types carry the mission out and produce, [[Projector]] types refine it, and [[Reflector]] types report back on whether the thing is working at all.

The same division appears in aura terms. Manifestors supply the initial spark and create a path for others to follow; Generators build on that spark and sustain it. Projectors, when invited, refine the goals and direct the energy so that effort is not wasted. Reflectors give everyone else a reading on the impact of what has been built and on where the community's energy is out of balance.

## What Happens When Two Auras Meet

The four auras are not four styles of the same gesture. They do different things to the person in front of them.

The Generator and Manifesting Generator aura is open and enveloping. It does not merely receive, it takes hold, closing around whoever steps into it in what Ra Uru Hu called an auric bear hug, and it can hold a crowd at once. That openness is why opportunity arrives at Generators without their chasing it, and it is also the exposure.

"This openness in the Generator is also a vulnerability; that is, in being so open and enveloping, you take deeply into yourself those that you allow into your aura." - Ra Uru Hu

The Projector aura is focused and penetrating, shaped more like a bow and arrow than an embrace. When a Projector recognizes someone, the aura goes straight into them, to the [[G Center]], the seat of identity and direction, where it amplifies both what is beautiful and what is out of alignment. This is what makes a Projector deeply significant as an ally and a guide, and what makes the same aura, uninvited, deeply uncomfortable to sit under. It also caps the Projector at one person at a time. Put two people in front of a Projector and one of them will end up feeling slighted.

The Manifestor aura is closed and repelling. It is not merely hard to penetrate; it pushes the other aura back, and that pushback is what keeps the Manifestor's initiating energy free of interference. Others routinely read it as coldness, aloofness or hostility when it is only the mechanics of the field.

The Reflector aura samples. It is not repelling like the Manifestor's and it is not closed, but it holds a fundamental tension at its surface so that it can do the one thing it is for: taste the other without sticking to them.

## The Numbers, and What They Mean Socially

Population figures vary by source and the spread is not an error to resolve. A single reference document in the source library gives the enveloping aura 66.30 percent of the population, the focused aura 22.5 percent, the closed aura 9.7 percent and the sampling aura 1.4 percent. The rounder figures carried on this wiki's [[Energy Types]] section page are about 70 percent sacral, about 20 percent Projector, 8 to 9 percent Manifestor and about 1 percent Reflector. Contemporary teaching material splits the sacral majority at roughly 38 percent Generator and 33 percent Manifesting Generator and puts Manifestors nearer 8 percent. Ra Uru Hu put Reflectors at around 1.3 to 1.4 percent.

What matters for interaction is not the decimal place but the shape. Ra put Generators and Projectors together at roughly 90 percent of the species. That means the overwhelming majority of human contact is an enveloping aura meeting a focused one, and it means the ambient conditions of ordinary life are generated conditions.

"It's a generator world. You walk down the street and you're in a generator aura. You cannot escape it." - Ra Uru Hu

Everything else in this article follows from that. Every type that is not sacral spends its life inside a field built by types that are.

## Generator and Projector

Because they account for that 90 percent between them, Generators and Projectors have the deepest auric interference of any pairing in the system, and the relationship is described as still only beginning to emerge. The enveloping aura and the focused aura belong to each other. The Generator is here to know themselves; the Projector is here to know the other, and will spend far more time studying somebody else's life than their own.

The exchange is genuinely two-way. A Projector is not an energy type and is therefore always going to be reliant on others to process things in life, and those others are dependent on the Projector in turn, because they are waiting for direction. When a Projector and a Generator work together well, each gives the other exactly what the other cannot produce alone: sustainable fuel on one side, accurate seeing on the other.

## The Outliers

The other pairing is the two types that are least shaped by the people around them and most shaped by the transiting program. The Manifestor's aura holds people off; the Reflector's lets them slide away. Neither is built to take another person deeply in the way a Generator does.

They are also the two smallest groups, and the Manifestor's position relative to the majority is stated bluntly in the source material.

"The manifestor is always the odd one out, and the manifestor is inherently different from at least the majority, that is the generators and projectors." - Ra Uru Hu

The Manifestor's nearest relative is not the Generator but the Projector, since both lack a defined [[Sacral Center]]. Both carry the potential to lead, and the whole difference between how they do it is auric.

"The manifestor, with its repelling aura, operates inherently on its own, dragging other forces through impact with it. But the projector needs to be invited." - Ra Uru Hu

## Where the Dependency Goes Wrong

The three non-sacral types depend on sacral energy they do not own, and there is a predictable way that dependency turns bad.

The Projector, surrounded by generated energy, borrows it and runs on it. The result is the exhausted imitation Generator: a person running around on other people's fuel, dropping physically exhausted each day, with bitterness as the receipt. The specific mechanical damage is that a Projector's focused aura leaves them highly vulnerable to conditioning while they are focusing, and particularly to generative and manifesting conditioning. This is the reason the sleeping recommendations exist. Sleeping in one's own aura lets a Projector disengage from the generative field and clear it out, and a Projector should go to bed before exhaustion rather than after it. Generators are the ones designed to empty their energy out and regenerate it in sleep; a Projector who copies that pattern is not regenerating anything.

The Reflector's version is environmental rather than energetic. Who a Reflector is around is who a Reflector becomes, so a Reflector held for too long in a wrong or toxic [[Environment]] can become unwell in every sense and will rarely connect the illness to the people and the place producing it.

The Manifestor's version is social. Because the aura repels, Manifestors do not attract connection the way Generators do, and the not-self answer is to withdraw into independence until anger and isolation cut them off from the people they need.

## What Each Type Gets Wrong About the Others

Conditioning by type is largely one type being pressed into the shape of another. The Manifestor is very often conditioned to behave like a Generator, told young that they are too much, too intense, too unpredictable, until they shrink the force and ask for approval rather than simply informing. The Generator is taught the opposite error, that success requires initiating, planning and pushing, so they override the body and say yes out of obligation. The Manifesting Generator is told to slow down, pick one thing and stop quitting, when the cyclical zigzag was always the design. The Projector is raised to keep up with sacral energy they do not have, to work longer and prove worth through effort. The Reflector is told to be consistent and decide quickly, neither of which is possible on a solar timeline; they are not inconsistent, they simply run a bigger cycle.

Read as a set, these are not five separate misunderstandings. They are four types being measured against the habits of the majority, plus the majority being measured against a picture of initiative that belongs to the smallest group of all.

## Families, Couples and Work

In families, the sharpest friction is the Manifestor child. A Generator or Projector parent meets an aura that repels, gets no warm and cuddly feedback from it, reads the child as uncontrollable, and responds with restriction, limitation and control. That is the standard route by which a Manifestor arrives at adulthood angry. The Reflector child needs the opposite kind of attention: an undefined G Center pushing constantly at the question of where the right place is, which makes comfort in their environment the single most important thing for them.

In couples and households, the recommendation that runs through all of it is to sleep in your own aura. Sharing a bed means sharing a conditioning field all night, and the guidance to sleep alone exists so that a person can wake up as themselves.

At work, the fit is the division of labor stated plainly. Generators and Manifesting Generators are the sustainable output and should be doing what genuinely engages them rather than what they were talked into. Projectors are suited to overseeing, guiding and managing the work of others rather than performing it, and their guidance lands when they have been recognized and invited and slides straight off when it has not. Manifestors need the freedom to operate without feeling controlled; that is the single condition for any productive relationship with them. Reflectors belong at the center or hub of a group rather than at its edge, because that position is where they can see who is who and what is different.
