# Reflector

The Reflector is the rarest of the [[Energy Types]] in [[Human Design System]] and the only one built with no [[Definition]] at all. Every one of the nine centers is open. No channel is permanently coloured in, there is no consistent life force, no fixed identity and no inner voice that answers in the moment. Where the other types are described as solar, imprinted by their own chart and carrying that imprint through the day, the Reflector is lunar. Their consistency does not live inside them. It arrives from outside, month by month, as the [[Moon]] travels around the wheel.

## How Rare Reflectors Are

Sources differ, and the spread is small but real. In a recorded lecture on type and aura Ra Uru Hu said that "Reflectors hover around 1.3 or 1.4% of the human population", and in another talk he cited 1.4 percent of humanity on the basis of a global survey of charts. A standard reference entry widens the band to approximately 1 to 2 percent. Most contemporary teaching material rounds down and simply says 1 percent. This wiki publishes the range as approximately 1 to 1.4 percent.

Whichever figure is used, the practical picture is the same, and Ra put it plainly: "we are a planet of 7 billion humans: one percent is an awful lot of human beings." One person in a hundred is a Reflector. That is a great many people, and it also means most Reflectors spend their lives as the only one in the room. There are a hundred people standing in a room, and chances are that only one of them is a Reflector.

## No Definition, No Hard Wiring

For every other type, definition is the bedrock. It is what makes type readable, and it is where the hard wiring comes from, the things that are consistent and therefore reliable. A Reflector's chart has none of it.

"There is no definition. There is no consistent life force in that sense. There is no consistent hard wiring." - Ra Uru Hu

It is tempting to read that as an extreme case of an open chart, as though a Reflector were someone with a great many [[Open Centers]]. Mechanically that is wrong. For anyone with definition, openness is the vulnerable part of the chart, where [[Conditioning]] enters and pulls a person off what is fixed in them. "But the openness in a reflector is not the same. It is not a counterpoint to what is hardwired at all," Ra said. There is nothing hardwired for it to counter.

What a Reflector has instead is gates. The BodyGraph is full of activations, but every one of them is a hanging gate: a receptor at one end of a channel with nothing at the other. Each waits for its harmonic gate, and can only receive one from another person's aura or from a transiting planet. When the connection lands, the channel completes and a center defines. Reference material puts the Moon at slightly less than twelve hours in a gate, so each of those definitions holds for roughly half a day.

## A Lunar Being

The Moon moves around the wheel faster than any other imprinting body, so it is the Moon that does most of this work. Map the harmonic gates of a Reflector's hanging gates onto the wheel and you can watch the Moon touch each one in turn. On one day it brings emotion, on another splenic awareness, on another sacral energy. The Moon, as Ra described it, "will make you a generator, will make you a manifestor, will make you anything."

This does not make Reflectors inconsistent. It puts their consistency on a longer cycle. Each of those definitions is not merely a temporary borrowing; it is the Reflector's own definition, reliable because it will be repeated every lunar cycle for the rest of their life. The sequence is fixed, unique to that chart, and endless.

What follows is the sentence the whole type rests on.

"a reflector on any given day is only an aspect of who they are" - Ra Uru Hu

The same Reflector met three weeks apart is not being unreliable. Those are two frames of something that only resolves at the scale of a month. At ordinary speed a Reflector looks like slow motion. Speed the life up, turn a month into a day, and the person finally becomes visible.

## The Resistant, Sampling Aura

The Reflector aura is resistant, and it is often called the Teflon aura because nothing sticks to it. It is not enveloping like a [[Generator]] aura, not focused and penetrating like a [[Projector]] aura, and not closed and repelling like a [[Manifestor]] aura.

"The reflector has a resistant aura. Not repelling like the manifestor, and not closed, but resistant." - Ra Uru Hu

Sampling is a mechanic, not a mood, and the resistance is what makes it possible. The aura "keeps a fundamental tension in terms of the quality of penetration between it and the other so that it can do one thing and one thing only: sample." Rather than taking another aura in and holding it, the Reflector's aura holds it at a workable distance, tastes it and lets it pass. "You rub up against a reflector and they sample you. They sample and they compare."

The comparison is the point. A Reflector is not absorbing a person, they are measuring that person against everyone else they have tasted. The aura is an instrument for detecting what is out of place and what does not fit. For how the four auras differ, see [[Aura Types]].

## Strategy: Wait the Lunar Cycle

The Reflector [[Strategy]] is to [[Wait the Lunar Cycle]], and for this type the strategy is also the whole of the authority. Looking at a Reflector, Ra said, "there is only strategy. That is the authority. Time, time, nothing else." The cycle does not run on a calendar. It starts the moment something arrives that will demand a decision, and it ends when the Moon has completed its circuit and delivered the full sequence of definitions the design can produce. The mechanic itself is treated in full at [[Wait the Lunar Cycle]] and [[Lunar Authority]].

What is type-specific is the social cost of the wait, and it is where Reflectors break most often. A world organised around instant answers treats a month as unreasonable. Ra's response was that the request is the Reflector's power rather than their weakness: "If you are correct in your demand for respect, you will get it. They will wait. And those who do not wait -- you did not lose anything. They were never for you in the first place." He was equally blunt about the damage done when that respect is never offered. A Reflector who reaches the edge of adulthood having never been granted the time is, in his words, already lost by then. The luxury of being able to wait, and to process before coming to any kind of conclusion, is one that only the enormously fortunate among them ever had. This passage survives in the library only as an unpunctuated transcript, so it is given here as report rather than as quotation.

The pressure is external but it is amplified from within. "The great enemy of the reflector is their mind," Ra said. Mind insists that a month of waiting means the opportunity is lost. Mind is not the faculty that measures for a Reflector. Experience is.

## Environment, Place and the Undefined G Center

Every Reflector has an undefined [[G Center]], and no other type can be described that way as a rule. Undefined, the G Center pushes at one question: "Where is the right place to be? If I'm in the right place, I'm going to find the love that's there for me."

This is not a preference. The [[Environment]] is the material a Reflector is made of on any given day, so the quality of the place and the people determines the quality of the person. Reflectors are always the samplers of their community, and the community has a deep influence on them in return. The healthy version of this is described as borrowing an environment on purpose: entering it with curiosity, giving it a month, noticing what was worth keeping, then clearing the residue before borrowing the next one. Many Reflectors are drawn to nature and stillness for exactly that clearing.

Because they cannot initiate, Reflectors depend on being brought to new places by others, and the discernment afterwards is their own work. Pressure is the reliable warning sign, in friendships as much as in romantic relationships: being continually pushed to decide, with nobody willing to grant the full cycle, is information about the environment rather than about the Reflector.

## Mirror of the Community

Reflectors sample, compare and reflect the state of an environment, and they do it for everyone in it. Their attunement to the transiting program gives them a second kind of sight. In every one of those hanging gates they are connected to the transits that condition everybody, and from inside that connection they can see who is not being conditioned.

"Reflectors are the great cosmic police." - Ra Uru Hu

Ra called this the gift of the Reflector. "Like the canary in the mine. A great gift." He also said that "they are the only ones that truly can tell who's in and out of the program." Where other types resist the program, the Reflector is here to embrace it, which is why being alive is described as the ultimate surprise for this type.

There is a darker version of the same perception. Reflectors who are themselves deeply conditioned turn it into a tool of homogenization, spotting the people who do not fit in order to pull them back in.

## The Conditioning Paradox

Two positions in the material sit in genuine tension, and both are worth holding.

The first is that a Reflector is unusually resilient to conditioning. The sampling aura is described as considerably more resistant to being conditioned by another person than a Projector aura, which sits directly inside everyone else's energy. Nine open centers do not automatically make a Reflector the most vulnerable type.

The second is that a Reflector is more shaped by their surroundings than anyone alive. Practitioners describe watching a Reflector take a new job and become, within a few years, an unrecognisably different person, absorbed by the industry around them. If you are not conscious of how your environment is affecting you, that environment becomes your personality.

Both describe one mechanism from opposite ends. The aura does not retain what it samples, but the Reflector still becomes whatever is being sampled for as long as the sampling lasts, and a life spent in one wrong place is a life spent as one wrong version of themselves. The protection is real but conditional on the Reflector knowing what they are.

"The vast majority of reflectors do not know that that is what they are. They do not know the mechanism." - Ra Uru Hu

Without it, Ra said, they are overwhelmed by the constant imposition of the authorities of others and never able to hold on to anything consistent.

## Disappointment and Surprise

The [[Not-Self]] theme is disappointment. It shows up when decisions are rushed, when the place or the people are wrong, and when nobody will grant the time. Lived in long enough it curdles into pessimism: a settled sense that people are letting them down, that nobody is listening, that nobody sees or feels what the Reflector sees and feels.

The [[Signature]] is surprise, and it cannot be manufactured. A Reflector who sets out to produce their own surprise has already lost it, in the same way a Generator cannot force satisfaction. Surprise is what arrives when a Reflector enters an environment without a conclusion already formed, and it includes being surprised by what they mirror back to the people around them. Both themes turn on one hinge: each is the experience of something not being what you expected. Which one arrives depends on whether the Reflector entered with expectations at all.

## The Reflector Child

Reflector children have the same strategy as Reflector adults, and the most important thing an adult can give them is time. Ra was pointed about this, describing the reflector children who are never given such a privilege of time, and the parent who asks a child which school they would like without expecting to wait out a full cycle for the answer.

The other requirement is comfort in the environment. It is the environment the child samples, and the sampling determines their nature. A thriving Reflector child, or a struggling one, says a great deal about the state of the household. Non-sacral children in general need an organised bedtime so that their system actually rests.

## Energy, Rest and Health

Reflectors have an undefined [[Sacral Center]], so there is no consistent life force and no built-in sense of when enough is enough. Filled to overflowing by other people's sacral energy, non-sacral beings routinely run on borrowed fuel, override tiredness and push through. They are easily exhausted, tend to be the last to go to bed when they need to be the first, and their rest is not optional.

Position matters here too. A Reflector who is never given a chance to make a decision may come to feel unimportant; a Reflector who is permanently put in charge will burn out. Both are what happens when a lunar being is run on a solar schedule.

## The Wisdom of a Correct Reflector

The openness that makes a Reflector vulnerable is also, when it is not being personalised, an exceptional window. Nine undefined centers can sample the frequencies of everything around them, which gives a Reflector the potential to taste what is really happening in a way no one else can. Undefined centers that are not being identified with stop distorting and start informing.

The self that emerges is cumulative rather than fixed. "Definition by definition by definition is brought to you through the agency of the moon," Ra said, each one a platform on which the essence of who you are gets built layer by layer. The identity is not revealed moment to moment. It is revealed across the full cycle.

"For the reflector -- you are a cycle being. You have no whole picture until you go through your cycles." - Ra Uru Hu

Someone who has lived that way is hard to rush and hard to fool. Ra's closing instruction to the type was as short as the mechanic itself: "Honor the moon. Be a true reflector." Or, more simply still: "Follow the moon, find your purpose."
