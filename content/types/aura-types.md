# Aura Types

Aura is the layer of [[Human Design System]] underneath everything people notice about each other. Before a word is spoken, two energy fields have met and begun to influence one another. The aura is not an accessory to the body. It is the mechanical way a type meets the world, and the reason [[Strategy]] takes the shape it does for each type.

## What an Aura Is

Every living being gives off an energy field. What Human Design adds is that these fields are not all alike, that they differ by genetic design rather than by mood or effort, and that the differences are consistent enough to classify. Type is described as concerning the body, genetics and the aura, and as having nothing to do with psychological or personality typing. Each type carries a prime auric frequency the literature calls as distinguishable as a blood type.

The aura is also not treated as an output of the person. It is treated as the person.

"...the most important thing to understand about aura is that aura isn't a byproduct of what we are. It is what we are. It carries the essential energetic information of our nature." - Ra Uru Hu

This is why type cannot be self-selected or improved. [[Definition]] in the BodyGraph, meaning which [[Centers]] are colored in and how they connect, determines type, and type is expressed through the aura.

## The Reach of the Aura

The sources give a physical extent and do not entirely agree. The most commonly repeated figure describes the aura as a sphere around the body stretching out roughly twice the length of the arms. Another source, noting the disagreement directly, gives about three feet to six feet. The figures overlap rather than contradict. Either way the field is much larger than the body, and other people are inside it more often than anyone notices.

Auras are also described as passing through physical barriers. Walls, floors and ceilings do not stop them. Sharing a wall with a neighbor means an aura is permeating that neighbor's space whether either party is aware of it or not.

"We are aura beings." - Ra Uru Hu

## Four Auras and Five Types

Human Design names five types but only four auras. The [[Generator]] and the [[Manifesting Generator]] share one. The source material groups auras into four: Manifestors, Generators including Manifesting Generators, Projectors, and Reflectors. The older literature went further and divided humanity into four types outright, treating the Manifesting Generator as a subcategory of the Generator.

The reasoning is mechanical. A Manifesting Generator is a hybrid, carrying a motor connected to the Throat on top of a defined [[Sacral Center]]. That changes their speed and adds informing to their strategy. It does not change their aura, which is described as the same open and enveloping field the Generator carries.

Population figures vary. One library reference gives Generators and Manifesting Generators together at 66.30 percent, Projectors at 22.5 percent, Manifestors at 9.7 percent and Reflectors at 1.4 percent. Lecture material gives rounder numbers: about 70 percent, 20 percent, 9 percent, and one person in a hundred. Both sets belong to the range.

## The Open and Enveloping Aura

The Generator and Manifesting Generator aura is open and enveloping. It does not stop at a boundary: it goes around whoever steps into it and takes them in.

> Generators are the key to life on this planet. They literally generate the life force of the planet, and as such, they have the most penetrating, in that sense, of auric fields. A Generator will envelop anyone that steps into its aura. This openness in the Generator is also a vulnerability; that is, in being so open and enveloping, you take deeply into yourself those that you allow into your aura. - Ra Uru Hu

Two things follow. The first is magnetism: an open field draws people and opportunities inward, which is the mechanical ground for the [[Respond]] strategy, since a Generator does not need to chase what the aura is already bringing close. The second is exposure. What envelops also absorbs, and the same openness that makes this the sustaining field of the planet makes it the field most thoroughly conditioned by whoever is allowed into it.

Because this aura belongs to the majority, it is the background condition of the world. The lecture material describes a generator world: a generator aura on the street, in the workplace, and in the overwhelming majority of cases in the bedroom.

## The Closed and Repelling Aura

The [[Manifestor]] aura is closed and repelling. It is described as tight and difficult to penetrate, and as doing more than merely closing: it pushes the other aura away.

The purpose is protective. A closed field keeps the initiating impulse intact and unavailable for interference, which is what a type designed to move first requires. The cost is social. Others register the pushback without understanding it and read it as coldness or aloofness.

"The inability to approach the Manifester doesn't mean that the Manifester is unapproachable, but it is expressed in the aura, and you cannot change that." - Ra Uru Hu

That last clause matters. The aura cannot be softened by good intentions. What can change is what accompanies it, which is why the Manifestor strategy is to [[Inform]]. Informing supplies the information the closed aura withholds, and the resistance that would otherwise gather has less to gather around.

## The Focused and Penetrating Aura

The [[Projector]] aura is narrow rather than wide. Sources name it two ways, focused and penetrating and focused and absorbing, both describing the same mechanic. It goes into the other person rather than around them, reaching the [[G Center]], the center of identity and direction.

> Their ability to recognize the other is rooted in the way in which their aura penetrates the other, literally to their core. Yet at the same time, for the Projector, because their aura is so focused, it means that they are deeply vulnerable to conditioning while they are focusing... the Projector aura is only designed to deal with one person at a time. - Ra Uru Hu

Three consequences follow. Projectors see the other accurately, which is the basis of guidance and the reason [[Recognition]] and the strategy to [[Wait for the Invitation]] matter so much to them. Projectors are noticeable, because a penetrating field gets attention from across a room and from behind. And Projectors are conditioned hardest at the moment they are doing what they are built for, because focus is also an opening.

## The Resistant and Sampling Aura

The [[Reflector]] aura is named resistant and sampling, and in lecture material simply the Teflon aura. It is not closed and it does not repel. It holds a surface tension that keeps whatever it meets from sinking in and sticking.

> And finally, we come to the Reflector and we come to this sampling aura, what I like to call the Teflon aura, the way in which the Reflector tastes the world around it in order to discover what is out of place, what doesn't work, what is different. - Ra Uru Hu

The resistance is what makes the sampling possible. Because this aura tastes rather than takes in, it can register a person, a room or a community and then let it go, which grounds the Reflector's capacity to report on the health of a collective. It is also why [[Environment]] is decisive here, and why the strategy is to [[Wait the Lunar Cycle]] rather than trust one day's sample. The [[Moon]] supplies the rhythm the aura does not supply on its own.

## When Two Auras Meet

When two auras meet they influence each other, and conditioning takes place. Nothing needs to be said or intended.

The pairings behave predictably. A Generator meeting a Manifestor is an enveloping field trying to surround a closed one, which the lecture material describes as not necessarily very satisfying. A Generator and a Projector make a mutual arrangement, the Generator enveloping and open around the Projector while the Projector focuses deep into them, and the source adds that this is not always pleasant unless it is correct. Those two account for the great majority of any room, and their fields are described as having a fundamental connection rooted in availability through aura: the Generator envelops, the Projector focuses. A Reflector samples and moves on.

## Aura, Not Personality

This is why what people react to when someone walks into a room is so often unrelated to who that person is being. The reaction is auric, it arrives before assessment, and nobody controls it. A Manifestor who has said nothing is still felt as a barrier. A Projector who has said nothing is still noticed. [[Conditioning]] works at this layer, which is why [[Deconditioning]] is described as a matter of awareness rather than of adjusting behavior.

## Aura and Children

Aura arrives with the infant, and the family it arrives into is usually a different aura. A Manifestor infant does not present the connectivity parents expect. Manifestor children are described as not the warm and cuddly kind, not because of temperament but because the field repels, and parents meet that closed aura with concern and then with control. Without awareness these become heavily restricted and regularly punished children, where punishment means restrictions and controls rather than anything physical. The same dynamic scales up in a nursery room, where most of the other children are Generators and Projectors whose fields connect easily. The Manifestor child is left outside a connection everybody else is making, nobody can explain why, and alienation follows. The remedy described is not a change of aura but a set of manners: asking while a child, informing as an adult, so the field is no longer the only information others get.

Projector children sit at the other extreme. Because the focused aura is an opening as well as an instrument, and because the child is a non-energy being dependent on the energy types around it, Projector children are described as some of the most deeply conditioned beings on the planet, with the parent unable to see what operates underneath the surface.

## Aura and Sleep

Sleep is where the aura becomes a health question: several unconscious hours inside somebody else's field.

"...one of the most important recommendations to anyone who has their first encounter with human design in terms of a reading is the recommendation that they sleep in their own aura." - Ra Uru Hu

The argument is specific to the open Sacral. A non-sacral being spends the day taking in sacral energy that is not theirs, and sleep is when that gets processed out. Another body lying inside the aura interrupts the clearing and the energy stays. The source is careful that this has nothing to do with love, sex or intimacy, and everything to do with two unconscious beings occupying one field. Related guidance for non-sacral types is to go horizontal well before sleep rather than collapse into it exhausted, letting the aura change gradually. Sleeping in one's own aura is described as the protection that allows a person to wake up as themselves.

## Strategy Follows the Aura

Each strategy works with the aura a person actually has rather than the one the world rewards. The open and enveloping aura is designed to [[Respond]] to what it has already drawn in. The closed aura is designed to [[Inform]] so resistance clears. The focused aura waits for the invitation that makes its penetration welcome. The sampling aura waits out the lunar cycle. The [[Strategy]] section covers each in depth, along with the [[Signature]] and [[Not-Self]] themes that report whether the aura is being used correctly.
