# Projector Types

The [[Projector]] is one type but not one mechanic. Two people can both be Projectors, share the same focused aura, the same [[Strategy]] and the same not-self theme, and have almost nothing in common in how energy moves through the body. The foundational literature sorts Projectors into three categories on one question: what, if anything, is defined below the [[Throat Center]]. The answer governs how much fuel a Projector carries, how they rest, how they are conditioned, and how they can answer an invitation.

## The Mechanical Split

Every Projector shares two absences: no definition in the [[Sacral Center]], and no motor connected to the Throat. A motor to the Throat with an undefined Sacral would make the chart a [[Manifestor]]; a defined Sacral would make it a [[Generator]] or a [[Manifesting Generator]]. The Throat itself may be defined, by spleen to throat or head to throat or similar, but no motor is pumping energy into it.

Past those absences, Projector charts vary enormously. Some carry most of their centers defined; some almost nothing. The categories sort that variation with two further questions.

First, is any motor defined? There are four motors: the Sacral, the [[Root Center]], the [[Solar Plexus Center]] and the [[Heart Center]]. The Sacral is ruled out for every Projector, so three remain. If one or more of those three is defined, the chart is an Energy Projector.

Second, if no motor is defined, is there any definition at all below the Throat? If so, the chart is a Classic Projector. If not, it is a Mental Projector.

## How Many Categories, and What They Are Called

The foundational text names three categories of Projectors: Mental Projectors, Energy Projectors and Classic Projectors. Every source that counts them counts three, and none names a fourth.

Vocabulary drifts. Energy Projectors are also called energetic Projectors, and Classic Projectors are also called pure Projectors, since no motor is present. A separate habit is to label a Projector by [[Inner Authority]], so one hears of emotional, splenic and ego Projectors. Those are Authority names, not extra categories; an emotional Projector is an Energy Projector whose motor is the Solar Plexus.

Frequency is disputed. Some sources call the Energy Projector the most common of the three, since a chart needs only one motor to qualify. Others call the Classic or pure Projector the most common, and the Energy Projector the one that struggles hardest to accept a non-energy design. No primary source gives a breakdown. On the Mental Projector, the sources that offer a number put it near 1 percent, but they are inconsistent about whether that means 1 percent of Projectors or 1 percent of the population; the foundational text gives no figure, describing a design with no center of inner personal Authority only as rare and unique. Projectors as a whole are usually given as roughly 20 percent of the population.

## Energy Projectors

An Energy Projector has one or more motors defined, any except the Sacral. That motor is real fuel. It generates and moves energy through the design, and it produces pressure to discharge what has built up. These Projectors feel bursts, surges or drive that other Projectors do not, and other people sense that power and want access to it.

The mechanic does not make them an energy type. The motor supplies access to energy, not a renewable supply. The trap is that it makes keeping up possible for a while: because these Projectors can compete with the pace of a sacral world, they try to, and they are the Projectors most prone to burnout. A defined Root is the hardest case, because root pressure reads like adrenaline and produces a persistent urgency to get it done. What is in alignment instead is work in bursts, then real rest.

The foundational text names two temptations that come with the extra energy. The first is initiating an action simply to release the pressure. The second is committing to an activity and becoming a laborer in it, rather than the one who can see how to direct the process. So Energy Projectors are told to be especially vigilant about invitations from people who want their energy only to fill a vacant position or get a job done. Recognition of the guide is the correct invitation; recruitment of the worker is not.

Authority follows the motor. A defined Solar Plexus gives [[Emotional Authority]], so the invitation has to be waited out across the wave rather than answered in the moment. A defined Heart, with no Spleen, Sacral or Solar Plexus definition, gives [[Ego Authority]], the voice of what I want and what I have the will for. An Energy Projector with a defined Root and a defined Spleen carries [[Splenic Authority]] alongside the motor, which is why a chart can be called a splenic Energy Projector.

## Classic Projectors

A Classic Projector has definition below the Throat that does not include a motor. Spleen to Throat, G to Throat and similar non-motor definition all belong here. There is consistency in the design, sometimes a great deal of it, but none of it generates energy.

The consequence is that the Classic Projector meets the standard Projector description without qualification. The pace is slower, softer. There is less energetic dominance and no motor humming underneath, and rest comes more naturally, sometimes as several deliberate stops across a day rather than one long night. Library material on Projector sleep suggests, while admitting no study behind it, that Classic Projectors sleep better and longer, and that Projectors without a defined Root sleep more easily than those with one.

The absence of motor definition carries a specific gift in the foundational text. Because a Classic Projector has no fuel of their own, they can test and taste the fuel coming from other people's motors and discern which of those people are correct for them, which gives them the potential to be more objective and selective about the invitations they accept. The same openness is the risk: a Projector takes in and magnifies the creative life force of a defined Sacral, exhilarating but carrying whatever is toxic in that energy, so the purity of the fuel in the people they let in is a matter of health, not preference.

Their conditioning runs the other way from the Energy Projector's. Rather than pushing from a motor, the Classic Projector fears that protecting their energy will cost them money, clients or standing. Bitterness here often arrives through being recognized and then overused, so boundaries around how much of the gift is available become part of the design.

Authority here is [[Splenic Authority]] or [[Self-Projected Authority]]. Self-Projected Authority originates in the [[G Center]] and reaches the Throat through one of four channels, and no motors are present in that configuration at all, which means a self-projected Projector is always a Classic Projector. Its instruction is to listen to what you say when you are invited, rather than deciding mentally what to say.

## Mental Projectors

A Mental Projector is defined by any combination of defined [[Head Center]], [[Ajna Center]] and Throat, with no definition below the Throat. The possible arrangements are Head to Ajna, Ajna to Throat, or Head, Ajna and Throat all connected. Everything else is open. Some secondary descriptions say instead that a Mental Projector has no definition anywhere, only activated gates, which does not match the foundational definition; a chart with every center undefined is a [[Reflector]].

"And the mental projector is always a being with four open motors." - Ra Uru Hu

That makes the design open and deeply reliant on the mind. The whole lower body graph takes in and amplifies what is around it, and with an open Spleen and no energy underneath there is a real temptation to act spontaneously. The definition that exists is powerful but is not a decision-making mechanism.

"They have definition. And that definition is deeply, deeply powerful in the sense that it is their inherent life force. But the definition is strictly mental." - Ra Uru Hu

The difficulty is that the mind feels like the strongest thing they have, so it is hard to accept that a strong and agile mind cannot be their Authority. Mental Projectors are the archetype of outer Authority: guidance that is genuinely valuable to others, from a mind good at pattern and strategy and at seeing what people miss, but not built to decide for itself. The lifelong pull toward more study and one more qualification belongs here, and so does the point at which more learning stops being the answer.

Recognition and invitation land differently here because the invitation cannot be answered in the moment at all. There is a process instead, and it is rooted in interaction.

"In other words, all mental projectors need to discuss with others what is before them until they finally hear their truth spoken." - Ra Uru Hu

This is the sounding board, and the point of it is not advice. What the other person supplies is the aura connection that lets the Mental Projector hear themselves, so the conversation only has to be ordinary. Because each person draws a different articulation out of them, it has to happen several times, one to one, until a consistency appears in how they keep describing the thing. Somewhere in that repetition it becomes clear whether they want it, and the deciding factor is never what any of those people said.

"They're not looking for advice. They're not looking for the other to guide them. They're looking for a mirror on their own process." - Ra Uru Hu

The environment is the other half of the mechanism, which is why this is called environment Authority and why it is filed under [[Mental Authority]] and [[No Direct Inner Authority]]. With no inner center to consult, guidance arrives as sensory information about the place they are in, read through the [[Open Centers]]. The first question is whether this environment is correct, because if it is wrong then the people in it and the bargains made in it will not be right either. So it is worth physically visiting a place before deciding about it, and a crowded room is a poor place to reach clarity, since what is felt there is mostly other people. A Mental Projector is still not a Reflector; they have definition, they are not lunar beings, and the moon cycle is not their process.

## How the Invitation Lands Differently

All three wait for the same recognition; what differs is the response. The Energy Projector can say yes too fast, because the motor supplies energy to start and the pressure to discharge makes starting feel necessary. The Classic Projector has the cleanest read on whether an invitation is correct. The Mental Projector cannot answer in the moment at all.

## Strategy

The Strategy is the same across all three: [[Wait for the Invitation]]. The categories change nothing about what a Projector waits for, only what they tend to do while waiting and how fast they can answer. See [[Strategy Overview]] for the mechanic.

## What the Categories Do Not Change

The aura is identical in all three: penetrating, focused, at its best one to one. The [[Signature]] is success and the [[Not-Self]] theme is bitterness for every Projector regardless of category. None of the three is designed for sustained output, and none generates its own life force. Knowing the category matters mainly because it names the particular [[Conditioning]] a Projector is most exposed to, and because a Projector who does not know which one they are will often try to live as one of the others.
