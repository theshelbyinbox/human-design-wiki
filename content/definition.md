# Definition

Definition is what is consistent in a person. Two centers joined by a completed channel are defined, and the energy between them runs the same way for a lifetime. Everything else is open to influence.

This section is about how those defined parts sit in relation to each other. All in one piece, or separated into two, three or four islands with gaps between them. That arrangement is the definition type, and it shapes processing speed, what a person looks for in others, and where conditioning gets in.

Definition is derived, never typed. Count the separate groups of connected defined centers: one group is Single, two is Split, three is Triple Split, four is Quadruple Split, none is No Definition.

## The Five Types

Population figures below are the Definitive Book's. Three of them are stable across sources; two are not, and both articles give the range rather than a single number.

- [[Single Definition]] one continuous flow, approximately 41 percent
- [[Split Definition]] two separate areas, approximately 46 percent, the most common arrangement
- [[Triple Split Definition]] three separate areas, approximately 11 percent
- [[Quadruple Split Definition]] four separate areas, sources differ between roughly 0.5 and 0.63 percent
- [[No Definition]] no centers defined at all, sources differ between roughly 1 and 2 percent
