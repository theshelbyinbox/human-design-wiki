# Deconditioning

Deconditioning is the process by which a person releases the patterns, habits and identities formed through [[Conditioning]] and returns to operating from their own design. In the [[Human Design System]] it is not a technique, a therapy or a belief. It is what happens to a body when decisions stop being made by the mind and begin to be made through [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]].

## Why Strategy and Authority Is the Mechanism

The mind is the channel conditioning arrives through, which is why it cannot be the instrument that removes it. Ra Uru Hu was blunt about this in his lecture on Gate 17.

> "It doesn't matter what you learn from me or anyone else. It doesn't matter that you understand this. Please recognize that your mind cannot stop conditioning. It is subject to it. It cannot stop it. The only thing that you can do to operate correctly on this plane is to navigate according to your strategy and your authority."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

This is why studying Human Design deconditions nobody. Understanding the chart is more information, and information enters through the same openness the conditioning did. The one lever outside the mind is the decision itself, repeated. Generators and Manifesting Generators respond, Manifestors inform, Projectors wait for the invitation, Reflectors wait the lunar cycle, and an Inner Authority then answers in the body rather than in thought; those mechanics belong to [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]].

## The Seven Years

> "We have a process in this life of cellular renewal. Just about all the cells in our body are renewed every seven years. Deconditioning is a seven year process. It takes dedication. It takes discipline. It takes the dream of wanting to fulfill your purpose on this plane."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

In his Introduction lecture Ra said it three times in a row: seven years, it takes seven years, it takes seven years to change the cells in the body. He called it a seven year cellular cycle and a process of cleansing yourself of that being that was created out of not-you, and said everything which came to that not-self life, your lovers, your friends, your work, had all been conditioned, all of it. The repetition is the teaching. He was not setting a completion date, he was refusing to let the duration be negotiated down.

Nor is the figure an endpoint. Ra called deconditioning a life process, about transforming the life rather than an experience in the moment. The seven years reach a foundation from which the rest of the life is lived, with things still arriving to challenge it. *The 7 Year Human Design Deconditioning Journey* adds that more than half the body's cells turn over inside the first year and a half, which is why a shift is often felt long before year seven.

> "Be patient, live your process moment by moment, but understand it is a journey. There is no instant gratification in that."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

## What the Early Stage Is Like

The opening stretch is rarely smooth, for mechanical rather than moral reasons. Conditioning does not stand aside once a person begins deciding differently.

> "And we are all designed to be seduced by conditioning. All of us. And that conditioning in and of itself is not something that's passive. It is actually quite aggressive in the way in which it tries to take over and determine what you're going to do and be."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

Two things happen at once. Familiar reference points stop working: a Generator who has always initiated has nothing to do while waiting to respond, a Projector who has always volunteered sits inside the silence where no invitation has come. At the same time the [[Not-Self]] theme gets louder rather than quieter, because it is finally being noticed. The frustration, bitterness, anger and disappointment were all there before; what is new is that they are legible.

The mind's answer to that discomfort is to settle the question early.

> "I often listen to beings who have entered into this process in which their minds are trying to codify who they've become. But it's all premature."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

The urge to announce a new identity, to decide what kind of Generator or Projector one now is, is conditioning again in fresh vocabulary. The instruction is unglamorous: keep deciding correctly and let the body do the rest.

## What Deconditioning Is Not

Deconditioning is not:

- **Rejecting** other people, relationships or the world
- **Arriving** at a final purity after which no conditioning occurs
- **Silencing** the mind
- **Suppressing** the emotions and reactions that arise
- **Closing** the open centers so they stop taking anything in

The [[Not-Self]] does not disappear. What changes is its authority. Conditioning keeps arriving for as long as there are other people, and the transit field conditions everyone continuously while being neither visible nor avoidable. Ra was clear that if Strategy and Authority carry you towards a conditioning element there is nothing wrong with that, provided the yes came from the body. A contemporary course lesson on deconditioning and shadow work puts it honestly: the conditioning is never escaped, it stops being the thing that chooses.

## The Open Centers Are Where It Happens

The open centers are the main sites of conditioning and therefore of deconditioning. What is white amplifies what the other brings, and that is where the not-self strategies get built. The aim is not to make the open parts behave like the defined ones.

> "It is through the open centers that we achieve wisdom in this life. You know, one of my earliest metaphors for the body graph was that what you have colored in is the student. And what is white is where you go to school."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

Ra spoke of his own completely open Sacral as something that had distorted his life deeply and then, across twenty-two years, opened the whole mystery of life to him. The openness stays open. What ends is its grip on the decision, and he named the condition exactly: you never reach the beauty of that wisdom until you stop it driving your life, which it can only do through the mind. Each center in detail belongs to [[Centers]] and [[Open Centers]].

## What It Costs

> "Theoretically, it's very easy to be correct. But the moment that you step into this world is the moment that you have to deal with the consequences of your own potential and your own authority. It may negatively influence beings around you. It may change the circumstances of the way in which you deal with others and operate in this life."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

Relationships formed while making Not-Self decisions are, in part, agreements about who a person will keep being. When the decisions change, the agreement breaks without anyone intending it, and some bonds do not survive. Ra treated this as mechanics rather than affection: Strategy and Authority also have to lead you to who is correct for you, and it is not about liking or disliking. Only in correct relationships, he said, can the process continue without constant struggle against the resistance of those around you.

His commentary on sleeping alone makes the same point from the other side. Relationships are not bound together by sleeping together, he said, but by meeting each other. Sleeping in your own aura lets the system cleanse itself of conditioning overnight, so you wake as yourself, and he counted that as part of the experiment.

## Not Belief, Experiment

> "Human design is not about believing. It isn't. It is not something to believe in. It is not a philosophy. It is not a religion."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

In the Introduction lecture Ra sharpened this into a distinction. It is not about believing him or anybody else who tells you about it, because this is not sheep knowledge, it is wolf knowledge. Sheep knowledge is taken on someone's authority; wolf knowledge is what you find out for yourself, and the only way to understand what it is to be correct is to experiment. In the same breath he described the alternative: holding on to that temptation of the mind to run your life, which really makes a mess of things, because the mind does not know and cannot know.

That makes the experiment testable by the only person whose test counts. The evidence is not agreement with the theory but the quality of the life, the frequency of the [[Signature]] and the quieting of the Not-Self theme, across years rather than weeks.

> "There is a morning that you wake up and realize how much everything has changed and how much you have changed with that."
> -- Ra Uru Hu
