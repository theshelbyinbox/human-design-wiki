# Conditioning

Conditioning is the process through which a person's undefined or open centers absorb and are shaped by the energies of others and of the larger transit field. In [[Human Design System]] it is the primary mechanism that produces the [[Not-Self]]. It is not a figure of speech for social pressure, and it does not begin as psychology. It is mechanical: one body's energy field meets another's, and what is open in each takes in what is fixed in the other. It cannot be avoided, and Human Design does not treat it as damage. What can change is whether a person makes decisions out of it, which is the work of [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]].

## Why We Are Open at All

The openness is not an accident or a defect in the design. It is genetic.

> "The imperative of our genes is to make more, and not simply make more but make something different. For its survival our species demands it; we are attracted to what we are not."
> -- The Human Design Dictionary, Jovian Archive

That attraction is what keeps the openness filled. You are drawn to the person who has what you do not, you take in the whole of them rather than the one connection that drew you, and the two of you condition each other. Ra Uru Hu described this as the genetic imperative, and he was blunt that it operates below any choice a person makes about who they like.

## The Aura Is the Mechanism

Ra taught the aura not as an energy field given off by the body but as a full expression of, and a controlling agent of, the way the form works. Its reach is larger than most people assume.

> "Auras form a sphere around our bodies, stretching out twice the length of our arms. When two auras meet, they influence each other and conditioning takes place. Auras transcend physical barriers like walls, floors, and ceilings."
> -- The Human Design Aura Types, Ra.TV

Ra used the example of an apartment wall: stand beside it and your aura is already in the neighbor's space, and can be conditioned by whoever is over there. Aura size also fluctuates. Each of the four aura mechanics takes the other in differently, which is covered in [[Aura Types]] and [[Energy Types]].

This is also why most of a chart matters more than the colored-in part.

> "Most of what you see in your BodyGraph, if you look carefully most of what you're looking at is white. That is, most of what you're looking at are receptors. We are mostly receptors."
> -- The Complete Guide to the Human Design System, Ra Uru Hu

Open centers are receptors. They take in definition from anything that can connect to them, and the mechanics of that are held in [[Nine Centers]] and [[Open Centers]].

## Why the Amplification Convinces

Reception alone would not be enough to build a life on. What makes conditioning stick is that an open center does not merely receive a frequency, it magnifies it.

> "That frequency of distorted emotional energy would pour through me and become amplified, because all those things you have that are white in your Design are amplifiers. You don't just take in the others; you crank it up. And in most cases, it amplifies until it distorts."
> -- You and the Shadow, Ra Uru Hu

The amplified version is more vivid than anything the person generates alone, so the mind identifies with it and builds strategies to keep it. Ra's example was the stop-smoking client with an open Heart Center who felt genuinely willful in the room with his defined one, and lost it walking past the tobacco shop afterward, because he was no longer amplifying their Heart Center. Ra put the open Heart Center at about 68 percent of human beings in that lecture and at around two thirds in his Gate 17 commentary. The pattern generalizes: a person with an open Sacral keeps working past their limit because in the presence of Sacral beings they feel energized, and openness of any kind, he said, brings dependency.

## The Conditioning Agents

**Other people.** The most immediate source, and the one you can at least observe. Any defined center conditions the matching open center of whoever is inside the aura.

**Family and upbringing.** The earliest and deepest layer, and it starts before there is any awareness to notice it. Ra described the infant with an open Spleen whose mother's definition colors that center in: the child becomes wholly dependent on her, and shrieks when the aura is broken. By seven years of age, The Definitive Book of Human Design says, several layers of familial and societal conditioning have been piled over the true self.

**The Program.** The transit field, moving continuously through the gates and conditioning everyone with the corresponding openness.

**Culture and education.** Ra called the not-self strategies of the open centers the thing that homogenizes us, starting at the earliest education and in the expectations about how we are supposed to be.

## The Program

Underneath personal conditioning runs an impersonal layer. Ra was specific that it has no intent toward you at all.

> "And it's not like the program is a consciousness. It isn't. It's not like it's God saying, okay, my way. It isn't. It is a program. It is a construct. It is something that has evolved over time. It is heartless. It does not care about you or me or the other."
> -- The Complete Guide to the Human Design System, Ra Uru Hu

We cannot live outside it, he said; it is the essence of the maya, it is life. The imprinting travels on the neutrino stream. The Definitive Book describes three trillion neutrinos passing through every square inch of the planet, and of us, every second, carrying the material information that leaves the imprint. Because the transit field has no body and no voice, it is the one conditioning agent a person can practically never observe directly, which is why it is felt as the mood of a day rather than recognized as a force.

## Openness Is Where You Are Nurtured

The single most common misreading is that open centers are the broken part of a chart and the goal is to fill them in.

> "The undefined centers are not broken, they are not empty, and they do not need to be fixed."
> -- The Definitive Book of Human Design, The Science of Differentiation

Ra Uru Hu put it as a binary: where your definition is your nature, your openness is where you are nurtured. He also used the image of a school.

> "If you look at your Design, what's colored in is the student. What's open is where you go to school. This is what you're here to learn. This is how you get to master this plane and become wise."
> -- You and the Shadow, Ra Uru Hu

Because an open center samples every version of its frequency rather than holding one, a person who stops being run by it can become genuinely wise about it in a way that someone with it defined cannot. Ra, with an open emotional system, said he knew more about emotions than anyone on the planet. An open [[Head Center]] conditioned into pressure to answer unanswerable questions becomes, with awareness, a person who can recognize that pressure in others without being driven by it. The wisdom is not a consolation prize for the conditioning. It is the same openness, no longer making the decisions.

## What It Costs, and What Changes

The cost is not that conditioning happens. It is that the mind, conditioned through the openness, is then the thing making decisions, so a person spends a life chasing what they cannot be. Ra's phrase for the result was that homogenization is what distorts us, and the more we are pushed into sameness the fewer opportunities we have for awareness.

Nothing about that is fixed by trying to close the openness. You cannot turn off the conditioning you are taking in. What changes is the decision-making mechanism, which is why the whole system points at Strategy and Authority rather than at protection.

Proximity still matters, though. Because conditioning is auric, sleeping alone is one of the deepest practical recommendations in Human Design: asleep, you are deeply vulnerable and there is no awareness watching what is being absorbed, and sleeping in your own bed means you wake up as yourself. Choosing environments and company consciously matters most for those with a great deal of openness, and above all for the [[Reflector]], who has no definition at all. The long process of untangling what was absorbed from what is actually yours is covered in [[Deconditioning]].
