# Inner Authority

Inner Authority is the body-based decision-making mechanism specific to each person's chart configuration. Paired with [[Strategy]] as the two foundational tools Human Design offers, Inner Authority answers the question: *how should I decide?*

The system's central claim is that the mind is not an appropriate decision-making organ. Each Authority is a different mechanical signal -- sacral sound, emotional wave, splenic hit, heart conviction, self-articulation through speech, or for [[Reflector]]s the time-based sampling of the lunar cycle -- that bypasses the mind's conditioned criteria and routes the decision through the body's own intelligence.

Which signal is yours is not a matter of type or preference. It is decided by which centers are defined in your BodyGraph, checked in a fixed order, and the first one that carries authority wins. That is why two people of the same type can decide in completely different ways, and why getting the Authority right matters more than almost anything else in the system.

## Two Kinds of Timing

The deepest structural division in this section is not between the seven authorities but between two ways of experiencing time. A defined Solar Plexus means clarity arrives over time and never in the moment. An undefined Solar Plexus means the body knows now, or does not know at all. Roughly half of humanity sits on each side, and almost everyone is taught to operate as though only the second kind exists.
