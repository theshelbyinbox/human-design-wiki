# Planets Overview

Every activation in a BodyGraph was put there by a planet. The gates and channels people talk about do not arrive on their own: each one is switched on because a particular celestial body occupied that hexagram at one of two calculated moments. The Definitive Book of Human Design calls the Sun, Moon, Nodes and planets "the local programming agents of our solar system," and describes their function plainly. "The locations of these celestial bodies at the time of our birth determines the way they influence or 'flavor' how we experience our definition."

There are thirteen of them, and they are used twice. "The database within the BodyGraph is made up of a total of 26 activations, 13 each from the Design and the Personality positions." That is the entire raw material of a chart. Type, Authority, Profile, Definition and Incarnation Cross are all derived from those twenty-six positions.

## The Thirteen

Human Design uses the Sun, the Earth, the North Node, the South Node, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto. The Definitive Book lists them as teachers, and gives each one a keynote for what it expresses and teaches.

- [[Sun]], Personality Expression and Life Force
- [[Earth]], Grounding and Balance
- [[Moon]], Driving Force
- [[North Node]], Future Direction and Environment
- [[South Node]], Past Direction and Environment
- [[Mercury]], Communication and Thinking
- [[Venus]], Values and Sociology
- [[Mars]], Immaturity and Energy Dynamics
- [[Jupiter]], Law and Protection
- [[Saturn]], Discipline, the Judge, Restraint
- [[Uranus]], Unusualness, Chaos and Order, Science
- [[Neptune]], Illusion, Art, Spirituality
- [[Pluto]], Truth, Transformation, Psychology

Two of the thirteen are not planets in any astronomical sense, and one pair is not made of bodies at all. The Sun is a star. The Nodes, as the source material is careful to state, "are not planets, but positions -- points where the path of the Moon crosses the ecliptic." They are counted among the thirteen because they activate gates, which is the only membership test that matters here.

## What Does Not Activate a Gate

This is one of the sharper boundaries in the system, and it is worth stating because it is where Human Design and astrology visibly part company. According to the source Ra Uru Hu called the Voice, "no other objects in the heavens, other than the ones listed, can activate gates."

That exclusion is specific rather than dismissive. Titan, the moon of Saturn that is larger than Mercury, "filters the neutrino stream, and like everything else adds its quality, but it does not activate a gate." Kiron, also spelled Chiron, does not activate or open a gate either, though the Definitive Book allows that "If the thematic presumptions are correct, however, and this object is associated with healing/wounding, then seeing where it is in the BodyGraph can provide an insight." The astrological ascendant, midheaven and Arabian points do not activate gates.

Kiron still matters to Human Design, but as a timing marker rather than an activation. That role is covered in [[Planetary Returns]].

## Why Planets Imprint Anything

The mechanism is the neutrino stream, and it is what makes the planetary positions more than symbolism in this system. Neutrinos carry a very small amount of mass, which means they carry information, and they pass through matter without resistance. Three trillion of them pass through every square inch of the planet every second.

A planet in the path of that stream leaves something in it. The Definitive Book uses a deliberately unmystical image. "When the neutrino stream goes through Mars it communicates with Mars and an exchange takes place. After being filtered by Mars, the stream passes through humans and there is another exchange. It's as if a black car and a white car bang into each other, and there is now a little black paint on the white car and a little white paint on the black car." Each planet contributes its essence to the way the stream imprints a person.

This also explains the Sun's dominance. Our Sun is the closest star, and it "produces about 70 percent of all the neutrinos that travel through our solar system." The remaining thirty percent comes from other stars in the Milky Way, with a small share attributed to Jupiter. The Sun therefore carries the heaviest imprint in any chart, which is why the Sun and Earth positions generate Profile and Incarnation Cross while the outer planets do not.

## Fast and Slow

The thirteen do not move at the same speed, and speed changes what a placement means.

The Moon crosses gates rapidly and moves through lines every couple of hours. The Sun spends roughly 5.7 days in each gate and changes line about once a day. Mercury, Venus and Mars move through gates and lines over days to weeks. Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto can sit in a single gate for months or years while still progressing through the lines inside it.

The consequence is that the slow planets describe something shared. Everyone born within a long window carries the same Pluto gate, so a Pluto placement says as much about a generation as about a person. The fast placements are where a chart becomes individual. Contemporary teaching often frames this as the top of the planetary column moving quicker with a deeper personal impact, and the bottom of the column moving slower and carrying generational energies.

Slow does not mean weak. It means the theme is held in common, and that it forms a background rather than a foreground.

## Reading a Planetary Column

Each planet activates a gate, and within that gate a specific line. Both halves carry meaning: the gate gives the theme, and the line gives the way that theme is expressed. Some placements also fix a line in exaltation or detriment, which emphasises one pole of the line's meaning. The Definitive Book is careful that this is not a verdict. "There is no judgment here of good or bad. The detriments in your design are also perfect for you in the continuity of your overall design. In fact, it is generally in the detriments that we have the greatest opportunity to learn."

Two pairs in the column are always exact oppositions. The Sun and Earth sit opposite each other in the wheel, and so do the Nodes. That is a mechanical certainty rather than a feature of a particular chart, and it is why the Sun and Earth are read as a pair, and the Nodes as a pair.

## Where the Planets Sit in a Reading

Planetary detail is not an entry point. The foundation of any reading is [[Energy Types|Type]], [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]], because those describe how a person is designed to make decisions. The planets add colour and specificity to a life already being lived correctly. Read the other way round, a planetary column becomes thirteen more things for the mind to work on, which is the opposite of the intent.

The classical teaching order puts Sun and Earth first, since they carry the most imprint and generate [[Profiles & Lines|Profile]] and the [[Incarnation Cross]]. The Nodes come next, because they divide a life into halves. The rest fill in from there.
