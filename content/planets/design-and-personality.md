# Design and Personality

A Human Design chart is built from two calculations, not one. Every planetary column appears twice, once in red and once in black, and the difference between those two columns is the difference between the body a person lives in and the person they think they are.

"Two calculations are needed to generate a BodyGraph. We begin with the date, place and exact time of each person's birth to establish the Personality or natal calculation." The second is worked backwards from the first. It is the pre-natal or Design calculation, "based on a time approximately 88 days (or precisely 88 degrees of the sun) prior to the actual birth."

The use of two calculations is what most obviously separates this system from traditional astrology. One chart describes a moment. This describes two, and reads the gap between them.

## The 88 Degrees

The Design moment is not a fixed number of days. It is a fixed arc of solar movement, and the days fall where they fall.

The Definitive Book gives it as "88 degrees of solar arc or 3 months prior to your birth." The Jovian dictionary defines the Design calculation as 88 degrees of the Sun prior to the natal or Personality calculation, and elsewhere describes the two calculations as being made "approximately 88 or 89 days apart." The Definitive Book of Human Design puts the same thing in terms of measurement: "The exact time is based on the length of time it takes the Sun to move eighty-eight degrees of arc."

Because the arc is fixed and the Earth's orbit is not perfectly circular, the interval in days varies slightly from person to person. A chart shows both dates. Everyone has a Design birth date as well as a Personality birth date.

One consequence is worth stating clearly, because it comes up constantly. The Design date is calculated backwards from the moment of birth, so the circumstances of that birth do not disturb it. "Since both calculations are based on the moment of your birth, it does not matter whether you were a full-term baby, premature or delivered by caesarian section."

Birth time itself is defined precisely. It is the moment the baby has left the womb and is separate from the mother's body, not the moment the umbilical cord is cut. Accurate birth time matters, and where it is unknown the classical recommendation is chart rectification with an astrologer who specialises in it.

## Red and Black

The two calculations are recorded as two columns. The Design column is printed in red on the left, the Personality column in black on the right. The gate numbers say which hexagrams were activated, and the planetary glyphs say which body was in the neutrino stream of that hexagram.

The distinction between them is not a matter of importance but of access.

Personality is "recorded in black on your BodyGraph; the mind/psyche/light of the soul, that which is eternal; who you 'think you think you are.'" It is the conscious side. A person recognises their black gates when they read them, because those themes are available to introspection and generally feel like self-description.

Design is "neutrino imprinting pertaining to the bioform; a consciousness that lies below one's level of conscious awareness; recorded in red on a BodyGraph." It is the body, and it runs whether or not anyone notices. Red themes are typically recognised by other people first. A person tends to hear them reported back rather than see them directly, which is a reliable way to tell the two columns apart in practice.

Both columns are equally real, and both define centers and channels in exactly the same way. When people say a chart has a defined Sacral, the definition may have come from red, from black, or from one of each.

## The Two Crystals

The mechanism behind the two calculations is the pair of Crystals of Consciousness that Human Design says every living thing carries.

"Every living thing is endowed with two Crystals of Consciousness: the Design Crystal, an aspect of the original Yin, and the Personality Crystal, an aspect of the original Yang." The Design Crystal "transforms the neutrino data into the body and the life," and is described as sitting in the [[Ajna Center]]. The Personality Crystal "transforms the neutrino data into the potential of self reflected consciousness," and is situated just above the [[Head Center]].

Ra Uru Hu described the difference between them in terms of filtering. The neutrino stream passes through both crystals, and each filters it differently. The Design Crystal's filtering governs "the way in which the whole vehicular process is going to operate." The Personality Crystal's filtering "leads to the phenomena that we refer to as mind."

He also drew a sharp line about their histories. "Your Design Crystal is new. Every life brings a new model, a new vehicle, but we have the consistency of a Personality Crystal." In the same teaching he pushed back on the idea that some people are older souls than others, on the grounds that all Personality Crystals date from the same event.

The two calculated moments correspond to two arrivals. The Design Crystal enters at conception and begins building the body. The Personality Crystal arrives later: once the neocortex is fully formed at the end of the second trimester, the Magnetic Monopole signals it into the body, and the moment of birth roughly three months after that is when it receives its imprint from the neutrino stream.

## Conscious and Unconscious in Practice

Some parts of a chart are read as a pair across the two columns, and the pairing carries specific meaning.

[[Profiles & Lines|Profile]] is the clearest example. It combines the line of the Personality Sun and Earth with the line of the Design Sun and Earth, conscious line first. A 1/3 carries a conscious first line and an unconscious third line, which is why the investigating is recognised and the trial and error is often not.

The Design Sun and Earth carry a further meaning in classical teaching. They "represent the direct inheritance from the Father (Sun) and the Mother (Earth)," and are described as genetic themes lived out unconsciously.

Mercury is a useful illustration of the general rule at work in a single planet. On the Personality side, its gate shows what a person needs to communicate and how they will express it. On the Design side it "denotes a theme that is largely unconscious but equally important, and therefore builds pressure until it finds a way to express itself." The same planet, the same kind of theme, and two different relationships to awareness.

## What This Is Not

The red column is not a past life, and it is not a hidden or shadow self in the psychological sense. It is the body's imprinting, calculated at a different moment because the body was formed at a different moment.

Nor does unconscious mean inaccessible forever. Design themes become visible over time through consequence and through feedback from others, and living correctly by [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]] is the classical route to that. What unconscious means is that the theme does not arrive as a thought, so the mind cannot be the instrument that verifies it.
