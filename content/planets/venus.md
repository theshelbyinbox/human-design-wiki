# Venus

Venus is the values planet. It activates one gate and one line on the Design side of a chart and one on the Personality side, and what it puts there is not a preference or a taste. It is a moral position: what a person holds to be right, what they hold to be worth having, and the terms on which they are willing to deal with everyone else.

The two reference sources in the library name the keynote slightly differently, and the difference is worth keeping rather than resolving. The Jovian Human Design Dictionary gives Venus the keynote "Values and Relating". The Definitive Book of Human Design, in its table of the thirteen celestial bodies as teachers, gives "Values/Sociology". Both describe the same mechanism from different ends. Relating is what values do between two people. Sociology is what they do between a person and a community. It is worth noting that the Definitive Book's own longer entry on Venus is headed simply Values, so the sociological half of that keynote is carried by the table rather than by the description underneath it.

## What Venus Establishes

The Definitive Book's entry is the most direct statement of the theme in the library. "Venus establishes our values, and represents our morals and the natural laws for how we deal with each other and the world around us." It adds an archetype and then a warning. "Venus is the archetype of the youngest daughter, and also represents love and beauty." Then: "For you personally, Venus is the right and the wrong, and your moral questions and issues. If you do not act with moral clarity, and if those around you do not act with moral clarity, Venus can be harsh in her retribution."

The dictionary covers the same ground with the relating half in front. "Venus establishes our values, and affects how we interact and relate with each other, and what we deem valuable. The qualities of Venus are associated with beauty, grace, and aesthetics." Beauty and aesthetics belong here, but as an expression of what is held valuable rather than as a separate subject.

Contemporary teaching keeps the emphasis on morals. One widely used gate reference glosses the Venus placement as your moral values and what you love. Another treats the Venus gate as the foundation of a personal moral compass, an energy watched and measured in other people during early childhood and then drawn on for ethical decisions later in life.

## Not the Love Goddess

This is where Human Design and the inherited astrological picture of Venus come apart, and Ra Uru Hu addressed it directly. He described what the classical world did to the archetype. "The Romans who were really macho, and the Greeks, really did a number on Venus. They turned Venus from the archetypal deep, deep, deep yin force into being a kind of love goddess." He set that against a culture that read the same object entirely differently: "To the Mayans, on the other hand, who didn't have the same kind of culture, Venus was the goddess of execution."

His conclusion was not that either version was wrong but that neither was binding. "Now, for me Venus isn't the Roman version and it isn't the Mayan version. I don't have to rely on their concepts."

So Venus in a chart is not a romance indicator. It does have a relationship dimension, because values are the thing two people either share or do not, but the mechanism is moral rather than romantic. Some contemporary teaching also attaches a prosperity theme to Venus. The classical material puts prosperity, expansion and good fortune under [[Jupiter]] instead, and that is the difference to watch for when two readings of the same placement do not agree.

Human Design also uses only the gate and the line. Venus activates a hexagram and a line inside it, not a sign, a house or a rulership, and none of the aspect logic of astrology travels with it.

## Values Become Laws, and Laws Have Consequences

Venus does not act alone. The Definitive Book places it at the head of a three-planet sequence. "The values that are established in Venus become laws in Jupiter, and any retribution takes place through Saturn."

That is a useful piece of structure. Venus is where a standard forms. [[Jupiter]] is where it hardens into the law a person is meant to live by. [[Saturn]] is where the consequences of breaking it are felt. Contemporary teaching follows the same chain, describing the values held in the Venus gates as reflected in the Jupiter gates, with Saturn as the place where being out of integrity shows itself quickly.

## The Sociological Dimension

Sociology is the harder half of the keynote and the more interesting one. A value is never only private. It places a person inside or outside a group, and it decides who they can stand alongside.

The dictionary makes the scope explicit: "Venus represents our personal morals, which determine how we relate on an individual and communal/collective level." That single sentence carries both ends of the Definitive Book's keynote at once.

Ra took the collective end further when teaching the Program, the ongoing planetary conditioning everyone lives inside. He identified three bodies as the controlling agents at the Personality level. The Moon brings the focus, Saturn brings the restraint, and between them sits Venus. "And then there's Venus. And it is Venus that maintains the status quo in the way in which we operate vis-a-vis each other." In his own teaching material the Venus section under this heading is titled The Standard.

The point is not that Venus is malign. It is that shared values are the mechanism by which a society holds its shape, which means they are also the mechanism by which a not-self life gets held in place. Ra described the whole apparatus as the Maya: "The Maya has its focus and its standards and its restraints." Seeing the standard operating is the beginning of not being run by it. See [[Conditioning]].

## The Line Carries the Detail

The dictionary is unusually specific about where to look in a Venus placement: "The Venus theme in our life is described by the gate, and more specifically by the line that is activated."

That emphasis has a mechanical basis. In the Rave I'Ching certain planets fix a line in exaltation or in detriment, weighting one pole of that line's meaning, and Venus is one of the two planets that do this most often. Ra's material on the body carries a section heading that says exactly that: "Venus & Mars Dominate the Rave I'Ching Exaltations & Detriments." A Venus fixing is therefore a common thing to find, and it is the line rather than the gate that tells you which way the theme leans.

Worked examples in the library show the flavour of it. In [[Gate 1]], line 2 is fixed by Venus in exaltation: "Venus exalted as a symbol of beauty. The required harmony between established values and ideals that enriches inspiration." In [[Gate 19]], Venus fixes two lines in opposite directions, exalted in the third and locked in detriment in the fourth, and Ra's expansion of that third line reads "the natural ease with which communion is maintained." In [[Gate 25]], line 4 is one of the rare lines carrying two exaltations at once, Venus and Jupiter together, with no detriment set against them. Contemporary gate commentary counts Venus as the most frequent fixing planet in [[Gate 37]], the Family, where it fixes three lines, which fits a gate whose entire subject is what holds a household together.

Detriment is not a fault. The Definitive Book is explicit that exaltation and detriment are simply another way of saying this and that, that there is no judgment of good or bad, and that it is generally in the detriments that there is the most to learn.

## Conscious and Unconscious Venus

Like every one of the thirteen, Venus is used twice. See [[Design and Personality]].

The Personality Venus, in black, is the value a person knows they hold and would state if asked. It is deliberate, and it is available to the mind.

The Design Venus, in red, is the value the body brings whether or not the person has noticed it. It shows up as what is instinctively carried into relationships and instinctively expected back from them. A gate commentary in the library describes an unconscious Venus in Gate 25 as something brought into relationships and something valued, then adds that it is unconscious, which is precisely the shape of the thing. A Design Venus is usually more obvious to the people around a person than to the person themselves.

Where the two differ, the useful question is not which one is real. It is whether the stated value and the lived value are the same value.

## Speed and Transit

Venus is one of the faster bodies used in a chart. It completes an orbit in about 225 days, and it moves through gates and lines over days to weeks, which places it with Mercury and Mars in the band between the very fast Moon and the slow outer planets. A Venus placement is personal rather than generational: two people born a week apart will not usually share it.

In transit, Venus passing through one of your gates colours it with the values theme for as long as it stays. Contemporary transit commentary treats a Venus retrograde period as a turn toward reflection rather than action, and specifically toward looking back over relationships, since the planet lingers in the gates it passes back through.

## Living With Venus

Planetary detail is not an entry point. [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]] come first, and Ra was direct that Strategy and Authority do not stop the conditioning of the Program at all. What they do is take decision-making away from the mind that the Program is conditioning, so that a transit can be watched inside the mental process without having any power over what gets decided.

Once that is in place, his instruction for the planets was experimental rather than doctrinal. He held that we are here to have our own relationship with them, and warned against trying to digest all thirteen at once: pick a few, watch them, and wait for them to move in and out of your own gates so you can feel what happens when they define you. Venus is a good candidate for that experiment. It is fast enough to move visibly within a single month, and its subject, what you actually value and who you can stand alongside, is one you can check against your own week.
