# Motivation

Motivation is the colour underneath the Personality Sun and Earth. It is the top right of the four arrows at the head of the BodyGraph, it sits on the mind side of Variable, and it points either left, which is strategic, or right, which is receptive. What it describes is not an intention. It is the background frequency that tints whatever the mind says and does, without the mind having any access to it.

Ra Uru Hu taught colour by way of the word motive. "Everybody is looking for motive and the motive for everything we do is in the color," he said, and then went further: "it's this coloring, this filtering that is the underlying motivation for what we do." He used the metaphor of an actor holding a script. The line is written, the role is given, and still the performer asks what the motivation is, because without it the line cannot be delivered. Profile gives the role. Motivation is what colours the delivery.

The uncomfortable part of the teaching is that this layer is not available to introspection. "There is an underlying motivation that's driving us in different ways and our surface doesn't know why," Ra said. He described the purpose of teaching colour bluntly, as telling people why they are helpless and incompetent in the face of something operating beneath them. Motivation is therefore not something to pick, adopt or perform. It is something to recognise, and, more usefully, something whose failure mode can be recognised.

## Mechanics

Motivation is derived from the colour of the Personality Sun and Earth. Colour is the layer of substructure directly beneath the line. Beneath colour sits tone, and beneath tone sits base. Ra called tone and base "the department of planning" and said plainly that we have no access to them.

The same colour layer carries two different names depending on which side of the chart it is read from. On the Design side, the Sun and Earth colour is called [[Determination]] and concerns digestion. On the Personality side, the Sun and Earth colour is called Motivation. Ra was explicit that these are not interchangeable keynotes: colour "has a basic name for design and for personality," and the two sets of keynotes are genuinely different.

The Personality Sun and Earth are the seat of the [[Incarnation Cross]] and of conceptualizing. As Ra put it, the Sun and Earth "conceptualizes all of that and delivers the performance of that role." So Motivation is not a general mood. It is specifically the colouring of the conceptualizing function.

Whether the arrow points left or right is not decided by colour at all. It is decided by the tone beneath, which is also the source of [[Cognition]]. Ra: "It's tone that establishes whether something is to the left or something is to the right." Tones one to three throw the arrow left, tones four to six throw it right.

Left is strategic. Right is receptive. Ra described the left mind as deeply focused, and said that in its focus it loses its periphery, while the right mind is "the new mind," a mutation in the visual cortex that takes in broadly rather than drawing a line to memory. Contemporary reference material lists the left orientation as strategic focus, defined directionality, structure, specificity and concentrated attention, and the right as receptivity, openness, flexibility, peripheral awareness and non-strategic processing. That same source is careful to add that "These are not value judgments." Left is not superior to right.

One practical caution belongs here. Colour is fine-grained enough that birth time matters. Ra advised that to calculate colour reliably you need a birth time accurate "at least within two to three minutes to be safe." A chart with a rounded or remembered birth time may show the wrong colour and therefore the wrong arrow direction.

## Resonance, Harmony and Dissonance with the Lines

The six colours run parallel to the six lines, and Ra read them against each other. A first line with a first colour is resonance: "how you're going to operate on the surface is going to resonate deeply to the motivation underneath." A first colour under a fourth line is harmony, because one and four sit in the same position in the lower and upper trigrams of the hexagram. A first colour under a third line is dissonance, and Ra said that process "is much more uncomfortable."

This is why Motivation is often introduced alongside [[Profiles & Lines]]. The line theme is what shows on the surface. The colour is what pushes it. A person can carry a line theme and a motivation that pull in different directions, and nothing is wrong with that; it simply makes the internal experience less smooth than the diagram suggests.

There is one mechanical curiosity worth recording. Ra noted that every transitional profile, the 1/4, 2/5, 3/6, 4/1, 5/2 and 6/3, carries a sixth colour Personality. All of them therefore share the same underlying motivation, innocence, whatever else differs between them.

## The Six Motivations

The six run in the order below. Names in common use today are given first, with Ra's own vocabulary noted where it differs.

### 1 Fear

The first colour. Ra called fear "the very first motivation," the basic response to being, and tied it directly to the first line's insecurity until there is authority. What drives the first line to dig, research and establish a solid foundation is fear that the foundation is not solid and that everything will otherwise be chaos. Ra gave this colour a binary in his own words: "the underlying motivation is fear and the fear works two ways: you're either a communalist or you're a separatist." The separatist builds a wall and pulls away. The communalist seeks safety in numbers and in shared research. Ra insisted these are not fixed polarities but a prana, an ongoing flow from one state to the other. See [[Motivation: Fear]].

### 2 Hope

The second colour. Ra's developed treatment of it does not use the word hope at all. His contrast is between colour one and colour two as two ways of meeting reality: "The first color is exoteric and the second color is esoteric." Second colour beings, he said, are open to what is not provable, and therefore open to everything that is possible, which is also why a purely first line structure of proof can meet resistance in them. The keynote Hope is the name contemporary teaching uses, and it describes the same territory of possibility and potential. The word does surface in Ra's later material, as a section heading and in passing, but the characterisation he built out is the esoteric one. See [[Motivation: Hope]].

### 3 Desire

The third colour. "Recognize that in its direct resonance to the third line, we're dealing with something that's fundamentally mutative," Ra said. Because it is a mutative force, what comes out of desire is the unexpected, and the unexpected is part of its goal: to reach the place where nobody has been before. Ra gave this colour a binary too. Desire, he said, "has a trajectory of follower to leader." He was uncompromising about how deep that runs, arguing that it is built into us to follow and built into us to lead followers, and that the two states keep turning into each other. See [[Motivation: Desire]].

### 4 Need

The fourth colour, and the place where vocabulary has moved furthest from Ra. He did not call it Need. He called it wanting: "the motivation of the four is what it wants. It wants something." He described it in its transferred form as motivated to want without knowing what it wants, producing a mind that becomes very confused. Contemporary teaching calls this fourth colour Need and characterises it as being moved by what is needed, particularly by the needs of others and of the community. The library does not record a left and right binary for this colour from Ra. See [[Motivation: Need]].

### 5 Guilt

The fifth colour. Ra introduced it as an uncomfortable heading, and gave its binary directly: "The motivation in the fifth color is either to accept conditioning or to be the conditioner." In the same breath he framed the fifth colour's pair of terms as guilt and innocence, which is worth noting so that it is not confused with the sixth colour. Contemporary teaching reads guilt as taking responsibility for what is not working, and therefore as the motivation that reaches for the fix. See [[Motivation: Guilt]].

### 6 Innocence

The sixth colour, and the odd one out. Ra set it against desire and stripped it of drive entirely: "You have something that is innocent. It is totally without motivation. Think about it. The third color full of desire is a motivation. The desire to lead. Six color. No motivation. No motivation. Anti motivation."

That is not a poetic flourish. It is the definition. Where the other five colours describe something pushing from underneath, the sixth describes the absence of push. Ra's point was that this is how sixth colour beings are supposed to be naturally, and that it is precisely not how they show up in the world. The people most visibly motivated to lead, he argued, are often the ones who should carry no motivation to lead at all, because they are in transference to the third. See [[Motivation: Innocence]].

## Transference

Transference is the mechanic Ra defines at the colour level, and Motivation is where he defines it. The definition is short: "Transference is a movement in a harmonic."

The harmonic is the hexagram's own structure. The first line sits at the base of the lower trigram and the fourth at the base of the upper trigram, so one and four are harmonic, two and five are harmonic, three and six are harmonic. Carry that down to colour and the same pairs hold. Under conditioning, a person does not experience their own colour. They experience its harmonic partner.

For Motivation the pairs are therefore:

- Fear and Need, one to four and four to one
- Hope and Guilt, two to five and five to two
- Desire and Innocence, three to six and six to three

Ra's fuller statement puts the cause where it belongs: "In a sense, one's motivation is always being pulled away. It's always being pulled away, being pulled into transference by conditioning. This same pulling away with the nodes is called distraction."

Two things follow from that sentence. First, the same mechanic runs in both mind side arrows, but it is only called transference at the Sun and Earth. At the Personality Nodes it is called distraction, and that is the word to use when discussing [[Perspective]]. Second, transference is not a personal failing or a phase. It is the default condition of a being who is not living correctly. Elsewhere Ra says it directly: "Motivation is subject to transference, and transference is the byproduct of incorrectness."

The mechanism he described is physical rather than psychological. The magnetic monopole captures the colour frequency emitted through the crystal. When the overall frequency of the life is not correct, a distortion field forms and the monopole captures the harmonic instead of the actual colour. A six is emitted and a three is pulled in. What is lost in that substitution is not only the colour but everything underneath it. Ra put it this way: the moment "that you transfer to that four, you lose all your cognitive values." The true motivation is backed by the tone and the sense beneath it; the transferred one is not backed by anything, which is why it produces confusion rather than drive.

Ra was emphatic that the colour itself is never the problem. The colour is emitted perfectly. The failure is always in the capture.

## Transference as a Practical Signal

This is where Motivation becomes usable rather than merely interesting. Because the transferred state is a specific, named, predictable thing, it functions as a signpost in the same way that a not-self theme does for Type.

The logic runs in one direction only. You cannot verify your motivation by inspecting it, because the layer is below awareness. What you can notice is the arrival of its harmonic partner. A person whose colour is fear who finds themselves circling what they want, without being able to say what it is, has the shape of the four rather than the one. A person whose colour is innocence who finds themselves assembling goals and campaigning to lead has the shape of the three. Ra used himself as the example, a third colour Personality Sun and Earth who in transference wanted to lead nothing and take responsibility for nothing, and called it a very easy way out.

The correction is never to adopt the correct motivation, because motivation cannot be adopted. The correction is to go back to the entry point: whether the thing was entered through [[Strategy]] and decided by [[Inner Authority]]. Transference reports that the frequency is off. Strategy and authority are the only things that change the frequency. Contemporary teaching frames the same move as backtracking, checking first whether the decision was made correctly and only then asking which motivation is running.

It also explains something about the [[Not-Self]] world at scale. Ra pointed out that the transferred and distracted themes are what the planetary program broadcasts, so the pull toward the harmonic is constant and collective, not merely personal.

## The Prerequisite

Motivation belongs to the fourth of [[The Four Transformations]], and the order of those transformations is not optional. The chain runs dietary regimen through [[Determination]], then [[Environment]], then [[Perspective]], then awareness and conceptualizing here at the Personality Sun and Earth. Ra: "if the mind does not have access to correct perspective, then the mind can never, ever, ever, ever, ever be correct. And you cannot have the correct perspective if you're not in the right place. And if you're not in the right place, and you're not eating correctly, then there is no way that you're going to go through any of these transformations."

Before any of that, there is [[Strategy]] and authority. Ra called them the catalyst, not the content. Strategy and authority end the rule of mind and begin the removal of resistance; they do not by themselves make anyone aware. The seven year cellular [[Deconditioning]] process runs underneath. Variable, in his framing, is what becomes available afterwards, and it is not a shortcut around the foundations. Contemporary reference material says the same thing in plainer words: Variable "does not function correctly without Strategy and Authority."

The endpoint of the fourth transformation has a name. Ra called it outer authority, and described the Personality Sun and Earth as "the fourth and final transformation that brings about cognitive awareness. The ability to conceptualize exactly the way you were designed to." Outer authority is defined in the Human Design dictionary as "the true and proper realm of mind," the mind liberated from making decisions and become a source of knowledge for others. Motivation matters because it is the colouring of that conceptualizing. A mind in transference conceptualizes from a motivation that is not its own, working on material supplied by a view that is distracted, and whatever it produces is not outer authority.

## Naming Traps and Common Misreadings

Three points of vocabulary cause most of the confusion.

The word wanting names two different things. Ra used it for Motivation colour four. Contemporary teaching uses Need for Motivation colour four and reserves Wanting for [[Perspective: Wanting]], the fourth colour of the Personality Nodes. Both usages are in circulation, so it is worth checking which arrow is under discussion before reading anything into the word.

Ra's binaries exist for three colours only. Fear is communalist or separatist. Desire moves from follower to leader. Guilt is the conditioner or the one who accepts conditioning. The library does not give left and right binaries for Hope, Need or Innocence, and none should be invented for them.

Finally, Motivation is not a preference or a brand. It cannot be chosen, improved or swapped, and forcing a preferred style is described in contemporary reference material as pure not-self behaviour. Nor should one arrow be read in isolation from the other three. The four arrows work as a chain, and Motivation is the last link in it.
