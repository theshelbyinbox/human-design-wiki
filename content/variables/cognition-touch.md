# Cognition: Touch

Touch is the sixth tone, the second half of the solar plexus pair in the tonal architecture and the last of the six. Ra Uru Hu defines it in one sentence in the Rave Anatomy material: "In talking about the 6 I'm talking about a tactile sensitivity in which contact can lead to the absorption of enormous amounts of discernable information." That is the whole mechanism. Contact is not merely felt. It carries data, and the sixth tone is the equipment for reading it.

This is a cognitive category rather than a preference or a personality trait. Tone is what Ra calls the cognitive architecture, the layer of the substructure that sets out how a being is built to take in and interpret the world. Of the six ways that architecture can be wired, Touch is the furthest along the right side. It is not a talent for physical affection, and it has nothing to do with the dietary circumstance of the same name, which is read off colour rather than tone and is covered below.

## Mechanics

Tone comes from the substructure beneath gate and line. Ra describes a neutrino stream striking a crystal of consciousness: the entry frequency is base, the frequency inside the crystal is tone, the exit frequency is colour. Tone therefore sits under colour, and colour under line. Ra is explicit that the architecture runs upward. "From the bottom up, it is the tone that leads to the color that leads to the line that leads to the gate and so forth and so on."

Cognition is read from the tone beneath the Sun and Earth, a configuration Ra calls "a 70% key to the programming". In the Variable Overview lectures he locates it in the Design Sun, calling the upper left corner of each variable configuration "the cognitive potential that is there in the design sun"; in the Rave Anatomy material he discusses tone under either the Personality or the Design Sun and Earth.

Tone is graphed as a triangle, and Ra says the shape is deliberate, since colour is drawn as a hexagon and base as a square and the triangle "mirrors the BodyGraph itself". It is split down the middle into a left side and a right side, and the six tones are grouped in binaries within it. The splenic binary, tones one and two, occupies the lower left. The ajna binary, tones three and four, sits at the top. The solar plexus binary, tones five and six, occupies the lower right. Touch is the far end of that corner, the last position in the sequence and the one furthest from tone one.

There is no left or right variant name for Touch, or for any of the six cognitions. Tone does not take sub-labels the way Determination values do; what it does instead is decide direction for everything above it. Ra: "It is tone that establishes whether something is to the left or something is to the right." A sixth tone under any of the four Variable calculations turns that arrow to the right.

## Contact as a Mode of Knowing

The claim Ra makes for the fifth and sixth tones is unusually large. He is not describing heightened comfort but a quantity of information. He calls this "a level of sensory sensitivity that is not something other than unusual", and notes that the recognisable examples in the population tend to be mutative or the result of injuries rather than ordinary variation.

The underlying logic is frequency. In the Tone lecture Ra argues that everything is frequency at differing densities, and that cognition is simply the interpretation of frequency. What differs between people is the instrument. "Do you interpret the frequency" he asks, and then lists the options: "with your nose, with your mouth, with your eyes, with your whole body, with your touch." For the sixth tone the instrument is the whole surface of contact, and what registers is not a feeling about an encounter but the content of it. Reaching the solar plexus in the same lecture, he speaks of "capacities that have to do with sensory attunement to frequencies in the environment that the left has never known", naming the auric field, the electromagnetic field and the tactile field. Touch is the tactile end of that list.

## The Solar Plexus Binary and the Wave

Ra treats the gap between the tonal potential of the solar plexus pair and what shows up on the surface as the widest in the system. "For me this difference between the BodyGraph Solar Plexus sensory potential and the tonal, this is the greatest divide." The reason is the motor: the Solar Plexus on the surface runs a wave, and the wave stands between the tonal potential and its clean expression.

The practical consequence is that at the BodyGraph level touch registers as emotional affect. "What we're talking about is the emotional response, for example, in touch, what is the emotional affect of that touch. And that is an energy quality that is taken in." Ra does not treat this as worthless; he says emotional feeling and emotional touching in the wave context are more profound in impact than any thought or any splenic spontaneity. But it is not the tonal capacity, and for the vast majority of humanity these senses operate in a way that is "very crude and superficial" relative to what they could be. The route out is the standard one for the emotional system: patience, and the processing of the wave over time rather than the reading of any single moment.

He also notes that these senses are "fundamentally social. This is the great contact awareness." Touch requires something to be in contact with, which is why the solar plexus pair is the social end of the tonal architecture.

## Touch and Feeling as a Pair

Tones five and six are a binary and are best understood against each other. Ra draws the line by what is taken in. The fifth tone, [[Cognition: Feeling]], is "a capacity to take in complex information from auras, from the electromagnetic field, from chemistry". The sixth tone is the tactile sensitivity, where information arrives through contact. Feeling therefore operates at a distance, reading the field around a thing; Touch operates at zero distance, reading the thing itself. Both belong to the same awareness centre and both are distorted by the same wave, which is why Ra discusses them together and warns against reading either at the surface as if it were the architecture underneath.

## The Rightmost Tone

The split in the tonal triangle happens in the ajna, between tone three and tone four, and Ra describes crossing it as a change of mode: from the strategic and active left to the passive body and receptive right. Touch is the last tone after that split.

Ra frames this as a direction of travel rather than a ranking. The last fully right beings, he says, were Neanderthal and purely splenic; the last fully left beings were the seven centred, ajna dominated humans. "When we look to the future, the future is the solar plex." The solar plexus binary is where the mutation is heading, and the sixth tone is its far edge. Contemporary teaching in the library keynotes the pair as the fifth tone of Feeling and Judgment and the sixth tone of Touch and Acceptance, and describes a future design in which those mutated tones produce a steady emotional intelligence in place of the unaware wave. That pairing is not Ra's own wording.

Left is not better than right, and the corpus states this directly: "These are not value judgments." A sixth tone is not an advanced tone. It is a different instrument.

## Not the Same as Determination: Touch

[[Determination: Touch]] shares the name and nothing else. It is the fourth of the six Determinations, read from the colour of the Design Sun and Earth rather than from tone, and it is a dietary circumstance concerning the social setting in which a person eats. Its two variants are Calm Touch, which digests best alone and away from other people's drama, and Nervous Touch, which digests best with activity and company at a shared table.

Nothing about Determination Touch concerns physical contact or cognition. The two sit in different layers of the substructure and are calculated from different frequencies. A chart can carry one, both or neither.

## Common Misreadings

The first misreading is treating the tone as a description of behaviour. Ra is careful that tone is potential only. "Remember, that is only a potential." He adds that a not-self being is cut off from their cognition and therefore from the architecture underneath, and that step one for reaching it is [[Strategy]] and authority. The capacity is in the crystal whether or not it ever reaches the surface.

The second is confusing the sixth tone with the sixth line. Ra has a well known passage in which he says "the magic is in the word 'touch'" and discusses what it means to have the touch and to have been touched. That passage is explicitly about the sixth line being and the living example, and Ra says in it that those beings are moving away from the tactile. It is not a description of tone six, and quoting it as such inverts the meaning.

The third is expecting a transference partner. The corpus names transference for Motivation and distraction for Perspective, both running on the harmonic that joins three and six. It does not describe an equivalent slippage between tones, and none should be assumed here.
