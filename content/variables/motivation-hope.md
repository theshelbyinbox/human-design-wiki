# Motivation: Hope

Hope is the second of the six values of Motivation, the colour layer read from the Personality Sun and Earth. Motivation is the underlying drive beneath the mind, the thing that gives a line its reason for being played the way it is played. Ra Uru Hu put it through a theatre metaphor: the actor looks at the script and asks what the motivation is, because the same line delivered from a different underlying drive is not the same line at all.

Ra's own developed treatment of this second colour does not call it hope. He calls it esoteric, and sets it against the first colour, which he calls exoteric. In the lecture series on colour he says plainly: "The first color is exoteric and the second color is esoteric." He immediately flags the level of the claim: "Now, that's a generalization but it's very important to grasp." The name Hope belongs mostly to contemporary teaching, which describes this colour as being motivated by possibility and potential.

Both names are pointing at the same slot in the same structure. They are not pointing at it from the same angle, and the difference is worth holding rather than collapsing.

## Mechanics

Motivation is the colour of the Personality Sun and Earth. It appears in the chart as the top right arrow, on the mind side of [[Variables Overview|Variable]]. The arrow's direction is not set by colour at all. It is set by the tone beneath, which is why Ra says of the layers: "It is the tone after all, that creates in essence what the color is going to be." Tones one to three point the arrow left, tones four to six point it right. Left is strategic and right is receptive. Nothing in the library ties either orientation to Hope in particular.

Hope is colour two. Colour resonates with the profile line of the same number and harmonises with the line three places away, so colour two resonates with the second line and harmonises with the fifth. Ra states the principle directly: "color has a relationship to line: if you have a first line and you have a first color, they're in resonance." Where colour and line do not resonate or harmonise, he calls the relationship dissonance, and describes it as a less comfortable process.

Three of the six Motivations carry a binary in Ra's material. Fear runs communalist to separatist, Desire runs follower to leader, and Guilt runs conditioner to one who accepts conditioning. No binary pair is given anywhere in this library for colour two. Sources that supply one for Hope are adding something the recorded material does not contain.

## The naming question

Three things are true at once, and the honest presentation keeps all three.

First, the sustained teaching on colour two in Ra's colour lectures is built entirely around the word esoteric, in contrast with the exoteric first colour. That is where the mechanical description lives.

Second, the word hope does surface in Ra's later material, though only in passing. In one recorded lecture, describing how the daily transit motivations condition everyone at once, he says the whole planet "is going to be motivated by fear or hope or whatever". And the contents of the Holistic Analysis 2 lecture carry a section heading that reads "2nd Line: The Call/2nd Color: Hope". The heading exists. The body of that section is not present in this library, so what he said under it cannot be reported here.

Third, contemporary teaching has standardised on Hope, and that is the name most readers will meet first.

A related caution belongs here, because Motivation names are slippery in more than one place. Ra calls Motivation colour four "wanting", while contemporary teaching calls it Need and reserves Wanting for the fourth value of [[Perspective]]. The same word ends up naming two different things in two different arrows. Similarly, the second value of Perspective is called Possibility, and contemporary teaching also reaches for the word possibility when describing Hope. They are different arrows on different sides of the chart.

## What Ra describes in the second colour

Ra's account of colour two is about the unprovable. He builds it out of the difference between the first and second lines: "The first line always has something to prove and the second line doesn't." The first line needs a foundation it can demonstrate to others, and in the prana of communalist and separatist it finds the opportunity to prove something. The second line pursues truth as a personal process, not as something it must share or evidence.

From there the colour follows. "All beings that carry second color themes, because they are open to what is not provable, they're open to absolutely everything that's possible." Ra lists the range without irony: open to the Virgin Mary coming for dinner, open to seeing the light of Christ, open to every kind of potential experience. Underneath, he says, there is a need to believe in something, and that need is not touched by how logical the rest of the chart is. Colour is not personal, and it does not care what type or definition sits above it.

He also notes the friction this creates with a system built on proof. "You lock somebody who is basically second color into a first line structure ... and you will meet resistance." He reports second colour people asking of Human Design itself, "Where's the spirituality? Where's the stuff we can have that we can't prove so we can believe in it?" His own view was that the synthesis has to hold both, that Human Design "comes from the unbelievable, but it proves out in the reality."

## What contemporary teaching says Hope attends to

Later teaching gives the same colour a shorter description: motivated by possibility and potential. It is characterised as a way of seeing that inclines toward what could happen rather than what can be demonstrated. Motivation in this framing colours the whole chart, so everything arrives through a lens, and for this colour the lens is hope.

The same teaching stresses that Motivation is subtler than the other Variable arrows, more likely to drive or stall someone without being noticed, precisely because it sits below the line and is not available to conscious inspection.

## Transference to Guilt

Motivation is subject to transference, a slippage along the harmonic under [[Conditioning|conditioning]]. Ra describes the movement as one to four, two to five, three to six, "or the other way around", and adds that one's motivation is always being pulled away into transference by conditioning. The nodal version of the same mechanic, affecting Perspective rather than Motivation, he calls distraction rather than transference.

For colour two the transferred state is [[Motivation: Guilt|Guilt]], colour five. Ra's binary for the fifth colour is that "The motivation in the fifth color is either to accept conditioning or to be the conditioner." Contemporary teaching reads Guilt as being motivated by taking responsibility and fixing things, and treats it as the signpost for someone whose true Motivation is Hope: the pull toward repairing and correcting, in place of the openness to what is possible.

Ra's warning about transference is that the transferred motivation is not backed by the tone and the cognition underneath it. A mind running on a motivation that is not its own is conceptualising on inferior information.

## The prerequisite

Variable is not a shortcut. Ra treats [[Strategy|Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority|Authority]] as the catalyst that has to come first: "first the catalyzation and then you enter into the variable itself." The transformations then run in order, from dietary regimen to environment to perspective to awareness. If someone is not operating according to their Strategy and Authority, he says, they get the harmonic instead of the real thing, and the mind conceptualises from a motivation that is not correct.

Read outside that sequence, a Motivation label is a description with nothing underneath it. Read inside it, it names what the mind is actually running on.
