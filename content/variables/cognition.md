# Cognition

Cognition is the way a person takes the world in. Ra Uru Hu described it as the layer of the design where the senses live, and he was blunt about where it comes from: "what tone represents is cognition. In simple language, the senses as we experience them." Tone is the substructure layer beneath colour, and each of the six tones carries one of six cognitions: Smell, Taste, Outer Vision, Inner Vision, Feeling and Touch.

The first thing to say about Cognition is structural, and it corrects the expectation most readers arrive with. Cognition is not a fifth arrow. The four arrows on a Variable chart come from colour, and there are exactly four of them because there are four colour readings. Tone sits one layer below colour, and it does something the colour layer cannot do for itself: it decides which way each arrow points. Cognition therefore sits underneath the whole arrow system rather than beside it. It is the floor the arrows stand on.

Ra called tone "our cognitive architecture" and treated it as the blueprint of a being rather than a trait. In the lecture on tone he described the tonal triangle as a plan drawn before the building exists, something "designed from the bottom up." What emerges on the surface, in the BodyGraph and in a life, may or may not match that plan. Access to one's own cognition is not automatic. It depends on living correctly, which in Human Design means [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]].

## Mechanics

The substructure comes out of the interaction between a crystal of consciousness and the neutrino stream. Ra's illustration is simple. A neutrino approaches the crystal, and the frequency at the point of contact is the entry frequency, which is base. While the neutrino is inside the crystal, that interior is tone. When the neutrino exits, the exit frequency is colour. Base is the general condition, colour is what gets transmitted outward, and tone is the inside, the part that differentiates one crystal from another. Every crystal is unique, Ra said, in the way that every snowflake is unique, and it is the inside that makes it so.

Reading runs upward, not downward. "From the bottom up, it is the tone that leads to the color that leads to the line that leads to the gate," Ra said, which inverts the order in which almost everyone learns Human Design. A planetary activation lands on a base, inside a tone, inside a colour, inside a line, inside a gate.

There are six tones, matching the six colours and the six lines. Graphically they are arranged as a triangle, and Ra made a point of this shape. Base is drawn as a square, colour as a hexagon, and tone as a triangle with the same dimensions as the BodyGraph's own triangular form. He read that as deliberate: the tonal triangle is a microcosm of the surface, "the underlying architecture" the BodyGraph was built on.

Tone is read at the same four Variable positions as colour: the Design Sun and Earth, the Design Nodes, the Personality Nodes, and the Personality Sun and Earth. For sensory potential specifically, Ra pointed at the luminaries. "When we are looking at sensory potential at deeper levels we are looking at it through the sun/earth configuration." On the design side that tone speaks to the brain and its development, and it is the tone the Primary Health System works with when setting dietary regimen. On the personality side, the tone describes the architecture of the conceptualizing process, the way the sensory system is intended to operate for a differentiated mind.

## Not a Fifth Arrow

The four transformations arise from four calculations, and Ra listed them plainly: the Design Sun and Earth, the Design Nodes, the Personality Nodes, and the Personality Sun and Earth. Of those four he then said something that settles the question of where cognition belongs. "These elements are impacted directly by tone. In other words, these are elements that can be drawn to the left or to the right by the influence of the architecture underneath."

That is the whole mechanism. The tonal triangle is split down the middle. Tones 1, 2 and 3 fall on the left of the split; tones 4, 5 and 6 fall on the right. An arrow points left because the tone beneath it is a 1, a 2 or a 3, and it points right because the tone beneath it is a 4, a 5 or a 6. "It's tone that establishes whether something is to the left or something is to the right," Ra said. "It is the architecture that determines it."

So the left and right orientations described on [[The Four Arrows]] page, the strategic and receptive of mind, the active and passive of body, the focused and peripheral of view, are all downstream of tone. There is no separate calculation for arrow direction. There is only tone, read four times.

When Ra introduced the advanced imaging chart used in PHS and Rave Psychology, colour and tone were shown as two shapes beside each luminary, "the six-sided shape" for colour and "the triangular shape" for tone. Tone was never drawn as an additional arrow. It was drawn as the layer that produces them.

## The Three Binaries

The six tones are grouped in pairs, and the pairs are named for the three awareness centres. "In the tonal architecture we see it as Splenic binary, Ajna binary, Solar Plexus binary." Tones 1 and 2 form the splenic binary at the lower left of the triangle. Tones 3 and 4 form the ajna binary at the apex. Tones 5 and 6 form the solar plexus binary at the lower right.

The pairing is not decoration. It says which awareness system a given cognition is wired through, and it explains the geometry of the split. Because the ajna binary sits at the top and the split runs vertically, the divide between left and right passes straight through it, separating tone 3 from tone 4. That is the same place, in Ra's account, where the mutation of 1781 occurred: in the visual cortex, in the language of Design the [[Ajna Center]]. The architecture divides where the species divided.

The order of the three binaries also carries an evolutionary reading. "The evolutionary pattern is from Spleen to Ajna and from Ajna to Solar Plexus, this is the progression of evolutionary awareness." The splenic pair is the oldest and, in Ra's framing, the only awareness that can be trusted. The ajna pair belongs to the era of mind-dominated existence. The solar plexus pair points at an awareness he described as still emerging.

## The Six Cognitions

### 1 Smell

[[Cognition: Smell|Smell]] is the first tone, splenic, and the most basic layer of the whole architecture. Ra described it as a defence posture: "the very first tone is simply on alert all the time." In a lecture on colour he put it more compactly still, calling the first tone "the tone of security and that security is based on smell." At the tonal level this is not the nose as an organ so much as a continuous, low-level scanning for threat, the intelligence that keeps a form alive.

### 2 Taste

[[Cognition: Taste|Taste]] is the second tone, also splenic, and Ra treated it as the odd one in the pair. Where the first tone protects against what is already known, the second is "ready for what doesn't exist." He called it the ability "to have the sensitivity to be ready to recognize something new the moment it emerges," and named it an uncertainty principle in the architecture. He also noted, without elaborating, that these are mouth breathers, a different route for taking information in. Note that Taste is also the name of the second value of [[Determination]], where it means something else entirely.

### 3 Outer Vision

[[Cognition: Outer Vision|Outer Vision]] is the third tone, the first half of the ajna binary and the last tone on the left. Ra called it "the epitome of what it is to be strategic." It is binocular vision put to use: looking at the world, picking a focus, building a dimensional model of the environment out of that focus, and acting on it. He traced the whole strategic inheritance of the seven-centred being to this capacity, and traced the not-self mind to it as well, since a mind that can see, focus and plan is a mind that starts believing it should be in charge.

### 4 Inner Vision

[[Cognition: Inner Vision|Inner Vision]] is the fourth tone, the second half of the ajna binary and the first tone on the right. It is where the mutation shows. Ra described the movement from three to four as a movement from focused outer vision to "this breathing in and inner meditation and inner vision." His one worked example is an image rather than a definition: someone is walking towards you, and you close your eyes. "You're going to take in every piece of information that you need to take in about them. You just will. You just take it all in." Ra's recorded material on the two visual cognitions is comparatively brief, and most of what exists comes from this single lecture on tone.

### 5 Feeling

[[Cognition: Feeling|Feeling]] is the fifth tone, the first half of the solar plexus binary. At the tonal level Ra described it as "a capacity to take in complex information from auras, from the electromagnetic field, from chemistry." This is not emotion. He was explicit that what the BodyGraph currently gives us in the [[Solar Plexus Center]] is a motor and a wave, and that the wave stands between the person and the cognitive potential underneath. What the fifth tone describes is a sensitivity to frequency in the surrounding field, of a kind he said is usually only glimpsed.

### 6 Touch

[[Cognition: Touch|Touch]] is the sixth tone, the second half of the solar plexus binary and the furthest point on the right. Ra defined it as "a tactile sensitivity in which contact can lead to the absorption of enormous amounts of discernable information." Contact carries data, and the sixth tone is the capacity to read it. As with Taste, the name collides with a value in another category: [[Determination: Touch|Touch]] in Determination concerns the social setting of eating, not this.

## The Acoustic Seventh

There is a seventh element that sits differently from the six. Discussing the solar plexus tones, Ra remarked that "within the context of tone there are seven senses," with sound, understood as frequency itself, embracing the rest. The architecture, he said, is built on frequency from the start, since the neutrino passing through the crystal is a frequency event. Sound is not a seventh cognition to be assigned to anyone. It is the medium the other six work in. In the BodyGraph he mapped the same idea onto an acoustic triangle with a right ear in the now, a left ear in the wave, and an inner ear belonging to individuality.

Sound also appears as the fifth value of Determination, where it names something else again.

## Cognition as a Mode of Knowing

The most common misreading of Cognition is to treat it as a preferred sense organ, as though a Smell person simply has a good nose. Ra's framing is different. "Everything that we call cognition is our ability to interpret frequency," he said, and the six tones are the tools we interpret it with. "Do you interpret the frequency ... with your nose, with your mouth, with your eyes, with your whole body, with your touch." The question is not which sense is sharpest. It is which sense is the route by which a person knows anything at all.

He also drew a hard line between the tonal level and the surface. "When you look at cognition at the tonal level, it is very profound. And when you look at senses at the BodyGraph level, they're very mundane." At the surface the senses are tools for survival, reproduction and cultural development, and they are meant to be mundane. Smell at the tonal level is immune-system security; smell arriving in the BodyGraph, through gates such as [[Gate 44]], [[Gate 54]], [[Gate 32]] and [[Gate 26]], becomes the smell of money and the stink of failure. "The BodyGraph is a sensory system, period," Ra said. The same theme runs through both levels, but it does not mean the same thing at both.

## Cognition and Determination

Cognition and [[Determination]] are read off the same side of the chart, the body side, and off the same activation. Determination is the colour of the Design Sun and Earth; the cognition worked with in PHS is the tone beneath that same colour. This is why the two are taught together rather than as separate subjects.

The link is functional, not merely positional. Ra said the tonal influence sets the general parameters of a dietary regimen, and then went further: it is "also going to specifically align that dietary regimen to the enhancement of one particular sense, the one that's right for you, the one that is natural for you." Eating correctly is what gives the brain the conditions in which a particular cognition can develop. In a lecture on nutrition he put it as directly as he ever did: the tone underneath "is a potential that is concretized in the brain as a sense."

This also explains the ordering of [[The Four Transformations]]. Dietary regimen comes first because cognition is built in the body before it can be used by the mind. [[Environment]] and [[Perspective]] follow, and conceptualizing comes last.

## No Left and Right Variants

The value pages in some other categories carry paired variant names, so that Determination's Sound splits into High and Low, and Thirst into Hot and Cold. Cognition has no equivalent. There is no left and right pair attached to Smell, or to Feeling, or to any of the other four, and none should be inferred.

The reason is structural. Left and right are what tone produces for the layers above it. A tone does not have a left version and a right version, because the tone's own position in the triangle is the left or the right. Tones 1, 2 and 3 are the left; tones 4, 5 and 6 are the right. Asking whether someone has a left Inner Vision is asking whether the fourth tone is on the left half of a triangle it defines the right half of.

## Access and Conditioning

Having a tone and having access to it are separate matters. Ra returned to this constantly. The plans are perfect and mostly unbuilt: "most human beings are built rather haphazardly on their architecture." The [[Not-Self]] is cut off from its own cognition, and therefore, as he put it, "cut off from their architecture underneath."

He described the failure mechanically. The Magnetic Monopole holds the form together by attracting the colour frequencies emitted from the crystals. If the vehicle is not operating correctly, the attraction frequency is disturbed, and what comes through is not the true tonal architecture but a harmonic distortion of the colour, so that a 1 arrives as a 4, a 2 as a 5, a 3 as a 6. The distortion runs along the same harmonic that governs transference in [[Motivation]] and distraction in Perspective.

The remedy Ra gave was never a technique aimed at the senses. It was [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]], which align the vehicle to a correct frequency and, in doing so, put a person back in contact with the cognition that was always theirs. [[Deconditioning]] at the cellular level, in his teaching, takes seven years. Variable is not a shortcut past Type, Strategy and Authority; those remain the catalyst and the precondition.
