# Perspective: Power

Power is the third of the six perspectives, the six ways of seeing carried by the colour of the Personality Nodes. A person whose view is set to this colour looks at a situation and registers, before anything else, how force is arranged inside it. Who holds position. How influence actually moves, as opposed to how the organisation chart says it moves. What the structure is, and where the leverage sits in it. Contemporary teaching also calls this the political view, and describes it as perceiving the underlying power structures and hierarchies in every situation, with a strength in understanding and navigating the game.

The name is a trap worth clearing at once. Power here is the name of a way of seeing, a colour beneath a line on the Personality Nodes. It has nothing to do with [[Channel 34/57]], the channel that also carries the keynote of power. The two use the same English word for entirely different mechanics.

Like every perspective, this is a potential rather than a guarantee. Contemporary teaching is explicit that a way of seeing is a skill to be honed, and that carrying a colour does not mean a person is good at what that colour sees. It is simply the frame the eyes are built for, and what it can offer depends on the rest of the chart being in good standing.

## Mechanics

Perspective is derived from the colour of the Personality Nodes. Colour is the first layer of substructure beneath the line of an activation, and there are six colours in every line. The Personality Nodes hold the bottom right arrow of Variable, which sits on the mind side of the chart and describes how the mind is designed to view its environment. Power is colour 3 in the sequence Survival, Possibility, Power, Wanting, Probability, Personal.

Ra Uru Hu put the derivation plainly in his lecture on colour, describing "what color brings to the personality nodes, perspective, view, the way of seeing that is absolutely correct for you and it is your color."

Colour decides which of the six views a person carries. It does not decide the direction the arrow points. That is set by tone, the layer beneath colour, and the bottom right arrow reads as Focused when it points left and Peripheral when it points right. Left and right are orientations, not rankings. A contemporary reference on Variable states the point directly: "These are not value judgments."

The corpus gives no left and right variant names for the individual perspectives the way it does for several of the determinations and environments. There is a Focused Power view and a Peripheral Power view because every bottom right arrow points one way or the other, but no source in this library supplies distinct keynotes for the two.

One naming overlap is worth flagging while the sequence is in view. Contemporary teaching calls perspective 4 Wanting, while Ra used the word wanting for motivation colour 4, which contemporary teaching calls Need. The same word names two different slots depending on which source is speaking.

## Seeing the Arrangement of Force

What this view picks up is the game and the players. Contemporary teaching describes people with this perspective as seeing power structures, seeing hierarchies, seeing how the game is actually played, seeing who matters and who does not, and being drawn to hierarchical structures generally.

A contemporary teacher who carries this colour has described the pattern from the inside. As a teenager the ambition was to run a political campaign, driven by a persistent sense of seeing what the people already in the game were getting wrong and seeing how the strings were pulled. Later, in a corporate career, the same faculty showed up as knowing immediately who the important players were and how to form the relationships and alliances that mattered. The perception itself was never in question. What varied was what got done with it.

The same account names the underside of the view. Seeing a hierarchy clearly includes seeing your own place in it, and that can register as being small, which produces a reluctance to step up rather than a capacity to manoeuvre. Seeing the structure and feeling diminished by the structure are not the same thing, and the first does not have to produce the second.

The corpus also sets this view against its neighbours. Someone whose perspective is Possibility sees what could be, with no verdict attached about whether it will happen. Someone whose perspective is [[Perspective: Probability|Probability]] sees what is realistically doable and can be repelled by political manoeuvring altogether. Set beside those, the Power view is neither aspirational nor purely practical. It is structural. It reads the arrangement first and asks what can be done with that arrangement second.

## Correct Environment Comes First

No perspective is available on demand. It is the third of the four transformations and it depends on the two that come before it, dietary regimen and then environment. Ra was emphatic about the order: "if the mind does not have access to correct perspective, then the mind can never, ever, ever, ever, ever be correct. And you cannot have the correct perspective if you're not in the right place."

Put the other way round, from the Variable overview material: "Because if you're in the correct environment, then you're going to see what you're here to see."

This is why the [[Environment]] arrow, which sits directly across from perspective on the body side, is the practical entry point. Reading about the Power view will not install it. Being in the wrong place reliably prevents it. None of this operates outside [[Strategy]] and authority either, which are the catalyst for the whole process rather than something Variable lets a person skip.

## Distraction and the Personal View

When a person is not operating correctly, perspective does not simply fade. It slips along a harmonic to another colour. Ra named this movement distraction, and distinguished it from the transference that happens on the motivation side. In his lecture on distraction he described it as a case of a one moving to a four, calling it "this harmonic movement that pulls you away from seeing the way you're supposed to see."

The harmonic pairs are 1 with 4, 2 with 5, and 3 with 6. Power therefore pairs with [[Perspective: Personal]]. Ra confirmed this pairing from his own chart, in one of the few places where he spoke about his own perspective directly: "I'm a six. My view, my perspective is personal, but the distraction is a view to power. I was not self a long time. My view was totally distracted. I did not see clearly. I did not see at all."

Read from the other end, a person whose correct view is Power is pulled under conditioning toward the Personal view, toward relating what is seen back to the self rather than to the structure. The corpus states the mechanism and the pairing but does not describe in detail what that particular distortion looks like in daily life, so nothing more specific should be claimed for it.

What the corpus does describe is the cost. The view is what feeds the conceptualizing mind. Ra made the point while walking through the Possibility and Probability pair: "it is here in your view that you establish what your mind is going to process." A distracted view hands the mind material it was never supposed to be working with, and the mind then builds concepts on something that is not there.

## Resonance with Line 3 and Harmony with Line 6

Colours resonate with profile lines by number, so colour 3 resonates with the third line and harmonises with the sixth. That is the same 3 and 6 relationship that shows up in the distraction pairing, and it is the same relationship that makes [[Profile 3/6]] one of the harmony profiles the corpus identifies.

Ra described the general effect of the third colour on a third line as mental pressure rather than physical drive. He said that the third and fourth colours are "pushing you to mental resolutions" and that this is "an imperative that's juicing up your mental approach to life and forcing you to look at things from a mental perspective." He added that it can frustrate people who expect action and get analysis instead.

That fits the mind side placement of the perspective arrow. Seeing how power is arranged is not itself a move. It is information delivered to a mind whose proper job, once the chain of transformations is intact, is outer authority rather than control of the person's own life.
