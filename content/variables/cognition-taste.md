# Cognition: Taste

Taste is the second of the six cognitions and the second of the two tones that make up the splenic binary at the base of the tonal architecture. It is a way of knowing, not a food preference. Where the first tone recognises what it already has a memory of, the second registers what has not appeared yet. Ra Uru Hu called it "this uncertainty principle," and said of it, "I'm a great fan of the second tone."

Cognition is the layer of Variable read from tone rather than colour. It is not a fifth arrow. It sits underneath the four arrows, and a tone's position on the left or right of the tonal triangle is what makes an arrow point one way or the other. Ra located sensory potential in the tone beneath the Sun and Earth: "When we are looking at sensory potential at deeper levels we are looking at it through the sun/earth configuration." He also used the phrase cognitive root for this layer, which is a better name for what is meant here than the everyday sense of the word taste.

One warning before going further. There is a second thing in Human Design called Taste, and it is not this. That confusion has its own section below.

## Mechanics

Tone is the middle layer of the substructure. A neutrino entering the crystal carries an entry frequency, which is base; the frequency inside the crystal is tone; the exit frequency is colour. There are six tones, six colours, six lines and five bases. Ra Uru Hu was explicit about the direction of causation: "It is the tone after all, that creates in essence what the color is going to be."

The six tones are drawn as a triangle, and Ra noted that the graphic is not an accident, because the triangle mirrors the BodyGraph itself. Colour is drawn as a hexagon and base as a square; only tone gets the triangle. The tones are grouped in binaries. Tones 1 and 2 are the splenic binary at the lower left. Tones 3 and 4 are the ajna binary at the top. Tones 5 and 6 are the solar plexus binary at the lower right.

The triangle is then split down the centre, and the split runs through the ajna binary. Tones 1, 2 and 3 fall on the left; tones 4, 5 and 6 fall on the right. Taste, as tone 2, is always on the left. Crossing that line, Ra said, is the move "from the strategic active, the active body, the strategic left mind to the other side, the passive right body, the receptive right mind." Two thirds of the left side sits in the splenic binary, which is why Ra said the foundation of leftness is splenic alertness.

Cognition Taste has no left and right sub-variant of its own. Nothing in this library names a pair for it in the way Determination gives Open and Closed, or Thirst gives Hot and Cold.

## Not the Same as Determination Taste

Two entirely separate parts of Variable are called Taste, and readers, teachers and chart software all trip over it.

[[Determination: Taste]] is the second value of Determination. It is read from the COLOUR of the Design Sun and Earth, it sits among what Ra called the lower three conditions, and it is a dietary matter. Contemporary teaching splits it into Open Taste, benefiting from variety and experimentation, and Closed Taste, thriving on a consistent and familiar diet. It belongs to the first of the four transformations, the dietary regimen.

Cognition Taste is the second TONE. It is read from a different and deeper layer of the substructure, it has no Open or Closed variant, and it says nothing about what should be on the plate. It describes how a being takes information in.

The two are independent. Colour 2 under the Design Sun does not imply tone 2, and tone 2 does not imply colour 2. A person can carry one, both, or neither. When a chart says only "Taste," the only way to tell which is meant is to check whether the figure quoted is a colour or a tone.

## The Splenic Binary and the Mouth Breathers

Tones 1 and 2 are both splenic, and both, Ra was emphatic, exist to guarantee the survivability of the vehicle. "It is the splenic binary that establishes the ability to be intelligent to survive." Underneath that sits fear, which in this knowledge is not a defect. "Fear is the mother of intelligence."

The two tones divide the job. The first is the ground floor of protection, permanently on alert: "This is the tone of security and that security is based on smell." The second takes the opposite assignment. "It doesn't pay any attention to maintaining the security; it's not interested in that. It's interested in what hasn't happened yet."

Ra's reason for valuing tone 2 is architectural. An immune system built only on memory is excellent at the known threat and helpless before the unknown one, and plagues, he pointed out, have taken humanity apart exactly there. So the architecture includes something else: "the ability to have the sensitivity to be ready to recognize something new the moment it emerges." That is Taste at the tonal level, a readiness held open for the thing that does not yet exist.

In the middle of describing these two tones, Ra dropped one aside about the physical side of the pair: "These are mouth breathers, by the way. Very different way that they take information in." He did not expand on it, and no other passage in this library does either. It is the single remark pointing at intake through the mouth, at a knowing that requires the thing to be brought in and met rather than detected at a distance, but Ra left it as an aside and it should be reported as one.

## What Taste Knows

At the tonal level Taste is discrimination pointed forward. Ra tied the splenic binary to the awakening of awareness "in the language and the taste of instinct and judgment," with smell supplying the language and taste supplying the judgment. The judgment is not a verdict on the familiar. It is the act of registering that something present is new, and that it matters.

Ra followed that up into human experience: the second tone "leads to the kind of mind that we well understand within the human context, the kind of mind that looks beyond because it knows there's more. Yet, it doesn't know what that more is." Certainty belongs to tone 1. Tone 2 works with what has not resolved.

## Taste on the Surface of the BodyGraph

Ra drew a firm line between the tonal architecture and what emerges from it in the [[Splenic Center]], and Taste is his clearest example of the two not matching.

On the surface, taste is keyed into the logical stream of [[Gate 58]], [[Gate 18]], [[Gate 48]] and [[Gate 16]], the [[Collective Circuit]] current that corrects and perfects talent, skill and depth. "What taste really brings is judgmentation," Ra said of the surface expression. "It's all about perfecting patterns."

The inversion is exact. Underneath, Taste is readiness for what has never happened. On the surface, it becomes the establishment of fixed patterns and their correction. Ra granted the logic of it: holding a pattern firmly is what allows anything that does not fit to be noticed, which is "the by-product of what the architecture is all about in the first place." He also named the failure mode, that the same collective stream "can become a deep controlling collective force that locks people into conformity and challenges anyone who will not stay with the pattern."

That gap is the general rule for this whole layer, not a quirk of Taste. "When you look at cognition at the tonal level, it is very profound. And when you look at senses at the BodyGraph level, they're very mundane."

## The Binary Partner

[[Cognition: Smell]] is the other half of the splenic pair, and the two are best read against each other. Smell is memory, alertness and the maintenance of a secure foundation. Taste is openness to the unprecedented. Smell protects what is; Taste is ready for what is not yet. Neither is superior.

They diverge on the surface too. Smell, Ra said, attaches to the tribal stream of the 54, the 32, the 44 and the 26, and in a nine-centred world becomes the smell of money and the stink of failure. Taste attaches to the collective logical stream and becomes pattern and correction.

## Access and Misreading

The most common misreading, after the confusion with Determination, is to treat a tone as a description of who someone already is. Tone is a potential, not a possession. The plans are there, but access to them is not automatic, and a not-self being is cut off from its own cognition. Where the frequency is not correct, what comes through is a harmonic distortion of the colour rather than the true tonal architecture.

The way in is the one Ra always returned to. Cognition is not a shortcut past [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]]; those are the precondition, and Variable follows the catalyst rather than replacing it. Nor does cognition arrive ahead of the chain, since the dietary regimen and the correct environment come first.

The second misreading is to expect a behaviour. Taste as a cognition is not a personality trait, a preference or an aesthetic. It is the shape of a sensory potential, and the senses built on top of it are, in Ra's framing, tools used by a form that is "nothing but a sensory system."
