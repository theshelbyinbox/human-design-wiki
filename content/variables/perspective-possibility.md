# Perspective: Possibility

Possibility is the second of the six Perspectives, the six ways of seeing carried in the colour of the Personality Nodes. A being with this Perspective is built to notice what could be there rather than what is there or what is likely to arrive. Ra Uru Hu put the mechanic plainly in his Ra.TV lecture on distraction: "Think about somebody who has the two. Their view is possibility. This is the correct perspective for them."

What makes this particular view worth close attention is that Ra used it, more than any other, as his working example. When he wanted to show what a Perspective actually is, and what happens to a Perspective when a life is lived incorrectly, he reached for Possibility and its harmonic partner Probability. "Two of the very interesting views of the six views are a harmonic possibility and probability," he said. That pairing is the clearest illustration of distraction anywhere in the recorded material.

Possibility is not optimism, and it is not prediction. It is a frame. What arrives through it is the fact that something is open, unfinished, not yet decided. Ra was careful about this: "It's not about looking to see whether the possibilities are going to become something or where the possibilities are going to flourish. It is simply about seeing possibilities."

## Mechanics

Perspective is derived from the colour of the Personality Nodes. Beneath every gate sits a line, beneath the line a colour, and beneath the colour a tone. The colour at the Personality Nodes gives the Perspective; Possibility is colour 2 of the six, which run in the order Survival, Possibility, Power, Wanting, Probability, Personal.

Perspective is the bottom right arrow of the four, on the mind side of Variable rather than the body side. The arrow's direction, focused when it points left and peripheral when it points right, is not set by the colour at all. It is set by the tone underneath. Ra: "It is tone that establishes whether something is to the left or something is to the right." So Possibility occurs in both orientations, focused and peripheral, and the colour name says nothing about which one a given chart carries. The corpus gives no left and right variant names for the individual Perspectives the way it does for some of the Determinations and Environments, and none should be assumed.

Colour 2 also carries a general character that Ra described when he laid out the first binary of colour: "The first color is exoteric and the second color is esoteric." Colour 1 deals in what can be shown and counted; colour 2 deals in what cannot yet be. Applied to the Personality Nodes, that is the difference between a view organised around what can be relied on and a view organised around what has not happened yet.

## Seeing What Could Be

The practical content of this Perspective is narrow and specific. Things arrive in the field already carrying their openness. Where another view registers a situation as settled, this one registers the places where it is not settled. Ra described what is fed to the mind as a result as "just the interest of the mutated potentials," which is a precise phrase: potential that has not resolved, and that may never resolve, is the material this view supplies.

There is no bet inside it. Ra made the point twice over in the same passage, first as description and then as the thing that makes the distraction so tempting: "There you are, too, and your view is to see the possibilities. In that, there is nothing to bet on." A correct Possibility view does not tell its owner which of the open things will happen. It is not supposed to. Its job is to deliver the fact of openness to the conceptualising mind, which is the fourth and final transformation, so that the mind has correct material to work with.

Contemporary teaching describes this Perspective in warmer and more social terms, as the visionary or dreamer who naturally sees the world's unrealised opportunities, and as the philosophical member of a group who asks whether a thing might be done another way. Those descriptions are consistent with the mechanic, though they add a flavour of aspiration that Ra's own account does not contain. The distinction matters, because aspiration is close to expectation, and expectation is exactly where the distraction begins.

## The Distraction to Probability

On the Sun and Earth positions, the harmonic slippage that happens under conditioning is called transference. On the Nodes it has its own name. Ra: "It's color and it transfers at the note level we call a distraction." The harmonic runs 1 to 4, 2 to 5, 3 to 6, and back, so the distraction of Possibility is Probability, and the distraction of Probability is Possibility.

This is the pair Ra returned to. "Some people have a way of looking at the world where they see the possibilities. Some people have a way of looking at the world where they see the probabilities. Unfortunately, they're always distracted by the other because they're not self. And the person that's supposed to see possibilities thinks that they see probabilities."

Notice the shape of the error. It is not that the Possibility being stops seeing. It is that they keep seeing exactly what they always saw, open and unresolved potential, and then misread it as something to count on. The openness gets converted into odds. "When you think you see probability, when in fact you're only here to see possibility, and because you think you see probability here and there and over there that you do dumb things."

The damage does not stop at the view. Perspective is the layer that determines what the mind is given to think about, and a distracted view hands the mind the wrong material. Ra followed the chain all the way down: "The mind is building concepts on a probability that, in fact, is not a probability at all." He then added the second failure that usually accompanies it, since a being who is not operating correctly is unlikely to have a correct Motivation either. "So, you've got a mind without the correct motivation thinking about something that it's not supposed to be looking at."

Distraction is not a defect belonging to one Perspective. Every Perspective has one, and Ra described his own without flattery: "I'm a six. My view, my perspective is personal, but the distraction is a view to power. I was not self a long time. My view was totally distracted. I did not see clearly. I did not see at all."

## What Makes the View Distract

Distraction is a symptom, not a cause. It is what a correct Perspective does when the conditions underneath it are not met. Ra's phrase for the trigger is direct: "If you're not correct and you're not operating according to your strategy and authority and you're making decisions with your mind instead of having the perspective you're supposed to have, you get the harmonic. You get distracted. You get distracted by what you're not."

The other condition is place. Perspective is the third of the four transformations, and it depends on the two before it. The dietary regimen comes first, then the environment, and only then the view. Ra was unsparing about the order: "If the mind does not have access to correct perspective, then the mind can never, ever, ever, ever, ever be correct. And you cannot have the correct perspective if you're not in the right place." Elsewhere he said the same thing from the other side: "You cannot take advantage of the unique perspective if you're not in the right place looking at the right things."

This is why Possibility cannot be practised as a technique. Nobody arrives at a correct view by resolving to see possibilities rather than probabilities. The correction runs through [[Strategy]] and authority first, then through [[Environment]], and the view settles as a consequence. Ra also tied this stretch of Variable to the Saturn return and the decades that follow it, describing the period between thirty and fifty as when environment and its partner perspective are established.

## Resonance, Harmony and Common Misreadings

Colour and line share the same six-part structure, and they relate to each other by number. Colour 2 resonates with [[line 2]], the Hermit, and harmonises with [[line 5]], the Heretic, in the same way that the second and fifth lines of a hexagram occupy matching positions in the lower and upper trigrams. Ra described this directly at the colour level: "There is a harmonic relationship that exists between the first and the fourth color, the second and the fifth color, and the third and the sixth." That harmonic is the same one the distraction travels along, which is why the pull is toward five and not toward some random other view.

Two misreadings are worth naming. The first is treating Possibility as a talent for spotting opportunities that will pay off. It is not a forecasting instrument, and the moment it is used as one it has already become the distraction. The second is confusing it with the second colour of Motivation, which contemporary teaching calls Hope. The themes rhyme, and contemporary sources note the likeness, but Perspective and Motivation are separate arrows derived from different chart positions, and a being's Perspective and Motivation frequently differ.
