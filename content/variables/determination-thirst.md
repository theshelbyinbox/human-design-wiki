# Determination: Thirst

Thirst is the third colour of Determination, and its name is the most misleading label in the Variable vocabulary. It is not about drinking, and it is not about how much water a body wants. It is about temperature. Ra Uru Hu was direct about this: "the third color is really about hot and cold. It's about temperature." Contemporary reference material in the source library says the same thing in plainer words, stating outright that Thirst is a misleading name and that the colour actually refers to the temperature of the food taken in at meals.

Ra placed Thirst at an evolutionary hinge. Where the first colour eats one thing at a time and the second repeats a fixed menu, the third is the point at which food starts being worked on before it is eaten. "It's about the manipulation of food," he said. "It is in the evolutionary sense when the beginnings of what we would call cooking, what we would call cuisine." Heat applied to food is the innovation the third colour is built around, and the condition it carries is whether that heat should be present when the food goes in.

Like the other Determination colours, Thirst describes how a body takes nutrition in, not what it takes in. Ra's summary of the subject applies exactly: "It's not about how well you eat. Remember that. It's how correctly you eat."

## Mechanics

Determination is read from the colour of the Design Sun and Earth, and it sits under the top left arrow of the four Variable arrows, the body side of the design. Thirst is colour 3 of the six, after [[Determination: Appetite|Appetite]] and [[Determination: Taste|Taste]] and before [[Determination: Touch|Touch]], [[Determination: Sound|Sound]] and [[Determination: Light|Light]].

The two variants are Hot and Cold. Which one applies is settled by the tone sitting underneath the colour, the same layer that decides which way the arrow points. Hot Thirst is the left side, Cold Thirst the right. Contemporary reference material describes the left tone as digesting best on food above body temperature and the right tone as digesting best on food below it.

Colours resonate with profile lines by number, so Thirst resonates with [[line 3]], and colour 3 harmonises with line 6. That harmonic is the route the colour travels when things go wrong, covered below.

## The Last of the Conditions

Ra split the six Determination colours in half and gave each half a keynote. "The bottom three have a super key note. And that super key note that they have is called conditions. So each of the lower colors, the one, two, and the three, they all bring certain eating conditions. The upper color brings circumstances." Thirst is the last of the three conditions, and Ra introduced it as "the final condition."

The distinction matters. The conditions are properties of the food itself, and Ra called them rigid. The circumstances of colours 4 through 6 are properties of the setting around the meal, and he described that half as "a much more sophisticated, if you will, digestive system," adding at once: "Not better. There's no better in any of this."

So Thirst is a condition, not a preference. Contemporary teaching makes the point explicitly by saying the temperature requirement holds regardless of the ambient temperature or how the person happens to feel. A Cold Thirst body does not get a pass on a freezing day, and a Hot Thirst body does not stop needing warmth in a heatwave.

## Hot and Cold

Ra described the two sides in terms of the heat the digestive system does or does not generate on its own.

Hot Thirst takes in food above body temperature. "The reason for that," Ra said, "is that they've got a digestive system that doesn't generate enough heat. You know, it doesn't generate enough chemical heat in order to be able to break down the food in the way that's necessary. So, these are beings that consistently need to have warm food that acts as an a to help the digestive system." The heat in the food does part of the work the body cannot do unaided.

Cold Thirst takes in food below body temperature. "These are beings that have an overheated digestive system. If you keep on pumping warm food into them, they burn off what is of value and they don't get the nutrition that is correct for them. They just don't." The failure here is not indigestion but loss. The nutrition passes through without being extracted.

Ra drew the boundary at the body's own temperature rather than at any fixed number of degrees. Anything above it is hot, anything below it is cold, and a Cold Thirst person, in his words, "can't have anything above what is your body temperature."
## Conditioning

The clearest example Ra gave of conditioning in the whole Determination lecture is a Thirst example, and it starts at birth. "One out of every 12 children comes into the world where the food that they need to take into their system needs to be below body temperature. And then they come into the world and there they are. Beautiful picture. Little baby breastfeeding. And you think all of that's so beautiful. Mother and child, you know, love, you know, sharing from her body, providing this nice warm milk. Yeah. But the child's being conditioned, deeply conditioned."

The point is not that breastfeeding is wrong. It is that a Cold Thirst infant has no route to correct nutrition at all, because the only food available arrives warm. Ra tied this to neural development, noting that brain expansion in most people shuts down by about age three and that the system predicts a potential of roughly seven years of it when nutrition is correct. Incorrect nutrition, on his account, homogenises that process where it matters most.

Culture keeps it going after infancy. Ra called food "a way in which we are socially conditioned by others" and noted how easily someone whose regimen runs against everyone else's is pulled back into the common way of eating.

## Transference to Light

Transference is a property of colour itself, not of Motivation alone. Ra described it as the magnetic monopole failing to capture the emitted colour frequency cleanly, so that the being operates from the harmonic rather than the real thing: "instead of getting a six, it pulls in a three. And that three is not substantiated by the tonal values underneath." He applied the term directly to this arrow, speaking of "determination transference" as a predisposition toward homogenisation rather than differentiation.

The harmonic runs in fixed pairs. "There is a harmonic relationship that exists between the first and the fourth color, the second and the fifth color, and the third and the sixth." Thirst therefore slips toward [[Determination: Light]], the sixth colour, whose two sides are daytime eating and nocturnal eating. The corpus does not describe what a Thirst person in transference to Light looks like in practice, so nothing further should be read into the pairing than the harmonic itself.

## How Thirst Is Commonly Misread

Three misreadings recur. The first is the one the name causes, treating Thirst as a statement about hydration or drinking habits. The second is reading the temperature requirement as a preference that can be negotiated against weather, mood or company, when Ra treated the conditions as rigid. The third is taking Hot as better than Cold, or the conditions as lesser than the circumstances, which Ra ruled out for the whole category.

Thirst is also not usable on its own. Ra placed the dietary regimen downstream of Strategy and Authority, which catalyse the process, and upstream of environment, perspective and awareness in [[The Four Transformations]].
