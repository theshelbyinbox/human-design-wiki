# Motivation: Guilt

Guilt is the fifth of the six colours of [[Motivation]], the underlying frequency that runs beneath the Personality Sun and Earth and colours everything the mind does with the role it has been given. Ra Uru Hu introduced it without enthusiasm: "So, we come to the fifth color and we come to an uncomfortable heading, having a fifth line personality myself. We come to the theme of guilt."

He then gave the fifth colour its binary in a single sentence: "The motivation in the fifth color is either to accept conditioning or to be the conditioner." That sentence is the whole of Ra's recorded teaching on this colour in the present library, and everything else written here is either structural, drawn from what he says about colour in general, or flagged as later teaching.

The word guilt is misleading in the ordinary way these substructure names tend to be. It does not describe an emotion the person is aware of feeling, and it is not a moral verdict. Colour sits far below the level at which we observe ourselves. Ra was blunt about the reach of it: "There is an underlying motivation we have no access to affecting how we end up living things out on the surface."

## Mechanics

Guilt is colour 5 of the Personality Sun and Earth. That colour is read as the top right arrow of the variable, the arrow on the mind side, alongside [[Perspective]] below it. The six colours in order are Fear, Hope, Desire, Need, Guilt and Innocence, so Guilt occupies the fifth slot, paired with Innocence at the top of the sequence.

The arrow's direction is not decided by the colour. It is decided by the tone underneath it, which is the layer of [[Cognition]]. Ra: "It is tone that establishes whether something is to the left or something is to the right." A left pointing Motivation arrow is described as strategic and a right pointing one as receptive, which is a statement about mind style rather than about the content of the motivation itself. Left Guilt and right Guilt are the same colour, read through a different mental orientation. See [[The Four Arrows]] for how the four read together.

The corpus gives no pair of variant names for the fifth colour of the kind Determination gives for Thirst or Sound. The only binary Ra attaches to Guilt is conditioner and accepts conditioning.

## Guilt as a Colouring, Not a Feeling

Ra approached colour through the ordinary sense of the word motive. The motive for everything a person does sits in the colour, and it filters upward without announcing itself. "It is the underlying motivation that's driving us in different ways and our surface doesn't know why." He liked the theatrical version of the question: an actor holding a script and asking the director what the motivation is, because the same line delivered from a different motive is a different performance.

So Guilt as a colour is the quality of the pressure, not the content of the thought. Contemporary teaching tends to translate it as taking responsibility for what is not working and moving to fix it, and notes that although the mechanism does often surface as something that feels like guilt, the feeling is the symptom rather than the driver. That gloss is not Ra's and does not appear in his recorded material here.

## Conditioner and Accepts Conditioning

The binary is unusual among the six motivations because it is stated in the vocabulary Human Design normally reserves for the not-self. Every other colour's binary names a stance toward the world. This one names a position inside the transaction of [[Conditioning]] itself: the being either takes the conditioning that is coming at them, or is the one supplying it to others.

Ra did not expand on either side. He also did not say that one side is correct and the other is not. Where he treats a colour binary at length, with Fear and its communalist and separatist poles, he explicitly rejects reading the two as fixed opposites: "I don't want you to look at this duality as polarities. I want you to look at it as a prana." He describes that movement as organic, running back and forth outside anybody's control. He says this about the first colour, not the fifth, so it is offered here as the pattern he sets for colour binaries generally rather than as something he stated about Guilt.

## A Sentence That Is Easily Misread

Immediately before giving the binary, Ra says: "Here in our binary we have guilt and innocence." Read quickly, that looks as though the two poles of the fifth colour are guilt and innocence.

They are not. Innocence is colour 6 and has its own article at [[Motivation: Innocence]]. Ra is naming the pair of colours, not the poles inside one of them. He describes the six as grouped, noting that "the six tones, like the six colors, are broken up into binaries", and Guilt and Innocence are the last of those three pairs. The two poles inside the fifth colour are the ones the very next sentence supplies: to accept conditioning, or to be the conditioner.

## Transference to Hope

Motivation is subject to transference, which Ra describes as a slippage along the harmonic: "Transference is a movement in a harmonic ... In a sense, one's motivation is always being pulled away ... by conditioning." The harmonic pairs are 1 with 4, 2 with 5 and 3 with 6, so the fifth colour's transference partner is the second, and the second colour's is the fifth. He calls transference "the byproduct of incorrectness".

The name for colour 2 needs a note. Ra calls the second colour esoteric, as against the first colour's exoteric. The name Hope for colour 2 comes from later teaching, and contemporary sources list the transferred state of Guilt as Hope and the transferred state of Hope as Guilt. See [[Motivation: Hope]].

There is a loop worth noticing in the fifth colour specifically. Conditioning is both the named theme of the binary and the named cause of transference. A being with this colour is pulled off their own motivation by the very thing their motivation is organised around.

## Resonance with Line 5, Harmony with Line 2

Colour has a fixed relationship to profile line. Ra: "if you have a first line and you have a first color, they're in resonance", and "If you have a first color and you have a fourth line, that's a harmony." Anything else is dissonance, and of dissonance he says only that "It is a process that in fact is much more uncomfortable."

Applied to the fifth colour: Guilt resonates with a fifth line and harmonises with a second line. The heretic and the hermit are the two lines of projection, and the fifth line in particular carries a projection built not on what is actually recognised in the person but on what is hoped for or assumed of them. A being with a fifth line and the Guilt colour is being driven from underneath by the same theme their surface role already carries. See [[Profile 5/1]] and [[Profile 2/5]].

Colour and profile are calculated separately and do not have to agree. Ra used himself as the example of the gap, noting a 5/1 profile carried over a fourth colour of his own.

## The Prerequisite

None of this is usable in front of the basics. Ra's order is catalyst first and variable second, and the sequence of transformations runs from diet to environment to perspective to conceptualising, so the mind's layer is the last to come into focus, not the first. [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]] are the entry point, and [[Deconditioning]] at the cellular level is described as a seven year process. Reading a Guilt motivation off a chart tells a person what is running underneath. It does not substitute for the decision making that would let it run correctly.
