# Perspective: Personal

Personal is the sixth of the six Perspectives, the values carried by the colour of the Personality Nodes. A Perspective is not an opinion or a philosophy. It is the frame the eyes are built into, the fixed way a particular mind is designed to take in what the environment puts in front of it. Ra Uru Hu described the colour of the Personality Nodes as bringing "perspective, view, the way of seeing that is absolutely correct for you and it is your color."

Where the first Perspective looks for patterns that can be counted on and the fifth for what is workable, the sixth relates what it sees back to itself. Contemporary teaching describes the Personal view as one in which the world is read as a reflection of one's own inner state, so what is perceived outside is taken as a mirror of what is happening inside. In Ra's recorded material the colour is named rather than elaborated, and the clearest statement of it in this library is his description of his own chart.

Personal is also the value Ra used to explain the mechanic of distraction, because he carried it himself. That makes this the one Perspective with a first-person account from the source of the system, and it fixes the pairing between the sixth view and the third.

## Mechanics

Perspective is derived from the COLOUR of the Personality Nodes, the bottom right arrow of the four. Colour sits one layer beneath the line of an activation, and there are six colours to any line. Personal is colour 6.

The six Perspectives in order are Survival, Possibility, Power, Wanting, Probability and Personal. The corpus gives no left or right variant names for the individual Perspective values, in the way Determination gives Light Direct and Light Indirect. Direction comes from the TONE beneath the colour, tones 1 to 3 pointing the arrow left and tones 4 to 6 right. The arrow's two orientations are Focused on the left and Peripheral on the right, and a Personal Perspective may carry either.

Perspective belongs to the mind side of Variable and is the third of the four transformations, not the first. It depends on the two body-side arrows that come before it. Ra was explicit that the Personality Nodes carry "the possibility that arises out of being in the correct environment," and that "if you're in the correct environment, then you're going to see what you're here to see." A Personal view in the wrong place is not a Personal view operating badly. It is a Personal view that is not being received at all.

## What the Personal View Sees

The consistent description across the corpus is that this frame takes the self as the reference point for everything it looks at. Contemporary teaching summarises it as viewing the world as a reflection of one's inner state, with everything perceived read as a mirror of one's own journey. In that reading, what is true is what has been personally lived, and material that has not passed through direct experience does not register with the same weight.

This is presented as a way of seeing rather than as an adopted practice. Contemporary teaching draws the distinction directly: a person may cultivate the habit of treating the world as a mirror, but for the sixth colour this is simply how the perceiving happens. The same source separates a low expression, showing up as self-consciousness and self-criticism because everything gets related back to the self, from a high one that uses the same mechanism without judgement, as curiosity rather than verdict.

The limits of the evidence matter here. The distinction between the Personal Perspective and ordinary self-absorption is drawn in contemporary teaching and not in Ra's recorded material in this library. Ra names the colour and gives his own case. He does not define what correct Personal seeing looks like as against a self-involved one. The nearest thing he offers is negative: what his own view looked like when it was not his.

## Ra Uru Hu on His Own Perspective

In a recorded lecture on distraction, Ra Uru Hu said: "I'm a six. My view, my perspective is personal, but the distraction is a view to power. I was not self a long time. My view was totally distracted. I did not see clearly. I did not see at all."

Two things follow. The first is the pairing: the sixth Perspective's distraction is [[Perspective: Power]], the third, and this is the only Perspective pair besides Possibility and Probability that Ra names outright. The second is the severity. He does not say his distracted view was a weaker version of his real one. He says he did not see at all.

The phrase "I was not self" is the technical one. Distraction is what the view does while a person lives as the [[Not-Self]], deciding with the mind rather than through [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]]. It is not a flaw inside the Perspective but what happens to it when the frequency underneath is wrong.

## The Distraction to Power

Distraction is the Perspective-side name for the slippage Ra called transference everywhere else in colour. Its shape is harmonic: the first colour slips to the fourth, the second to the fifth, the third to the sixth, and back. Ra: "You have a natural perspective, a natural correct view that gets distracted."

For colour 6 that means the pull is toward Power, the view that reads hierarchies, players and the way the game is run. A person built to see everything as a reflection of their own condition instead finds themselves reading the room for who has leverage. The corpus gives no worked example of this pair beyond Ra's own sentence, so the mechanism is best understood through the one he does work through at length, the two and the five, where someone designed to see possibilities becomes convinced they are seeing probabilities and acts on bets that were never there.

The consequence runs downstream. Ra was insistent that the view sets what the mind is given to work with, so distraction does not stop at the eyes. It hands the conceptualising mind at the Personality Sun and Earth the wrong raw material, which that mind then processes with a [[Motivation]] that is itself likely to be in transference.

## Colour Six at the Top of a Substructure Sequence

Colour, tone and line all run in sixes, and Ra taught all three the same way, as a lower three and an upper three built like the trigrams of a hexagram. The harmonics follow from that structure: one with four, two with five, three with six. Colour 6 sits at the top of the upper trigram, last in its sequence.

Ra's illustration of capture failure uses the sixth colour specifically. Speaking of the monopole's failure to capture the emitted frequency of someone not living correctly, he said: "let's say you're the sixth color. The monopole will capture the third color." He added that it "just can't carry the six," and that in the resulting distortion a person loses access to the cognitive potential underneath.

The corpus also shows what the sixth colour tends to look like at the top of a sequence, though the worked example is from [[Motivation]] rather than Perspective. Ra contrasted the third colour Motivation, desire, with the sixth: "On the sixth, you have something that's totally different. You have something that is innocent. It is totally without motivation." He called it "anti motivation," and noted the cruelty of the transference, that the people most visibly chasing leadership are often sixth colours who should have no motivation at all. The sixth colour is not simply more of what the third one is. In Motivation it is the negation of the sequence's driving theme, and the distraction to the third makes a person look like the opposite of what they are. The corpus does not extend that reading to Perspective, so hold it as a parallel rather than a rule.

On the line side, colour 6 resonates with line 6 by number and harmonises with line 3. The library's profile material describes the sixth line as sitting on the roof of the house, separate from the rest of it but with a view into the next hexagram, and as interested less in what goes on inside the house than in the broader view. The word view sits in both descriptions, which is worth noticing without pressing it further than the corpus does.

## Common Misreadings

The name is the main trap. "Personal" here is a colour name at the substructure level and does not carry the meaning the same word has in profile material, where lines 1 to 3 are described as personally focused and lines 4 to 6 as transpersonal. By that convention line 6 is transpersonal, while colour 6 is called Personal. The two uses belong to different layers and should not be read against each other.

A second misreading is treating Perspective as something to work on directly. It is the third transformation in the chain and cannot be reached ahead of the first two. Without correct [[Determination]] and correct [[Environment]], the sixth colour is not available to be lived, however accurately a chart names it.

A third is assuming the arrow's left or right orientation grades the value. Focused and Peripheral come from tone, not colour, and the sources treat left and right as orientations rather than as grades.
