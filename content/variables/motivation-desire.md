# Motivation: Desire

Desire is the third of the six Motivations, the colour that sits beneath the Personality Sun and Earth and tints everything the mind does with a pull toward the new. Ra Uru Hu introduced it in exactly those terms: "So, when we begin here with desire, we begin with the third color." It is not an appetite for objects and it is not something a person can feel directly. It is a filter laid over mental life, operating below the level at which anyone has access to it.

Ra was blunt about the inaccessibility: "There is an underlying motivation we have no access to affecting how we end up living things out on the surface." A person with Desire does not experience themselves as desiring. They experience themselves as thinking, deciding and choosing, while the shape of all of it is set one layer down. Ra described the purpose of teaching colour as an explanation of that helplessness, not a way past it.

What Desire adds to the mind is a bias toward movement and toward the unattempted. Contemporary teaching summarises the third Motivation as being moved by change, achievement and progress, and Ra's own framing is sharper still: "the unexpected is part of the goal of desire: to get to the place where nobody has been before." The third Motivation is not satisfied by a good outcome that anyone could have predicted.

## Mechanics

Motivation is the top right arrow of the [[The Four Arrows|four arrows]], on the mind side of the Variable. It is derived from the COLOUR of the Personality Sun and Earth, the layer of substructure directly beneath the line. Desire is colour 3 in that sequence, after [[Motivation: Fear|Fear]] and [[Motivation: Hope|Hope]] and before [[Motivation: Need|Need]], [[Motivation: Guilt|Guilt]] and [[Motivation: Innocence|Innocence]].

The Personality Sun and Earth are not an arbitrary place for Motivation to live. They are the basis of the incarnation cross, and in Ra's description "the sun earth represents our role ... it is your son earth that conceptualizes all of that and delivers the performance of that role." Motivation is therefore the colouring on the delivery of a role, not a separate drive bolted on beside it.

Two things about the arrow are commonly confused. Its left and right orientation, strategic when it points left and receptive when it points right, is decided by TONE and not by which of the six colours a person carries, so Desire can sit under either. Ra's rule is that "It is tone that establishes whether something is to the left or something is to the right." The colour names the Motivation; the tone underneath points the arrow and produces [[Cognition]].

Second, each colour carries a binary of its own, separate from that arrow direction. Ra noted that "the six tones, like the six colors, are broken up into binaries." For Desire that binary is follower and leader.

## The Follower and Leader Binary

Ra gave Desire a genuine two-sided mechanic rather than a single flavour. "The third color motivation is the motivation of desire," he said, and that desire "has a trajectory of you know follower to leader." He then put the sharper edge on it: "The third color full of desire is a motivation. The desire to lead."

The two poles are not two types of person. They are a flow, in the same way the communalist and separatist poles of the first colour are a flow rather than a fixed position. Ra described the whole leading and following mechanism as something built into the species and cycling without end: "it is built into us to follow and it is built into us to lead followers, and those who follow lead, and those who lead follow, and it never stops." He was candid that the following side is not a defect. Writing about his own third line mind he said, "Being a sheep is natural," and added that without it "there would be no potential for us to operate in unison, to work together, to group together."

Following is the earlier part of the trajectory, in which the mind attaches itself to an alpha, a project or an established direction. Leading is the later part, in which the same drive turns outward and takes the front position. Neither end is the correct one and neither is a graduation. Ra's own phrasing, "Once I've gone through my sheep business, then I can be an alpha and I can have my own sheep," describes a hierarchy that simply keeps turning over.

## A Mutative Motivation

The quality that most distinguishes Desire from the other five Motivations is that it is mutative. Ra tied this directly to its resonance with the third line: "Recognize that in its direct resonance to the third line, we're dealing with something that's fundamentally mutative. Because this is a mutative force, what can come out of that kind of motivation is truly the unexpected."

Mutative here means discontinuous, not derivable from what came before. A mind coloured by Desire is not motivated toward the best available version of a known thing. Its goal, in Ra's words, is "to get to the place where nobody has been before." The example he reached for was Heinrich Schliemann, who was not a trained archaeologist but believed he knew where Troy was and turned out to be right.

A mutative force guarantees nothing. The unexpected includes failure as readily as discovery, and nothing in the source material promises the person with Desire that the new place will be worth reaching.

## Resonance with the Third Line, Harmony with the Sixth

Colour relates to line in a fixed pattern that Ra laid out plainly: "if you have a first line and you have a first color, they're in resonance," and "If you have a first color and you have a fourth line, that's a harmony." Where the numbers neither match nor pair, the relationship is dissonance, and Ra described that as "a process that in fact is much more uncomfortable."

For Desire this means resonance with a third line anywhere in the profile and harmony with a sixth line. A profile carrying both, such as [[Profile 3/6]] or [[Profile 6/3]], has surface roles sitting in direct relationship with the third colour underneath. That does not make such a chart better. It means surface and substructure pull in a recognisably similar direction rather than against each other.

Ra placed this pairing inside the larger mutative story he told about the species: "we're going to have a 6th line mutation, which is ultimately impacting on the 3rd line, its harmony, in which this whole theme of leading and following has to change."

## Transference to Innocence

Motivation is subject to transference, a harmonic slippage under [[Conditioning|conditioning]] in which the colour a person actually carries is replaced in operation by its harmonic partner. Desire and [[Motivation: Innocence|Innocence]] are the pair on the 3 to 6 harmonic, and the slippage runs in both directions.

Ra described the mechanism at the level of capture: "let's say you're the sixth color. The monopole will capture the third color. It will, you know, it's not capturing the third. It just can't carry the six. And so there's distortion." That distortion is the source of one of his most pointed observations about the not-self world. Innocence, the sixth colour, is properly without motivation at all. Yet the people most visibly driven to lead, he said, are frequently sixth colour beings running on borrowed Desire, "trying to tell you that they can lead you" without correct access to it. He noted that all transitional profiles carry a sixth colour personality, which makes this a very large slice of humanity, and that "rather than a world that has as its bridge, innocence as its motivation, desire is usually what takes place."

Running the other way, a person whose true Motivation is Desire falls into Innocence when they are not correct. Ra gave his own case: "I'm a third color personality sun earth and I can remember what it was like for me to be in transference you know I didn't want to take responsibility for anything I didn't want to lead anything I didn't desire to do any of that it was a very easy way out." Contemporary teaching describes the same signpost in gentler terms, noting that hanging back and not caring what is in it for you is the sign of transference for a Desire Motivation.

Transference is a signal rather than a fault to correct directly. It reports that the underlying process is not being lived correctly, and the remedy lies upstream of the mind entirely.

## Desire Here Is Not Gate 30

The word desire is used in more than one place in Human Design and the overlap misleads. [[Gate 30]], the Clinging Fire, is conventionally called the gate of desire and is associated with the abstract collective emotional wave. The Motivation described here is a colour of the substructure beneath the Personality Sun and Earth, with no mechanical relationship to that gate. A chart can carry one without the other.

## Strategy and Authority Come First

Nothing in Variable is available to someone who is not running their own decision making. Ra was categorical: "Without strategy and authority, there is no way for any being to be able to operate correctly." Motivation sits at the far end of the sequence described in [[The Four Transformations]], after [[Determination|dietary regimen]] and [[Environment|environment]] and alongside the mental work of [[Perspective|perspective]], and Ra held that a mind without correct perspective "can never, ever, ever, ever, ever be correct."

Knowing that a chart carries Desire changes nothing on its own. The colour is always perfect, in Ra's phrasing, but the capture of it rarely is, and what makes the capture correct is living by [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]] long enough for the body to decondition. Desire read as a personality label, a permission slip to chase things, or an instruction to seek leadership is Desire misread.
