# Cognition: Feeling

Feeling is the fifth of the six tones, and with it the cognitive architecture crosses into territory that Ra Uru Hu treated as the leading edge of human sensory development. It is not an emotion and it is not a mood. It is a way of taking information in. Ra put the capacity plainly in his Rave Anatomy lecture on the senses: "When I talk about cognitive architecture in regards to the 5th and 6th tones, when I'm talking about the 5th tone I am talking about a capacity to take in complex information from auras, from the electromagnetic field, from chemistry."

That sentence is the spine of it. Feeling reads the ambient field: not the surface of a thing but the atmosphere around it, the charge in a room, the chemistry of a body, the shape of an aura. Where the splenic tones work by alertness and the ajna tones by sight, the fifth tone works by attunement to what is already in the air, and takes it in as data rather than as sentiment.

Feeling sits with [[Cognition: Touch|Touch]] in the solar plexus binary, the last pair in the tonal triangle and, in Ra's account of evolution, the pair whose potential has not yet arrived. Understanding it means holding both at once: what the architecture describes, and how little of it shows on the surface of the [[Solar Plexus Center]] as it is lived now.

## Mechanics

Tone is a layer of the substructure. Beneath every activation there is a gate, then a line, then color, then tone, then base. Ra described tone as the frequency of the neutrino inside the crystal, with base the entry frequency and color the exit frequency. There are six tones, and Ra was emphatic about what they carry: "the tone represents our cognitive architecture." He was equally emphatic that it is only a map of what is available. "Your tone represents what is your cognitive potential. Now, remember, that is only a potential."

The six tones are laid out as a triangle divided down the middle. Tones 1, 2 and 3 sit on the left; tones 4, 5 and 6 on the right. Feeling is therefore a right-side tone, and that placement carries the orientation Ra gave to rightness throughout Variable: "the difference between being strategic, which is left, and being receptive, which is right, being active which is left, being passive, which is right, being focused, which is left, being peripheral, which is right." A fifth tone is receptive in orientation. It does not go out after information. It lets information arrive.

This matters beyond the cognition itself, because tone is what points the arrows. Ra: "It is tone that establishes whether something is to the left or something is to the right." Cognition is not a fifth arrow on the Variable grid. It is the layer underneath the four arrows, and a fifth tone in any of the four Sun, Earth or nodal positions makes that arrow point right. See [[The Four Arrows]].

Ra located the deep sensory reading in the Sun and Earth configuration: when "we are looking at sensory potential at deeper levels we are looking at it through the sun/earth configuration," which he called "a 70% key to the programming." Unlike the six values of [[Determination]], the cognitions carry no left and right variant names in this corpus. There is no Feeling Active or Feeling Passive here, and none should be assumed.

## The Solar Plexus Binary

The tonal triangle mirrors the BodyGraph. The splenic binary is the lower left, tones 1 and 2. The ajna binary is at the top, tones 3 and 4. The solar plexus binary is the lower right, tones 5 and 6. Ra described the construct as indicating both the cognitive potential of a being and how that cognition is wired through the three awareness centers, [[Splenic Center|Spleen]], [[Ajna Center|Ajna]] and Solar Plexus.

Those three centers are also a sequence in time. "The evolutionary pattern is from Spleen to Ajna and from Ajna to Solar Plexus, this is the progression of evolutionary awareness." Ra placed the last fully right beings, the Neanderthals, as purely splenic, and the last fully left beings, the seven-centered form, as entirely ajna. The nine-centered being that arrived in 1781 is transitional and carries both directions. What is coming is right, and solar plexus.

This is why the fifth and sixth tones read as forward-looking in Ra's material. He described the right side as bringing "sensory attunement to frequencies in the environment that the left has never known," naming the field of the aura, the electromagnetic field and the tactile field. He was also careful about how rare a working expression of it currently is, describing the phenomena that display these qualities as usually mutative or the product of injuries. Feeling is a description of an architecture, not a talent already running.

Ra set the whole tonal system inside one frame: "everything that we call cognition is our ability to interpret frequency." The six tones are the tools for interpreting it. Feeling is the tool that reads frequency in the field.

## Feeling Is Not the Emotional Wave

This is where the fifth tone is most often misread, and Ra addressed it directly. Setting the BodyGraph Solar Plexus beside the tonal solar plexus binary, he said it is very different "even though the language appears to be the same." The words feeling and touch appear in both places. They do not mean the same thing in both.

The BodyGraph Solar Plexus is a motor. It produces a wave. Ra called the gap between the two "the greatest divide" in the whole sensory system: "For me this difference between the BodyGraph Solar Plexus sensory potential and the tonal, this is the greatest divide. You can see the connection from the architecture to the Spleen and the Ajna without much difficulty. But here there is this huge divide, because it's the motor that's standing in the way."

What the motor does is make the material crude. Ra described the emotional senses at the BodyGraph level as operating, for most of humanity, in a way that is "very crude and superficial," and named the distortion: "because we are still Ajna dominated what we have done historically as not-self is that we have created interpretations for our emotions which are mental." While the motor is in the way, the only handling the knowledge offers is patience and the wisdom of processing the wave over time.

On the question a reader will reasonably ask, whether a fifth tone means emotional authority, or whether emotional authority produces Feeling cognition, this corpus says nothing. It nowhere states that either determines the other. What it does state is that the two share a name, occupy the same evolutionary territory, and are separated by the motor. Ra's discussion of [[Emotional Authority]] in the same lecture is about the wave and about patience, and he keeps it distinct from the tonal capacity. Treat any claimed link between the two as unsupported here.

## Feeling and Touch

Feeling and Touch are the binary, and the halves divide by proximity. Ra's sixth tone is the direct counterpart of the fifth: "In talking about the 6 I'm talking about a tactile sensitivity in which contact can lead to the absorption of enormous amounts of discernable information."

Feeling works without contact. It reads the field a body sits in, at whatever distance that field extends. Touch requires contact, and the information arrives through the point of contact. Both are receptive, both are right-side, and both take in volumes of information rather than single signals. The difference is whether the reading happens across a space or at a surface.

## Conditioning and Access

Tone describes a plan, not a performance. Ra was blunt about most people's relation to their own architecture: "if somebody is not-self they are cut off from their cognition, and therefore they're cut off from their architecture underneath." When the frequency of the form is not correct, what comes through is a harmonic distortion rather than the true tonal architecture.

Access runs through the same door as everything else. Ra ended the senses lecture where he ended most things: "It is only through Strategy and Authority that you can align your vehicle to a frequency that is correct." Beneath that, the first transformation's dietary regimen feeds the sensory capacity physically. Ra described homogenized eating as spreading nutrition evenly across the senses so the one real capacity never gets fed, and correct regimen as "providing nutrition to those areas, specific areas that are going to free that cognition." Feeling as a working sense is downstream of [[The Four Transformations]].

## How It Is Commonly Misread

Three misreadings recur. The first treats Feeling as emotionality, warmth or empathy; Ra's fifth tone is an information channel and its content is field data, not sentiment. The second collapses it into the emotional wave. The third expects it to be visible. Ra placed the fully expressed fifth and sixth tonal potentials at the edge of what humans currently display, so tone 5 in a chart names what the architecture is for, not a sensitivity the person is using.

Contemporary teaching sometimes presents the six cognitions as a "super sense," one per person. That framing is later material, not Ra's own, though its point is his: each being has one specific cognitive gift written into the tonal architecture.
