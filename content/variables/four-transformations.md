# The Four Transformations

Variable is not a description of personality. It is a sequence. Read as a process rather than as a set of traits, the four arrows above the head of the bodygraph lay out four steps that follow one another in a fixed order: dietary regimen, then environment, then perspective, then awareness. Ra Uru Hu set the list out plainly: "the variable is made up of four things. It's made up of the dietary regimen. It's made up of the environment, the perspective and the awareness."

Ra called the graphic that carries these four arrows a map, and the word he used for what it maps was transformation. Introducing Variable to a general audience he said of the arrows, "We end up with a map for transformation." Each of the four steps changes something concrete. The first changes the body. The second changes the conditions the body lives in. The third changes what the mind is given to look at. The fourth changes what the mind does with what it sees.

This article follows that process. The derivation of the arrows from the substructure, their left and right orientations and the six values inside each category belong to [[The Four Arrows]], which is the mechanics article and should be read alongside this one.

## The Catalyst and the Prerequisite

Nothing in the four transformations begins before Type, [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]] are in operation. Ra was insistent on this and described strategy and authority as a catalyst rather than as a stage of the sequence itself. "So first the catalyzation and then you enter into the variable itself," he said.

His reasoning was mechanical. Deconditioning, in his account, is a change in the relationship between a person and the mental plane. Under homogenization the mind rules the life because the mind makes the decisions. Operating by strategy and authority removes decision making from the mind and gives it back to the body, and that single change begins to alter the geometry of the life. Ra put the consequence simply: it "begins the process of moving in a direction that eliminates resistance."

What it does not do is deliver awareness. In the same lecture Ra was careful to say so. "But it doesn't mean that suddenly you're going to be aware. It isn't. It doesn't mean suddenly that you're going to have a transcendent passenger consciousness at work. It does not. It is quite a process to get there." Variable is that process. It is not a shortcut around Type, Strategy and Authority, and the material in the corpus never presents it as one.

## The First Transformation: Dietary Regimen

The first arrow is read from the Design Sun and Earth, and the transformation it carries is [[Determination]], the way a body is designed to take nutrition in. Ra described it as the ground floor of the whole process. "The first variable points you. You know, the first aspect of variable points you to the most fundamental thing in combating homogenization in your life, and it is digestion."

The common misreading is that this is a diet in the ordinary sense. It is not. "You see, we do not eat correctly, and it is not about food," Ra said. "Funny enough, you know, it's not about whether it's good food or bad food, B.O. food or junk food. It isn't. It is the first thing to understand about the first transformation, is the first transformation is about conditions and circumstances when one is taking nutrition into one's body." What changes is the manner of eating, not a list of permitted ingredients.

Two results are claimed for it in the corpus. The first is cleansing and the differentiation of the body. In the short lecture that carries the title The Four Transformations, Ra said that "in changing the body you establish the platform for the true changing of the life," and that "the digestive system that is attuned and is aligned to what is correct transforms the life around it." The second result is neurological. Correct nourishment is presented as the condition for a healthy brain, and a healthy brain as the condition for cognition, which is the layer of Variable that sits beneath the arrows. Ra stated the dependency in its strongest form in a later broadcast: "You cannot transform the consciousness until you heal the brain system."

He also warned that the practice is socially costly. "The way we eat is so deeply conditioned. It is interwoven in our social interactions with each other." More generally, "Every single transformation in human design, every step in this process, has a quality of anti-socialism to it that always needs to be understood. It is not about being anti-social. It is about the way in which your own authority is perceived by others when you begin to make decisions based on your authority, in ways that others may not appreciate."

## The Second Transformation: Environment

The second arrow is read from the Design Nodes and carries [[Environment]]. Ra framed the pairing of the first two steps as inside and outside. Determination is what he called the internal, the tuning of the vehicle within. Environment is the external. In one lecture he set them side by side: "it's your environment that provides for you on the outside. What your dietary regimen provides for you on the inside."

Environment is about kinds of place, not particular addresses. "Every single human being has a correct place. And when I say place, I don't mean a place on a map in that sense. I mean an environmental place, the right place to be in, in terms of the circumstances of their environment, whether they're valley people or mountain people or whatever the case may be." The point is easy to miss, so Ra drew the parallel with the first step: "It's not about place any more than dietary regimen is about food."

The benefits he attached to the second transformation are long range rather than immediate. "We're here to live in the correct environment so that we can enjoy longevity, quality of life, and better associations." Being in the wrong environment, by the same logic, undoes what correct eating builds. Ra put it as a question: "You know, if you're not in an environment that's good for you, are you ever going to suffer?" Correct nutrition tunes the inside, and then the body is still thrown into auras and circumstances that are wrong for it.

## The Third Transformation: Perspective

The third arrow is read from the Personality Nodes and carries [[Perspective]]. With it the process crosses from the design side of the chart to the personality side, and from body to mind. Ra marked the crossing explicitly: "one moves to the third transformation, and this begins the mental process, and it is about perspective. It's about seeing."

Perspective is a way of looking, and a way of looking needs something to look at. That is the hinge between the second and third steps. "But you cannot take advantage of the unique perspective if you're not in the right place looking at the right things," Ra said, and he described the difficulty of bringing perspective to a conditioned person in exactly those terms, that they are not in the right place and so cannot really see.

Under conditioning, Perspective slips along the same harmonic that pulls [[Motivation]] out of place, but at the nodal level Ra called the slippage distraction rather than transference. His worked example is the possibility and probability pair: "the person that's supposed to see possibilities thinks that they see probabilities. And the person who is supposed to see probabilities thinks that all of those are possibilities. They get very very confused."

The third transformation is also where the fourth is prepared. "You know, this third transformation, it establishes the platform upon which our mind is going to conceptualize."

## The Fourth Transformation: Awareness and Conceptualizing

The fourth arrow is read from the Personality Sun and Earth. Ra named the step itself awareness. "And when we finally get to that fourth transformation, we are dealing with the theme of awareness. We are passenger consciousness."

A terminology note is needed here. The arrow in that position is named Motivation, and that is the name used throughout the arrow literature and in [[Motivation]]. Ra's name for the transformation carried by the same position is awareness, and what he says the Personality Sun and Earth govern is conceptualizing: it "represents the the way in which we conceptualize and the style of which we conceptualize mentally." Motivation is the arrow. Awareness and conceptualizing are what the fourth step is for.

Ra's account of the failure mode runs backwards through the whole chain. Conditioned seeing feeds a conditioned mind: "You're looking out of eyes that don't see the way they're supposed to see. They're distracted. That distraction gives your mind what it conceptualizes." And then, "you've got a mind without the correct motivation thinking about something that it's not supposed to be looking at."

The correct version reverses each term. "The further away you get from your mind manipulating your life, the more you can see. And out of that seeing, the richer the potential of your conceptualizing so that you can release your outer authority, which is what mind is about. Not controlling your life, but sharing your wisdom."

## Why the Order Is Not Optional

Ra stated the dependency chain as a single argument, and it is the clearest statement of the sequence anywhere in the recorded material: "to understand the nature of the mind is to understand that if the mind does not have access to correct perspective, then the mind can never, ever, ever, ever, ever be correct. And you cannot have the correct perspective if you're not in the right place. And if you're not in the right place, and you're not eating correctly, then there is no way that you're going to go through any of these transformations and what they bring."

Each step is the precondition of the next rather than merely its neighbour. The body must be nourished correctly before the brain system can support heightened cognition. The body must be in the right environment before there is anything correct to see. Seeing must be correct before conceptualizing has correct material to work with. Ra's summary of the first step makes the whole chain conditional on it: "But if you do not transform the physical health of the body, if you do not open up the potentials of the brain, if you do not take advantage of heightened cognition, then there is no way to fulfill all the transformations and to get to the end of that process, which is the awareness you're entitled to."

## Timing

Two different timescales appear in the corpus and they should not be confused.

The first is cellular deconditioning, which Ra treats as a seven year process and as a baseline rather than an achievement. "That it's not just strategy and authority and seven years of you know cellular change. It is much more than that. Much more than that." And more pointedly: "It's one thing to decondition cellularly the seven year process. But the deeper deconditioning, truly it begins with food." The seven years get the cells over. Variable is what is done with the body afterwards.

The second is developmental and is tied to Saturn. Describing how a nine centred being would mature under ideal conditions, Ra said that from birth to roughly thirty the only requirements are correct treatment, strategy and authority, and dietary regimen. "It's at 30 at the Saturn return. This is when the idealized upbringing takes on the demand of environment. It's between 30 and 50 that you establish through the environment its partner perspective and in that develop the foundation for awareness." He added that environment becomes a health issue from thirty onward, and told parents not to feel guilty about a young child's environment for that reason.

The wider frame is the difference between the seven centred Saturnian body, with its roughly thirty year cycle, and the nine centred Uranian body with its eighty four year cycle. The four transformations are laid out for a life of that length, "a life in which the Iranian expectation of 84 years can be lived out fully and completely."

## Outer Authority as the Endpoint

Outer authority is the far end of the sequence, and in Ra's usage it is not a role or a credential. It is what a differentiated awareness has to give. "To be a differentiated being is to be able to express as outer authority your unique awareness." The wisdom is offered because it was seen through eyes that were in the right place, in a body that was correctly nourished, by a mind that was not running the life.

That is why the chain closes where it opened. Ra put the two authorities in a single line: "Through inner authority you find your doorway to outer authority." Inner authority makes the decisions and starts the process. Outer authority is the wisdom that eventually comes out of it, "the wisdom that is here in each of us for the other."

Ra offered no guarantees about any of this. Of Variable he said it "lays out clearly the way to that transformation, guarantees nothing, promises nothing, but it's written there, written there at the deepest, deepest level of your genetic make-up." The map exists. Walking it is a separate matter.
