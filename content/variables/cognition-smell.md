# Cognition: Smell

Smell is the first of the six cognitions, the sensory architecture carried by the first tone. Ra Uru Hu put its keynote plainly: "number one is security. This is the tone of security and that security is based on smell." It is the ground floor of the cognitive structure, the layer at which a being first registers whether the world is safe.

Cognition is read off tone, not colour, and it is not a fifth arrow. Tone sits beneath colour in the substructure, and it is the layer the four arrows are built on. What tone does for the arrows is decide their direction. Ra: "It's tone that establishes whether something is to the left or something is to the right." Because the first tone sits on the left side of the tonal triangle, any position carrying it produces a left-pointing arrow.

The tonal triangle is the graphic form tone takes, and Ra taught it as a microcosm of the BodyGraph itself. It is divided down the centre, tones one, two and three on the left and four, five and six on the right, and the six are grouped into three binaries named after the awareness centres they mirror: the splenic binary at the lower left, tones one and two; the ajna binary at the top, tones three and four; the solar plexus binary at the lower right, tones five and six. Smell sits at the outer edge of the left, and Ra described its splenic alertness as exactly that: "this is the foundation of being left."

## Mechanics

Tone comes from the substructure of an activation. Beneath the gate is the line, beneath the line the colour, beneath the colour the tone, and beneath the tone the base. There are six tones, as there are six colours and six lines, and it is the tone of the Sun and Earth that carries cognition.

Smell is tone one. It shows up in a chart not as a label of its own but through its effect: a first tone on the design Sun and Earth turns the top left arrow, [[Determination]], to the left, which the corpus names as active. Left is not better than right. The Genetic Matrix reference in this library states the point directly: "These are not value judgments." Left describes strategic, focused, active engagement and right describes receptive, peripheral attention. They are modes of orientation, not grades of quality.

The corpus does not name any left and right variant for Smell itself, as it does for the Determination values. Smell is one thing, not two.

## The Splenic Binary

Ra's fullest treatment of Smell sits in "The Senses of the Body", where he describes the splenic binary as a defence system. "It's a basic defence system in which the very first tone is simply on alert all the time. This is the whole thing about smelling", he said, "on alert all the time in order to maintain one's security."

This places Smell at the start of what Ra called the progression of evolutionary awareness, which runs from the [[Splenic Center]] to the [[Ajna Center]] to the [[Solar Plexus Center]]. Splenic awareness, Ra said, "is still the only awareness that we can trust", because the mind is not here to make decisions and solar plexus awareness has not yet emerged. Smell is the oldest reliable intelligence in the architecture.

Ra also framed the splenic binary as intelligence born out of fear. Fear for any form, he said, is any threat to its survival, and at this depth the splenic binary is "the core intelligence to deal with and be inspired by the fear". The first tone is the most basic expression of that: being aware of anything that is a threat, and building from there the need of intelligence to have a secure foundation.

Its partner, the second tone, works the opposite way. The one is good at recognising things, building its defence mechanism, keeping the memory of it and protecting the vehicle. The two, as Ra put it, "doesn't pay any attention to maintaining the security", because it is interested in what has not happened yet. Together they guarantee the survivability of the vehicle, one by remembering what is dangerous, the other by being ready for what has never been met. See [[Cognition: Taste]] for the second half of the pair.

## Smell as a Way of Knowing

The name misleads. Smell here is not a preference for pleasant scents, and it is not primarily about the nose. Ra was explicit: "when I am speaking of smell I am not necessarily speaking of anything you recognize on the surface. This is about your Splenic system; this is about your lymphatic system with all its tiny little noses all over your body sniffing out information that is coming in."

What Smell does is recognise, immediately and without deliberation, whether something is safe. Ra described smelling another person as "a way of defining them genetically", a matter of "recognizing threats or not", and said the value of the sense lies not in aroma but in "the information that is carried in it". At the tonal level this is what Ra called immune system security, the inner security of the vehicle.

The same architecture reaches the surface differently on each side of the chart. On the design side, a first tone means a brain system focused on taking in sensory data and a heightened physical capacity to smell. On the personality side, it means "smelling in the sense of being able to interpret information and to be able to have a mind that is focused on security". The keynote holds either way. What changes is whether it operates as body or as mind.

## What Smell Becomes in the BodyGraph

Ra drew a sharp line between the tonal architecture and what the BodyGraph does with it. Tone is a floor plan for cognition; the BodyGraph is where the by-product shows up, and the by-product is not the plan. "It's the same, but it's different."

At the surface, smell is carried by a tribal stream: [[Gate 54]], [[Gate 32]], [[Gate 44]], [[Gate 26]] and its expression in [[Gate 21]]. All of these, Ra said, have a relationship to smell. In the [[Tribal Circuit]] the sense turns material. "In so many ways this stream is about the smell of money and the smell of success and the stink of failure", he said, noting that for a nine-centred being money is survival. Security, which at the tonal level is the inner security of the vehicle, "when you see it in the BodyGraph it becomes making money, it becomes survival on the material plane."

A strong activation in that stream is not the same as a first tone. Ra made the point directly: someone with heavy tribal activations but no first tone will have that channel work less efficiently, because another sense is their actual focus.

## Relationship to Determination

Smell and [[Determination]] are read off the same side of the chart and, for the design Sun and Earth, off the same activation. Determination is the colour of that position; Smell is the tone directly beneath it. They are not two readings stacked on each other but one structure seen at two depths. Ra described the sequence from below: "Color, being the medium, has a connection to the line above, but it also has a direct relationship to the tone below. It is the tone after all, that creates in essence what the color is going to be."

The first tone is therefore doing two jobs at once. It supplies the cognition, and it pushes the Determination arrow to the left. The corpus links the two again through [[PHS Diet (Primary Health System)]]. Correct dietary regimen, Ra said, does not merely feed the body generally; it feeds the brain regions that support one particular sense, "the one that's right for you, the one that is natural for you". For a first tone, that sense is smell.

Smell does not appear among the Determination values, which run Appetite, Taste, Thirst, Touch, Sound and Light. Taste and Touch appear in both lists and mean different things in each, but Smell belongs to cognition alone.

## How Smell Is Commonly Misread

The most common error is treating tone as a talent already in hand. Ra called it a potential and nothing more. The plans are there, but access begins with [[Strategy]] and authority. Describing a Generator operating correctly, he said that what the Sacral is tapping into is what lies underneath: "It's the smell and your ability to smell that's actually guiding your sacral." Human beings who do not follow their strategy and authority, he said, "cut themselves off from their unique cognition."

A second error is matching tone to line by number. Ra addressed this head on: "if you have a first tone and you have a first line, it does not mean that they have a direct relationship to each other. They do not." Colour is the medium between them. Contemporary material in this library does describe tones as resonating with profile lines by number, so the two framings sit side by side; Ra's caution is the stricter reading.

A third is reading Smell as squeamishness or sensitivity to odour. The corpus gives no support for that. What it describes is a survival-grade recognition running through the lymphatic system, whose surface form is an instinct about people, deals and value rather than an opinion about perfume.
