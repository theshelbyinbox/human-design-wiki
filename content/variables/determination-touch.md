# Determination: Touch

Touch is the fourth colour of [[Determination]], and the name is the single most misleading label in the whole variable. It has nothing to do with texture, with the feel of food in the mouth, or with being touched by another person. Contemporary teaching puts it bluntly: despite the name, this colour is about the social context of meals. Ra Uru Hu, who carried this colour himself, never used the word "touch" for it at all in the recorded nutrition material in this library. He called it by its two sides, calm and nervous, and he described it entirely in terms of who is around a person while they are digesting.

The question Touch asks is not what goes on the plate. It is whether the body gets the benefit of its food in quiet and solitude, or in company, noise of activity and the ordinary commotion of other people. Ra put the split plainly: "in this fourth color, you've got two sides. One side is calm and the other side is nervous." One of those sides eats alone. The other gets nothing out of a meal taken alone.

Touch is also the first of the three circumstances. Colours 1, 2 and 3 of Determination are what Ra called conditions, strict rules about the food itself: one thing at a time, a repeating menu, a temperature. From colour 4 upward the rules leave the plate entirely and move into the room. Ra called what sits above that line "a much more sophisticated, if you will, digestive system".

## Mechanics

Determination is read from the colour of the Design Sun and Earth, the top left arrow of [[The Four Arrows]], the body side of the variable. Touch is colour 4 of the six, which run Appetite, Taste, Thirst, Touch, Sound, Light. Its two variants are Calm and Nervous, and which one a person carries is decided by the tone underneath the colour, since tone is what establishes whether something falls to the left or to the right.

Contemporary reference material maps the pair as left tone Calm and right tone Nervous, with Calm needing to eat alone and away from other people's drama, and Nervous digesting best at a family or communal table. That left and right assignment comes from contemporary teaching rather than from Ra's own recorded words in this library, which name the two sides without placing them.

Colour sits in the same six-fold construct as line and tone. Ra described the architecture as "the six tones on one side, the six lines on the other side, the six colors in the middle", which is why the colours resonate with the profile lines by number. Touch, as colour 4, resonates with the fourth line and stands in harmonic relationship to the first: "there is a harmonic relationship that exists between the first and the fourth color, the second and the fifth color."

## From Conditions to Circumstances

Ra read the six determinations bottom to top as a record of human evolution. Colour 1 is an old digestive system that cannot handle complexity. Colour 2 is selective to the point of repetition. Colour 3 arrives with cooking, with heat, with what Ra called "the manipulation, if you will, of food". At colour 4 the whole logic changes. The strictness about the food itself relaxes and the strictness moves outward.

His own description of the crossing is the clearest statement of it: "when we go up here and we get to the um the circumstances, circumstances are very different. It's no longer these very very strict conditions. It isn't. It is about circumstances. It is about the environment." He was careful to add that this is not an upgrade. "Not better. There's no better in any of this."

Ra also noted that the circumstance digestive system "is relatively modern", which is part of why it can be served by arrangement and by technology in ways the conditions cannot. A calm person can choose to eat earlier. A nervous person can put a busy room around themselves. Nothing similar helps a colour 1 who has been handed an omelette.

## Calm and Nervous

Ra was a fourth colour, calm side, and his description of living it is the most concrete account of Touch in the corpus. "I'm a circumstance person, you know, I'm a fourth color, so I'm a circumstance person." What that meant in practice was solitude at meals: "one of the things for me that is so important in my life is that when I eat, it's as calm as it can possibly be." He ate alone most of the time, and he was explicit that the reason was mechanical rather than preferential. "It's about the environment around me because it's circumstance."

His eldest son carried the other side. "So, I'm calm and he's nervous." The nervous side needs the opposite arrangement entirely: "he needs a lot of things going on around while he's digesting his food." And the failure case is not a mild loss. Of a nervous person eating in quiet, Ra said, "if he's sitting by himself all in quiet like I would, calmly eating, he's not gaining any benefit" from the food at all.

That is the point worth holding. Neither side is being described as fussy. The same meal nourishes one arrangement and passes through the other.

## The Social Consequence

Because Touch is about the people in the room, it is the determination whose correctness collides most directly with everyone else's. Ra saw this in his own household: "And of course, this creates social consequences." With a calm father and a nervous son at one table, "somebody's got to lose, you know? I mean, somebody's got to lose." In his house it was usually him, "because it's never calm if everybody is sitting together".

This is a particular instance of something Ra said about the whole chain of transformation: "Every single transformation in human design, every step in this process, has a quality of anti-socialism to it that always needs to be understood." He immediately qualified it. "It is not about being anti-social. It is about the way in which your own authority is perceived by others when you begin to make decisions based on your authority, in ways that others may not appreciate." Eating apart from the family, or insisting on eating in the middle of them, reads socially as a statement. Mechanically it is neither.

Ra gave the conditioning version of this in an image of an infant. "The mother that only wants to breastfeed her nervous child in a totally quiet place, you know, and then she wonders why the child's got gas in his collic because the child wants nervous. It would prefer that the mother would breastfeed in a crowded cafe." The conditioning in Touch is rarely deliberate. It is a well-meaning arrangement of the room.

## Transference to Appetite

Colour operates under what Ra called transference, a slippage along the harmonic when the magnetic monopole fails to capture the emitted colour correctly. "The problem is always in the cap. It's never in the color." The harmonic for colour 4 is colour 1, so the transference partner of Touch, following the harmonic, is [[Determination: Appetite|Appetite]]. Ra works this mechanic out in detail for Motivation rather than for Determination in the recorded material here, so treat the Touch to Appetite pairing as the harmonic applied rather than as a worked example he gave.

What transference costs is stated generally: "the moment that you're in transference, you lose that", the "that" being the backing of the tone and sense underneath. The colour a person appears to be living is not supported by the architecture beneath it, and the result is distortion rather than a second valid setting.

## Not the Same as Cognition: Touch

Touch appears twice in the variable, and the two are unrelated. Determination: Touch is a colour, read from the Design Sun and Earth, and it governs the social setting of eating. [[Cognition: Touch]] is tone 6, read from the tone layer rather than the colour layer, and it is a mode of knowing. Ra described the sixth tonal potential as "a tactile sensitivity in which contact can lead to the absorption of enormous amounts of discernable information", paired with tone 5 in the Solar Plexus. One is about where a meal happens. The other is about how a person takes in the world. A chart can carry one, both or neither, and having Calm Touch says nothing about cognition. Because of this collision, the wiki never links a bare Touch; the qualified titles are always used.

## How Touch Is Commonly Misread

Three misreadings recur. The first is the name, taken literally as physical touch or as texture. The second is the collapse of Touch into [[Determination: Sound|Sound]], colour 5. Some contemporary summaries describe Touch loosely as sensitivity to environmental stimuli at meals, which is close enough to Sound to blur them. Ra kept them distinct: colour 5 is acoustic, "high frequency, low frequency, loud, soft", while colour 4 is about people and activity. The third is reading Calm as the healthy option and Nervous as agitation to be fixed. Ra's own framing rules this out. The nervous side is not stressed. It is unfed when the room is quiet.

Touch also does not stand alone. It is the first of the four transformations, the physical one, and Ra held that nothing later works without it. Until the food is taken in correctly, [[Environment]], [[Perspective]] and the awareness at the end of the chain remain out of reach.
