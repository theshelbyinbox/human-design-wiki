# Motivation: Fear

Fear is the first of the six Motivations, the colour lying beneath the Personality Sun and Earth that tints everything the mind does. Ra Uru Hu was blunt about where it sits in the sequence. "At the very basis of how we respond to being is fear. In other words, fear is the very first motivation."

It is worth being precise about what that claim does and does not say. Motivation is not an intention, a goal or a mood. It is the colouring underneath the line, the thing an actor asks the director for when the script alone will not tell them how to play the part. Ra used exactly that image when he taught colour at the personality level, describing the actor who looks at the line and asks what the motivation is, what drives the way this line comes out. The line is generic. The motivation is what makes the performance live. And it runs below the threshold of awareness: "There is an underlying motivation we have no access to affecting how we end up living things out on the surface."

So a person with Fear Motivation is not thereby a fearful person, and they will not usually recognise fear as the thing moving them. What they will recognise is the behaviour it produces. Ra described first colour Motivation as "a motivation that forces you to really make sure that you've got things, dig into things, look into things. You're driven by a fear of not knowing what's going on." Asked a question as ordinary as how are you, a first colour Motivation is genuinely listening for the answer, watching the face and the body for clues, because that is how it is moved.

## Mechanics

Motivation is read from the colour of the Personality Sun and Earth, the pair that also carries the conscious profile line and the incarnation cross. It is shown by the top right arrow of the four at the head of the BodyGraph, on the personality or mind side of Variable. Contemporary teaching labels this arrow Mind Style, with a left arrow described as strategic and a right arrow as receptive.

Fear is colour 1. The arrow's direction is not set by the colour at all. It is set by the tone underneath, so two people can both carry Fear Motivation and point in opposite directions. Ra's own formulation is that "It is tone that establishes whether something is to the left or something is to the right." The tone under colour 1 is splenic in nature, and Ra tied the first tone to security and to smell, which gives the Fear Motivation an unusually literal chemical floor: "I can be with you as a communalist because you smell right. I will leave you as a separatist because I don't like your smell anymore."

Colours resonate with profile lines by number, so Fear resonates with [[line 1]], and colour 1 harmonises with [[line 4]]. Ra set this out directly. "If you have a first line and you have a first color, they're in resonance." "If you have a first color and you have a fourth line, that's a harmony." A first colour sitting under a third line is dissonance, and Ra described that as a process that is "much more uncomfortable."

## The Communalist and the Separatist

Fear is the one Motivation Ra gave a fully worked binary, and it is the spine of the whole topic. "Everybody who's carrying a first line, wherever you see you have a first line (and I have ten), the underlying motivation is fear and the fear works two ways: you're either a communalist or you're a separatist."

The separatist pole moves away from the threat. Ra described it as running away, building a wall, arming oneself, becoming defensive to the point of paranoia, closing the barriers so that nobody gets close. He noted that people carrying strong first line themes often go unseen for long stretches of life because they are "hidden away somewhere digging into the foundation and trying to make it work," without any access to the fear underneath that is driving it, and that they become upset, nervous and cranky when the foundation is not coming together.

The communalist pole moves toward the group for the same reason. "I'm going to be safer if I share this space with others because there is safety in numbers." Ra added a second motive on top of safety: in a group there is the possibility of doing research and working together on the foundation. Either way the target is the same. "Fear drives us to find out how things work. Once we find out how things work, we get a sense of security."

## A Prana, Not a Pair of Boxes

Ra warned specifically against reading communalist and separatist as two fixed types a person belongs to. "I don't want you to look at this duality as polarities. I want you to look at it as a prana." It is an ongoing flow from one state to the other. He described himself as a separatist most of the time who knew how to be a communalist, and said the movement was organic and not under his control.

The flow has a characteristic timing that is worth knowing, because it is the part that shows up in ordinary life. Security does not settle the Motivation. It restarts it. As Ra put it, "the moment that they have the comfort of an established foundation is the moment that, already inside of them, separatism is growing to move on." He was explicit that this makes relationships difficult for first line beings, since a foundation that finally looks stable is precisely the condition under which the pull to separate appears again.

Ra read the same prana at species scale, as "an evolutionary way to establish the exoteric, to be able to establish the solid foundation." Communities form for security, reach a semblance of it, break up and move on, and the fragments form new communities that will break again. He compared it to cell division and to migratory pressure, one valley never being enough, and used the multiplication of religious sects as an example. He also thought this particular evolutionary programme was near its end, because there is very little territory left to be separatist about.

## Transference to Need

Motivation slips under conditioning, and the slippage runs along the harmonic: 1 to 4, 2 to 5, 3 to 6, and back. Ra: "Transference is a movement in a harmonic ... In a sense, one's motivation is always being pulled away ... by conditioning." Fear transfers to [[Motivation: Need]], and Need transfers back to Fear.

What transference costs is intelligence, not just accuracy. "The moment that you transfer to that four, you lose all your cognitive values." Ra's reasoning is that true Motivation is backed by the tone and the sense underneath it, by the capacity to conceptualise and assess. In transference that backing is gone, and what is left is appetite without information. "The motivation of the four is what it wants. It wants something. And because it's not backed by total intelligence, it does not do it very well."

A note on names is necessary here. Ra called colour 4 wanting. Contemporary teaching calls Motivation 4 Need, and reserves the word Wanting for Perspective 4, which is a different arrow entirely. The same word therefore names two different things depending on the source, and the two should not be conflated.

For someone with Fear Motivation, the signpost of transference is the arrival of a vague wanting that has no object and no research behind it, in place of the specific digging that Fear actually produces. Contemporary teaching glosses Fear Motivation as being motivated by security and by risk mitigation, and Need as assessing and meeting what the community requires, which gives a workable everyday contrast for spotting the slip.

## What Fear Is Not

Motivation Fear is not the same thing as the fears of the [[Splenic Center]], which are a separate mechanic: as Ra taught it, each of the spleen's gates is a gate of fear, and those are visceral, in the moment, survival fears. Fear as a Motivation is a colour under the Personality Sun and Earth and belongs to the mind side of Variable. A person can carry one without the other.

Nor is it a diagnosis of temperament. Fear here is a drive toward foundation, and its visible result is usually thoroughness rather than anxiety.

## Living It

Motivation is not an entry point. Ra was clear that without correctness the colour does not deliver: "if you are not correct, you don't get the underlying motivation that delivers that line the way it's meant to be delivered." Correctness means living [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]] first, and in the body the chain of transformations runs dietary regimen, then environment, then perspective, then conceptualising. Motivation sits at the last step of that chain, the personality Sun and Earth, which Ra described as the arrival point for awareness. Contemporary teaching agrees on the ordering, treating Motivation as most useful once type, strategy and authority are already being lived.
