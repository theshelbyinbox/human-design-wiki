# Determination: Sound

Sound is the fifth colour of Determination, the layer of Variable that describes how a body is designed to take nourishment in. It is not about what is eaten. It is about the acoustic surround in which eating happens. Ra Uru Hu introduces it in a single compressed line: "the fifth color, the circumstance of sound of sound. High frequency, low frequency, loud, soft."

That word circumstance is doing real work. Ra divides the six determinations into a lower three and an upper three. The lower three, Appetite, Taste and Thirst, are conditions: strict requirements built into the way food itself is taken. The upper three, Touch, Sound and Light, are circumstances, and circumstances point outward, away from the plate and into the room. "It's no longer these very very strict conditions," Ra says. "It is about circumstances. It is about the environment. This is a a much more sophisticated, if you will, digestive system. Not better. There's no better in any of this."

Sound is the second of those three circumstances. Where [[Determination: Touch|Touch]] concerns the social setting of a meal and [[Determination: Light|Light]] concerns whether the sun is up, Sound concerns one variable only: how much noise is in the air while the body is digesting.

## Mechanics

Determination is read from the colour of the Design Sun and Earth, the two positions that sit at the top left of the Bodygraph as the Determination arrow. Colour 5 in that position gives Sound. The arrow itself points left or right, active or passive, and the direction is settled one layer further down, by tone: tones 1 to 3 throw the arrow left, tones 4 to 6 throw it right. The colour names the determination; the tone underneath it names the orientation.

Ra's own description of the two poles is given as sensation rather than as labels. He describes "somebody that either is going to need to eat in a place that is absolutely quiet and, you know, no noise or for somebody that needs to eat and there is a volume of sound that just well fills them up." Contemporary teaching fixes those two poles as named variants, High Sound for the left orientation and Low Sound for the right. Contemporary sources also extend the sensitivity beyond audible noise to ambient electromagnetic frequencies at meals, so that a Low Sound body is described as wanting not only quiet but minimal wireless activity nearby. That electromagnetic extension does not appear in Ra's recorded material in this library and should be held as later teaching rather than as his.

## A Modern Digestive System

One of the more unusual observations Ra makes about the circumstances is that they are recent. "Interesting thing by the way about circumstance because the circumstance digestive system is relatively modern," he says, and he draws a practical conclusion from it: the upper three colours, and the fifth and sixth in particular, are the ones that technology can serve directly.

His two examples are symmetrical. Someone who needs volume but lives somewhere quiet is not stranded, because headphones and a crank of the dial supply the missing frequency at the table. Someone who needs silence but lives in a city is not stranded either, because noise-cancelling headphones subtract it. "I mean, you can sit in silence in the middle of the noisiest place in the world and eat and have a lovely digestive process."

For the lower three colours the requirement is bound up with the food itself, so the room cannot substitute for it. For Sound the requirement is the room, and a room can be engineered.

## What Correctness Looks Like

The evidence for Sound tends to arrive as a body's protest rather than as a preference the mind reports. One account in the recorded material describes an infant whose design called for quiet eating: fed with the television on or in a noisy public place he would fuss, squirm and cry, and fed in a quiet room he settled, ate and was content. The household response was to build a quiet time around feeding, with no television, no music and the sound level kept as low as possible.

That is Sound working in the direction the mechanics predict. Nothing about the food changed. Only the acoustic circumstance changed, and the digestion followed it.

Ra is blunt that living this out has a social cost. Food, he notes, is one of the things that binds us to others, and a regimen that will not bend to the table can be alienating. His answer is to weigh that cost rather than pretend it is absent: "Are you willing to sacrifice your your body, your health, your brain, your well-being because some people don't like the way you want to eat?" And the standard he sets is not quality but accuracy. "It's not about how well you eat. Remember that. It's how correctly you eat."

## Quietness That Spreads

The clearest statement in the whole corpus of what a circumstance actually does is a passage Ra gives about the Sound requirement itself. Describing someone whose dietary regimen asks that they be quiet while they eat, he says the practice does not stay at the table: "it isn't just about being quiet while you eat it's about understanding that the longer you follow your practice the more you will realize that your life is becoming quiet and it is in that quietness that the gifts of your awareness that the potential of your sensory acuity is all heightened it is the nature of transformation at its purest".

Two claims sit inside that. The first is that a correct circumstance propagates: a quiet meal, repeated, becomes a quiet life, because the body keeps selecting for the condition that serves it. The second is that the payoff is sensory rather than dietary. What heightens is acuity. This is why Determination is the first of [[The Four Transformations]], the digestive correction building the platform the later stages stand on.

## Transference and the Pull Toward Taste

Ra describes colour as something that transfers. Under conditioning the magnetic monopole fails to capture the emitted colour frequency cleanly and pulls in its harmonic instead, and the harmonic pairs at the colour level are the same as at the line level: first with fourth, second with fifth, third with sixth. For Determination specifically he frames the result as homogenization, a predisposition to form differentiation replaced by a predisposition to form its opposite.

Colour 5 therefore slips toward colour 2, [[Determination: Taste|Taste]], its harmonic partner. The corpus states both the harmonic relationship between second and fifth colour and the transference mechanic directly, but does not spell out this pair as a worked example, so it is given here as following the harmonic rather than as quoted teaching. What the mechanic predicts in outline is a determination whose real requirement is acoustic being lived out as though it were a requirement about food selection, the room ignored and the menu fussed over.

Because colours and tones resonate with the profile lines by number, colour 5 carries a resonance with [[line 5]] and a harmony with [[line 2]], which is the same 2 and 5 relationship seen from the other side of the structure.

## Sound, Tone and Cognition

Sound is a determination, not a cognition. [[Cognition]] is read from tone, not colour, and its six values are Smell, Taste, Outer Vision, Inner Vision, Feeling and Touch. Hearing is not among them. But the two are more closely bound than the separation suggests, because Ra treats sound as the frame the whole tonal architecture sits in. Describing the tonal diagram he calls what is listed there sound as frequency itself, and follows it with the claim that "everything that we call cognition is our ability to interpret frequency." The senses are simply the instruments: "Do you interpret the frequency? with your nose, with your mouth, with your eyes, with your whole body, with your touch."

That is also where the two layers meet in practice. Ra says of the design Sun and Earth tones that the tonal influence does not only set the dietary regimen in general terms, "but it's also going to specifically align that dietary regimen to the enhancement of one particular sense, the one that's right for you, the one that is natural for you." A Sound determination, correctly lived, is feeding one specific cognitive instrument named by the tone beneath it.

## How Sound Is Commonly Misread

It is read as a taste in music. The determination says nothing about genre or preference. It describes the level of ambient frequency a body needs present while it digests, which is a different question from what anyone enjoys hearing.

It is collapsed into Touch. The two sit next to each other in the sequence and both concern the room, but Touch is about the social setting of eating, calm or nervous company, while Sound is about acoustic volume alone. A person can need a busy room and silence in it, or an empty room and noise.

The variant names are taken as Ra's. High Sound and Low Sound are consistent with what he describes, but they are contemporary labels laid over his looser phrasing of high frequency and low frequency, loud and soft.

The arrow is read as the source. The left or right orientation of the Determination arrow is decided by tone, not by the colour, so two people can both carry Sound and point opposite ways.
