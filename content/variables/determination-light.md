# Determination: Light

Light is the sixth and last colour of Determination, the dietary regimen in which the deciding factor is not what is on the plate, not the company, not the noise in the room, but whether the sun is up. Ra Uru Hu introduced it at the end of his run through the six by calling it "The final and most sensitive of all digestive systems is the six this light and you can see it's about direct and indirect." Its two variants are Direct and Indirect, and in practice that means the body is built either to take food in during the day or to take food in after dark.

Determination is read from the colour of the Design Sun and Earth, and it sits in the top left arrow of the four arrows, the arrow marked Active on the left and Passive on the right. It is the first of the four transformations, the body level step that everything after it depends on. Light is the last value in the sequence that runs Appetite, Taste, Thirst, Touch, Sound, Light.

Ra divided that sequence in half. The lower three, Appetite, Taste and Thirst, carry a super keynote he called conditions. The upper three, Touch, Sound and Light, carry circumstances. Conditions are rigid rules about the food itself and how it arrives. Circumstances are about the setting the food is taken in. Ra was blunt that the split is not a ranking: "Not better. There's no better in any of this." Light sits at the very top of the circumstances, which makes it the most environmentally contingent of the six. Nothing about the meal is prescribed. Only the illumination it happens in.

## Mechanics

Light is colour 6 of the Design Sun and Earth. As with every Determination value, the colour gives the theme and the tone beneath it decides which of the two variants applies, and with it whether the Determination arrow points left or right. Ra described the whole scheme as six groups that "break down into a duality for each so that ultimately we have 12 kinds of ways in which human beings are designed to digest their food." Light is one of those six, and Direct and Indirect are its two halves.

Because Light belongs to the circumstances rather than the conditions, it does not tell a person what to eat or how much. The rule it imposes is about timing, and timing measured against the sun rather than the clock. Contemporary teaching states the two sides plainly: Direct Light functions best eating during daylight hours, and Indirect Light is suited to eating after sunset and before sunrise. A contemporary dictionary entry in the same library puts it more strictly still, describing the direct side as needing daylight and actual sun exposure at the meal, and the indirect side as eating only after sundown and never while the sun is up.

## Direct and Indirect

Ra's own framing is simpler and more absolute. "For those beings who are six color, they're either daytime eaters or nocturnals." He glossed it as the difference between "those beings that can only eat when the sun is up and those beings that can only eat when the sun is down."

He expected the nocturnal side to sound absurd on first hearing, and said so. In his account of clients and students, the intellectual shock passed quickly and what followed was recognition, that this was already roughly what the person had been doing when left alone, and that the pattern had been overwritten by the social conditioning of what you do with your time at lunch. He reported that people who moved into the nocturnal regimen tended to register immediately that it was healthier for them. Two practical notes came with it. Liquids during the day are not excluded, and hunger during the daylight hours was not what these people complained of.

The two variants also differ in what happens at the end of the day, and here the instruction reverses cleanly. The daytime eater needs the digestive system to finish its work before sleep. Ra called sleeping on food "very very unhealthy for these beings." For the nocturnal the opposite holds, and he said it is important for them to sleep on their food. Same colour, opposite mechanics, decided by the tone underneath.

## The Most Modern of the Regimens

Ra made two observations about Light that set it apart from the other five.

The first is that the circumstance regimens are recent. "The circumstance digestive system is relatively modern," he said, and he drew a practical conclusion from it: circumstance people, and specifically the fifth and sixth colours, can get real benefit from technology, because the variable being controlled is in the room rather than in the body. His worked example in the corpus is the fifth colour and sound, headphones to supply volume where there is none and noise reduction to remove it where there is too much. The library does not record him giving an equivalent worked example for Light.

The second is that he treated colour 6 as forward pointing rather than settled. He called Light "perhaps the most unusual of the dietary regimens" and tied it to the mutative forces he associated with 2027 and an emerging form principle. His general rule for sixes applies: "whenever you're dealing with sixes, you're really dealing with something that is pointing towards another place."

## Transference to Thirst

Colour transfers. Ra located the mechanism in the magnetic monopole, which captures the emitted colour frequency, and he was insistent that the fault is never in the colour itself but always in the capture. When the general frequency of a life is not correct, the monopole cannot hold the colour that is actually being emitted, and it pulls in the harmonic instead.

He used the sixth colour as his illustration twice over. Colour follows the same six part structure as line, with the lower three and the upper three standing in the same relative positions, so there is a harmonic relationship between the first and the fourth colours, the second and the fifth, and the third and the sixth. Applied to a six emitting colour: "So instead of getting a six, it pulls in a three." Elsewhere he put it as the monopole simply not being able to carry the six, so it captures the third, and in that distortion access to the cognitive potential underneath is lost.

For Determination, colour 3 is [[Determination: Thirst|Thirst]]. A Light person living out of alignment is therefore pulled toward a Thirst regimen, a condition about the temperature of what is taken in rather than a circumstance about when it is taken. The pull is the wrong shape twice over, since it drags a circumstance person back below the line into conditions. Ra's point about the captured colour is that it is not supported by the tonal values beneath it, so the borrowed regimen delivers none of the sensory advantage the real one would.

## Resonance with the Sixth Line

Colours resonate with profile lines by number, so colour 6 resonates with line 6, and the harmonic that colour 6 slips to, colour 3, is the same number as the line that line 6 pairs with. The transference partner and the line harmonic run on identical arithmetic, which is why Ra could describe both with the same hexagram image of a lower trigram and an upper trigram occupying the same positions. It is a structural echo rather than a claim that a sixth line profile and sixth colour Determination produce the same behaviour. Nothing in the corpus says a person with Light Determination will also have a sixth line profile, and the two are read independently.

## How Light Is Commonly Misread

The name invites metaphor. Because the word light carries connotations of visibility and exposure, the variant names get read as being seen or not being seen, and one contemporary discussion in the library does play with Indirect Light as a fear of the limelight. That reading is explicitly exploratory in its source and has no footing in Ra's material, where the subject is the illumination present while food is being digested and nothing else.

The second misreading is treating Light as a preference rather than a mechanic. Ra's framing throughout the nutrition material is that this is not about eating well, it is about eating correctly, and that the social cost of an unusual mealtime is not a reason to abandon it. He put the question directly: are you willing to sacrifice your body, your health, your brain, your well-being because some people do not like the way you want to eat.

The third is sequence. Variable is not a shortcut past Type, [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]], which are the catalyst and the precondition, and the dietary regimen is the first of four transformations rather than a standalone health tip. Correct food intake is what feeds the sensory capacity that [[Cognition]] depends on, and the cellular turnover involved is a matter of years rather than weeks.
