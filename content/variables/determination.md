# Determination

Determination is the first of the four Variable arrows and the first of the four transformations. It sits at the top left of the Bodygraph, on the body side of the chart, and it describes the way a particular body and brain are built to take things in. In Ra Uru Hu's own illustration of the material it carries a single keynote: determination is "a genetic predisposition to form differentiation". Everyone is born with a specific way of taking nourishment in, and taking it in that way is what allows a person to become the differentiated thing they were designed to be rather than one more member of the homogenized herd.

The same territory goes by several names. Ra taught it as digestion and as dietary regimen, and the body of work built around it is called the Primary Health System, or PHS. Contemporary sources also label the top left arrow Brain Style. All of these point at one mechanic. The wiki's existing overview of the applied side of this material is at [[PHS Diet (Primary Health System)]]; this page covers the category itself, its derivation and its six values.

What makes Determination unusual is that it says almost nothing about food. "It isn't about the food at all," Ra said in his Nutrition lecture. "It's about how you take the food into your system." The knowledge does not rank cuisines, ingredients or philosophies of eating. It looks at the design of the digestive system and asks under what conditions or in what circumstances that system can extract what it needs. His standing joke was that when he was correct in the way he took nutrition in, it did not matter whether he was eating roadkill or fine dining. The measure is not quality. "It's not about how well you eat," he said. "Remember that. It's how correctly you eat."

## Mechanics

Determination is derived from the colour of the Design Sun and Earth. Colour is one of the layers of substructure that sit beneath gate and line, and on the design side of the chart colour produces determination. Ra put it plainly in the Nutrition lecture: "it is through color that we get to devise or find out what is specifically your particular dietary regimen." Because colour runs one to six, there are six determinations.

Each of the six then splits in two. The split comes from tone, the layer sitting one level below colour. Ra described six regimens that "are dualities. In other words, that they have two sides to them that binary is the influence of the tone underneath the color." Six colours times two tonal sides gives twelve, and Ra counted them exactly that way: "for humanity, there are 12 different ways in which digestive process works uniquely."

Tone is also what makes the arrow point. In his teaching on substructure Ra said, "It's tone that establishes whether something is to the left or something is to the right. It is the architecture that determines it." Tones one, two and three sit on the left of the tonal triangle and tones four, five and six sit on the right. Which side the Design Sun's tone falls on is what turns the top left arrow left or right. This is why tone, and therefore [[Cognition]], is not a fifth arrow but the layer underneath all four of them. Ra described Variable as "rooted in what is the pure essence of our purpose, that is the cognitive potential that is there at the tonal level that determines the way in which those arrows are going to point one way or another."

For the Determination arrow specifically, left is Active and right is Passive. Ra connected these terms directly to the body: "What we get to see through leftness and rightness is the activity level of the brain, whether it is active or passive." He set the four orientations out together as "being active which is left, being passive, which is right". The pairing matters, because the other three arrows carry different words. [[Motivation]] is strategic and receptive, [[Perspective]] is focused and peripheral, and [[Environment]] has its own orientation. All four are described together at [[The Four Arrows]].

Left is not a better result than right. Contemporary reference material on Variable states the point directly: "These are not value judgments." Both orientations are correct configurations, not stages on a ladder, and Determination is not a personality label of any kind.

Because Determination comes from the Design Sun and Earth it is unconscious, and it depends on an accurate birth time, since small changes can move the tone and flip the arrow.

## Conditions and Circumstances

Ra divided the six into two groups of three, and he gave each group a super keynote. Looking at the graph of the six, he said, "You can see it's divided in half. The bottom three, the upper three, that is the one, two, three at the bottom and then the four, five, six at the top. The bottom three have a super key note. And that super key note that they have is called conditions. So each of the lower colors, the one, two, and the three, they all bring certain eating conditions. The upper color brings circumstances."

The difference is where the requirement lives. A condition is a rule about the intake itself: what arrives, in what order, at what temperature. Ra called conditions "very rigid" and described the lower three as older, primitive digestive systems that cannot handle much complexity. A circumstance is a rule about the surroundings: who is present, how loud it is, whether the sun is up. Ra, a fourth colour, called himself "a circumstance person" and explained the shift this way: "it's not about me. It's about the environment around me because it's circumstance."

Reading the six from one to six is therefore also reading a progression. Ra described the movement from bottom to top as "our evolution", with the first colour as a very old digestive system and the sixth as "the final and most sensitive of all digestive systems". Circumstance regimens, being relatively modern, can often be met with technology in a way conditions cannot.

## The Six Determinations

Each determination carries a nickname that names the sense it is organised around, and each splits into a left and a right variant. The colour numbers resonate with profile lines by number, so colour one carries something of the first line, colour two of the second, and so on.

### Appetite

Colour one, and the foundation of the whole structure. Ra nicknamed it the caveman or cavewoman regimen and called it "the most primitive digestive system". The requirement is simple and severe: these are "people that are designed to eat one thing at a time", and Ra observed that the effect of moving to that pattern can transform someone physically, because it is what the system was built for. The variants are Consecutive and Alternating. Reference material in the library describes both as eating one thing at a time, with the left variant finishing one food before starting the next and the right alternating between foods bite by bite. See [[Determination: Appetite]].

### Taste

Colour two, and Ra called it "incredibly incredibly selective". These are people designed for "a very precise and set food regimen. That is a menu, a set menu that they repeat every single day", changing only with the seasons. He described them as the desert island people, and described the suffering of second colour babies who are pushed to eat diversity. The variants are Open and Closed. The library's reference material describes Open Taste as gradually expanding its menu over time and Closed Taste as sticking to familiar foods and resisting new ones. See [[Determination: Taste]].

### Thirst

Colour three, and the last of the conditions. Despite the name, this is about temperature. Ra: "the third color is really about hot and cold. It's about temperature. It's about the manipulation of food." Hot Thirst takes in food above body temperature because, as Ra explained, the digestive system "doesn't generate enough chemical heat in order to be able to break down the food". Cold Thirst has an overheated system, and warm food burns off what is of value. The variants are Hot and Cold. See [[Determination: Thirst]].

### Touch

Colour four, and the first of the circumstances. Again the name misleads: this is about the social setting of the meal. The variants are Calm and Nervous. Ra, who is Calm, described eating alone most of the time so the digestion could work, and described his eldest son, who is Nervous, as needing "a lot of things going on around while he's digesting his food". He added the consequence plainly: if a Nervous person eats quietly and alone, "he's not gaining any benefit from the food at all. None." Reference material in the library describes Nervous Touch as digesting best breaking bread at a family or communal table. See [[Determination: Touch]].

### Sound

Colour five, where the acoustic surroundings regulate the digestion. Ra described someone who "is going to need to eat in a place that is absolutely quiet and, you know, no noise" and someone else for whom "there is a volume of sound that just well fills them up". The variants are High and Low. This is the regimen Ra singled out as most amenable to technology: headphones can supply the volume a fifth colour needs in a quiet countryside, or remove the noise of a city for someone who needs silence. Library reference material extends the fifth colour's sensitivity to ambient electronic frequencies as well as sound. See [[Determination: Sound]].

### Light

Colour six, which Ra called "perhaps the most unusual of the dietary regimens" and connected to the mutative forces at work in the form. Sixth colour people are "either daytime eaters or nocturnals", eating only while the sun is up or only after it has gone down. The variants are Direct and Indirect. Ra noted that a daytime eater needs digestion finished before sleep and that it is "very very unhealthy for these beings to sleep on their food", while the opposite is true for the nocturnal. He also noted that nocturnals can take liquids during the day and do not complain of hunger. See [[Determination: Light]].

## Determination and Cognition

Determination and [[Cognition]] are two readings of the same substructure. Determination is the colour of the Design Sun and Earth; cognition is the tone beneath that colour, and the tone concretizes in the brain as a sense. The six cognitions pair into binaries that mirror the awareness centers: tones one and two form the splenic binary, tones three and four the ajna binary, and tones five and six the solar plexus binary, corresponding to the [[Splenic Center]], the [[Ajna Center]] and the [[Solar Plexus Center]].

The connection between the two layers is causal and it runs in one direction. Nutrition is what feeds the sense. Ra's argument is that in a homogenized person the wrong nutrition is spread evenly across the brain, so nothing in particular is well fed and the designed cognitive strength never becomes available. Correct intake changes the distribution. "The moment that you enter into the right dietary regimen," he said, "what you're doing is you're providing nutrition to those areas, specific areas that are going to free that cognition."

He took [[Cognition: Smell|smell]] as his example. Someone designed for smell who eats correctly is not simply better at detecting aromas; they are getting information, the kind that defines another person genetically or recognises a threat, and that information then backs their decision making. This is the sense in which Determination governs intake of information and not only of food.

Ra also tied dietary regimen to the development of the brain itself. He described the ordinary pattern of neural development shutting down at around three years of age, and stated that PHS predicts a child raised correctly and fed according to their determination would not experience neural shutdown until seven. That claim is a prediction, and Ra presented it as such.

## Determination Transference

The same illustration that gives determination its keynote gives its failure mode a name. Beneath "determination, a genetic predisposition to form differentiation" Ra wrote "determination transference, a conditioned predisposition to form homogenization". Transference is not a mood or an attitude. It is what happens mechanically when intake is wrong: the system stops supporting difference and starts supporting sameness.

Transference is a property of colour generally. Ra described it as a harmonic slippage, the same relationship that exists between the lines of a hexagram. "There is a harmonic relationship that exists between the first and the fourth color, the second and the fifth color, and the third and the sixth." Under conditioning the monopole fails to capture the emitted colour correctly and the frequency slides to its harmonic partner. Ra called this "at the very root of what I refer to as the homogenized being or the notself".

Applied to Determination, that harmonic pairs Appetite with Touch, Taste with Sound, and Thirst with Light, in both directions. Ra states the harmonic and states that determination transference exists, but the library does not contain him naming the six determination pairs one by one, so these pairings should be read as following the colour harmonic rather than as a quoted teaching. The equivalent mechanic on the nodes is called distraction rather than transference.

The signature Ra described is physical rather than psychological. Not following the regimen produces homogenized intake, and he listed digestive problems, weight problems and diminished sensory acuity as the consequences.

## Why Determination Comes First

The four transformations run in a fixed order: dietary regimen, then [[Environment]], then [[Perspective]], then awareness. Determination is first because everything above it depends on the brain being properly fed. Ra: "It is the first step in transformation after catalyzation, and it is all about digestion."

The order is a dependency, not a preference, and Ra stated the consequence of breaking it: "if the mind does not have access to correct perspective, then the mind can never, ever, ever, ever, ever be correct. And you cannot have the correct perspective if you're not in the right place. And if you're not in the right place, and you're not eating correctly, then there is no way that you're going to go through any of these transformations." The full sequence is set out at [[The Four Transformations]].

Nor is Variable a way around the basics. [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]] are the catalyst and the precondition. "So first the catalyzation and then you enter into the variable itself." Strategy and authority change how a person moves and decides; they do not by themselves change how the brain is fed. Ra called entering the transformations "a highly disciplined inner voyage" with real social consequences, since eating is bound up with how people relate to each other. He described the process as carrying "a quality of anti-socialism", by which he meant the friction of following one's own authority, not hostility toward others.

## Common Misreadings

Several of the six names point away from what they describe, and two do so badly enough to mislead consistently.

Thirst is not about drinking. Colour three is about the temperature of what is taken in, above or below body temperature. Library reference material makes the point directly, calling Thirst a misleading name for what is essentially temperature.

Touch is not about physical contact. Colour four is about the social context of the meal, whether the body digests best in calm or in the presence of other people. Nothing in the material concerns being touched.

Three further errors are worth naming. The first is treating Determination as a diet in the ordinary sense, a list of permitted foods; the mechanic concerns conditions and circumstances of intake, not content. The second is reading the arrow in isolation, when contemporary reference material warns that the meaning emerges from the full four-arrow pattern. The third is treating Variable as a shortcut past Type, Strategy and Authority, which it is not.

The library's sources also disagree on Appetite. One contemporary guide describes Consecutive as thriving on larger structured meals and Alternating as benefiting from frequent small meals through the day. The reference dictionary and Ra's own lecture instead keep both variants inside the one thing at a time rule, using consecutive and alternating for the order of the eating. Ra's account is the older and the more specific.

Finally, Determination is measured, not chosen. Ra framed PHS as the work of determining what a person's determination actually is and then supporting them in it. The predisposition it names also guarantees nothing on its own: it is, as Ra put it, "like somebody who has talent and never practices".
