# Perspective

Perspective is the way a mind is built to see. It is the bottom right of the four Variable arrows, and it is derived from the colour of the Personality Nodes. Ra Uru Hu described the position plainly: "the personality nodes represent the fact that each and every one of us have a unique and differentiated way in which we look at the world. It is called perspective and it is our unique view." It is also labelled simply View, and some chart software prints it under that name.

Perspective is not an opinion, a belief or a philosophy. It is the frame that decides which features of a scene register at all before any thinking happens. Two people can watch the same meeting or the same argument and come away with genuinely different raw material, because one is built to register what is possible in it and the other what is likely to happen. Neither is editing the other's report. They were never looking at the same thing.

This is the third of [[The Four Transformations]] and the first of the two that belong to mind. It depends absolutely on the two that come before it. Correct food produces a brain system that works, correct environment produces a body at ease in the world, and only then does the seeing come right. Ra Uru Hu was blunt about the dependency: "if the mind does not have access to correct perspective, then the mind can never, ever, ever, ever, ever be correct. And you cannot have the correct perspective if you're not in the right place."

## Mechanics

Variable comes from the substructure beneath gate and line. Below every line there are six colours, and below every colour six tones. Colour supplies the four arrow categories. Tone supplies [[Cognition]] and also decides which way each arrow points.

The Perspective arrow is calculated from the colour of the Personality Nodes. The same nodal axis on the design side gives [[Environment]]. The Personality Sun and Earth give [[Motivation]], and the Design Sun and Earth give [[Determination]]. Perspective and Motivation sit on the right of the diagram because both are personality functions, mind rather than body.

The arrow points left or right according to the tone underneath the colour, tones one to three pointing left and tones four to six pointing right. Ra Uru Hu: "It's tone that establishes whether something is to the left or something is to the right." For this arrow the two orientations are named Focused on the left and Peripheral on the right. Ra Uru Hu listed them in his run through the binary of leftness and rightness: "being focused, which is left, being peripheral, which is right."

Describing what leftness and rightness do to seeing, he used the example of watching a crowd. The left approach hunts: "You have a strategic view. You're always looking for a focus." A single line of attention settles on one thing, and because there is a single line it can be carried back and stored as memory. The right approach does not hunt. "The right visual approach is peripheral. Rather than looking for something and honing in on it, it is simply opened up and it's taking it all in."

Left is not the better setting. A contemporary reference page states the point directly: "These are not value judgments." Focused is consistency in a defined field, specificity, concentrated attention. Peripheral is receptivity, flexibility, panoramic attention. They are two different ways of gathering, and the Variable configuration a person is born with does not change.

Because Variable is read from tone and colour, it needs an accurate birth time. Ra Uru Hu warned that two to three minutes is the outer edge of safety for a colour calculation, and that the shortage of accurate birth data was one reason he held this material back in the early years.

There is a small ordering note worth knowing. The standard display convention writes the personality code with the Motivation letter first and the Perspective letter second. Contemporary reference material observes that the correct order for working with correctness on the personality side is Perspective first and Motivation second, and that the display convention preserves an order Ra Uru Hu originally specified and later acknowledged as wrong. The transformation sequence itself has always put perspective before conceptualizing.

## Seeing Correctly Depends on Being in the Right Place

Perspective is the only one of the four arrows that is explicitly conditional on another. Ra Uru Hu tied it to the design nodes every time he taught it: "the personality nodes are the possibility that arises out of being in the correct environment. Because if you're in the correct environment, then you're going to see what you're here to see."

The chain runs body first. A brain nourished according to its dietary regimen can operate. A body placed in its correct environment settles. Out of that settling, the view comes online. Ra Uru Hu described the environmental transformation as the thing from which "comes the possibility of the first mental transformation, perspective." Contemporary teaching puts the same mechanic the other way round, noting that if a person is not in the right place they will not see properly, however clever the mind is.

This is why Perspective cannot be worked on directly. The correction is upstream, in food and in place, and the seeing follows or it does not.

## The Six Perspectives

The six values run in the same order as the six colours and resonate with the profile lines by number, colour one with line one and so on. The lower three and the upper three also harmonise across the gap, one with four, two with five, three with six. Ra Uru Hu named four of the six directly in the recorded material available here: possibility, probability, power and personal. The names Survival and Wanting for the first and fourth come from contemporary teaching, which supplies the full enumeration one through six.

### 1 Survival

The first view reads the world for pattern and for what can be relied upon. Contemporary teaching describes it as analytical, oriented to trends, evidence and what can be proved, and compares it to the analyst poring over trend reports to find the pattern that will hold. The search is for a foundation solid enough to stand on. See [[Perspective: Survival]].

### 2 Possibility

The second view sees what could be, and Ra Uru Hu described it in detail. "Their view is possibility. This is the correct perspective for them." What dominates the field is the possibilities themselves, "and just the fact that there are possibilities." Crucially, he added that it is not about assessing them: "It's not about looking to see whether the possibilities are going to become something or where the possibilities are going to flourish. It is simply about seeing possibilities." See [[Perspective: Possibility]].

### 3 Power

The third view sees structure, hierarchy and how the game is actually played. Ra Uru Hu used the phrase "a view to power" in describing his own distraction. Contemporary teaching fills in the texture, calling it political, and describing a person who registers the players, the alliances and the way influence moves through a situation before registering anything else. See [[Perspective: Power]].

### 4 Wanting

The fourth view looks outward at the other and at what the collective needs. Contemporary teaching describes it as social and community oriented, inclined toward activism and toward putting the needs of many ahead of one's own. Ra Uru Hu refers to the fourth position in this sequence when illustrating distraction, saying "If your perspective is going to move from, let's say, a one to a four, what we call this is distraction," but the corpus here does not record him naming it. See [[Perspective: Wanting]].

### 5 Probability

The fifth view is the realist's view, and Ra Uru Hu named it. "Some people have a way of looking at the world where they see the probabilities." It is not aspirational. It reads a situation for what is likely, feasible and useful, and it is very good at moving things that can actually be moved. A chapter heading in Ra Uru Hu's advanced material puts its shadow well: probability as "The Assumption That the Future is Going to Prove the Present." See [[Perspective: Probability]].

### 6 Personal

The sixth view relates what it sees back to itself. Ra Uru Hu described his own: "I'm a six. My view, my perspective is personal." Contemporary teaching describes the mature form as treating the world as a mirror of one's own inner state, used with curiosity rather than judgement, and the immature form as self-consciousness and self-criticism, everything taken personally. See [[Perspective: Personal]].

## Distraction

Under conditioning, an arrow slips to its harmonic. At the Sun and Earth this slippage is called transference. At the nodes it has its own name. Ra Uru Hu, having described transference as a movement in a harmonic, adds: "This same pulling away with the nodes is called distraction. We're all designed to see in a certain way, but just about everybody on this planet is distracted so that they don't actually see the way they're intended to see."

The movement follows the same harmonic as everywhere else in colour: one to four and back, two to five and back, three to six and back. Applied to the six views this gives Survival and Wanting, Possibility and Probability, and [[Perspective: Power|Power]] and Personal as the three distraction pairs. Ra Uru Hu confirmed the second pair at length and the third from his own chart. He used the first pair as his opening illustration of the mechanic without naming the two views.

His definition is precise. "This harmonic movement that pulls you away from seeing the way you're supposed to see, this is called distraction. You have a natural perspective, a natural correct view that gets distracted." It is not that a distracted person sees nothing. It is that they are convinced they are seeing the wrong one of the two.

The Possibility and Probability pair is where he made this vivid. "The person that's supposed to see possibilities thinks that they see probabilities. And the person who is supposed to see probabilities thinks that all of those are possibilities. They get very very confused." The person built to see possibility, whose whole gift is registering that potentials exist, starts betting on them as though they were odds, "and because you think you see probability here and there and over there that you do dumb things." The person built to see probability starts treating every likely thing as an open horizon.

Distraction is triggered by the same thing everything else in Variable is triggered by, which is mind making the decisions. Ra Uru Hu: "if you're not correct and you're not operating according to your strategy and authority and you're making decisions with your mind instead of having the perspective you're supposed to have, you get the harmonic. You get distracted. You get distracted by what you're not." See also [[Conditioning]] and [[Not-Self]].

## Environment, Perspective and the Saturn Return

Ra Uru Hu placed the environmental and perspective transformations in a specific window of life. In an idealised upbringing, the only requirements before the Saturn return at roughly thirty are that the child is treated correctly, lives by its own [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]], and follows its dietary regimen. Then: "It's at 30 at the Saturn return. This is when the idealized upbringing takes on the demand of environment. It's between 30 and 50 that you establish through the environment its partner perspective and in that develop the foundation for awareness."

The phrase "its partner perspective" is the important one. Environment and Perspective are not two topics that happen to sit near each other on the chart. They are the two ends of the same nodal axis, and Ra Uru Hu's advanced curriculum carries a chapter heading that says so outright, that environment and perspective naturally arrive at the Saturn return. Almost nobody arrives there on schedule, since almost nobody was raised that way, and cellular deconditioning alone is a seven year process. The window describes the mechanism, not a deadline.

## Why Perspective Feeds the Fourth Transformation

The fourth transformation is conceptualizing, the Personality Sun and Earth, the arrow of [[Motivation]] and the arrival of awareness. Perspective is what supplies it. Ra Uru Hu: "it is here in your view that you establish what your mind is going to process."

That makes the third transformation load bearing. "This third transformation, it establishes the platform upon which our mind is going to conceptualize." A distracted view hands the mind the wrong raw material, and a mind running on an incorrect motivation then builds concepts out of it. Ra Uru Hu followed the compounding all the way down: a mind without the correct motivation, thinking about something it was not supposed to be looking at.

Correct, the sequence goes the other way. "The further away you get from your mind manipulating your life, the more you can see. And out of that seeing, the richer the potential of your conceptualizing so that you can release your outer authority, which is what mind is about. Not controlling your life, but sharing your wisdom." The purpose of a correct view is not better decisions. Decisions belong to Strategy and Authority. The purpose is a mind with something true to say to other people.

## A Naming Overlap Worth Knowing

The word "wanting" names two different things in this material, and readers meet both.

Contemporary teaching calls Perspective 4 Wanting, and calls Motivation 4 Need. Ra Uru Hu used "wanting" for the Motivation slot, saying that "the motivation of the four is what it wants." So a reader who encounters Ra Uru Hu on wanting is usually reading about the top right arrow, while a reader who encounters a modern chart printout showing Wanting is usually reading about the bottom right one. The two are different arrows on different axes. [[Motivation: Need]] and [[Perspective: Wanting]] have separate articles here.

A related caution applies to all six Perspective names. They share vocabulary with the six Motivations, but Motivation is the force that drives a mind and Perspective is the frame that feeds it. A person can be motivated by one theme and see through another, and usually is.

## Common Misreadings

Perspective is not a talent. It is a way of registering, and whether a person is any good at what they register depends on everything else in their life, including whether they are living by [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]] at all. Contemporary teaching makes this point carefully: the view is a potential, and it has to be honed.

Perspective is also not a single arrow to be read alone. Variable is a system of sixteen configurations, and a contemporary reference lists reading one arrow in isolation among the standard errors, along with treating left as better than right and using Variable to bypass the foundations of the chart. The four arrows describe an integrated pattern of body and mind orientation.

Finally, no left and right sub-names exist for the individual Perspectives in the way that [[Determination]] has Hot and Cold Thirst or Direct and Indirect Light. The only left and right here is the arrow itself, Focused or Peripheral, applied to whichever of the six views a person carries.
