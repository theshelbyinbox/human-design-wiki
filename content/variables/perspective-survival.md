# Perspective: Survival

Survival is the first colour of Perspective, the bottom right arrow of the Variable. Where other views in this set reach for what might happen, or for who holds power, or for what a situation says about the person looking, this one goes to the pattern. Contemporary teaching describes it as seeing the world through patterns and analytics, with a strength in identifying reliable trends and information, always seeking what can be counted on. One contemporary source calls the first way of seeing investigative and names its people the analysts, those who read the trend reports and the graphs looking for what can be proven.

Perspective is one of the four arrows and belongs to the mind side of the Variable. A reference source describes the bottom right arrow this way: it describes how the mind is designed to view the environment correctly, and it belongs to the personality side of Variable. The view is not what the mind concludes. It is what the mind is given to work with. Ra Uru Hu put the point directly in his lecture on distraction: "Remember, it is here in your view that you establish what your mind is going to process."

A caution belongs at the front of this article. The name Survival for the first colour of Perspective appears only in contemporary material. In the recorded Ra Uru Hu material in this library he names four of the six views, possibility, probability, power and personal, but he does not name this one. What follows separates the two layers wherever they can be separated.

## Mechanics

Perspective is derived from the COLOUR of the Personality Nodes. Colour is the layer of the substructure directly beneath the gate and the line, and there are six colours for any line, with six tones beneath each colour. Survival is colour 1, the first in the sequence of six: Survival, Possibility, Power, Wanting, Probability, Personal.

The colour supplies the category. It does not supply the arrow's direction. That comes from tone, which sits below colour. Ra Uru Hu: "It is tone that establishes whether something is to the left or something is to the right." Tones 1 to 3 send the arrow left, tones 4 to 6 send it right. For the Perspective arrow, left is focused and right is peripheral. A chart carrying colour 1 here can therefore be either focused or peripheral, and the colour itself decides nothing about that. No left and right variant names exist for the six Perspective values in the source material, so a focused Survival and a peripheral Survival have no distinct names of their own.

Left and right are not a ranking. As one reference source states plainly of the arrow orientations, these are not value judgments.

## The Name, and What Ra Actually Said

Ra Uru Hu's own vocabulary for the six views is only partly recorded here. Possibility and probability are named and worked through at length, and in the Ra.TV lecture "Distraction The Great Deluder" he speaks of his own chart: "I'm a six. My view, my perspective is personal, but the distraction is a view to power." That accounts for four of the six. Colour 1 is not given a name in those passages.

There is one adjacent remark. Discussing how colours resonate with profile lines of the same number, Ra says of the third and fourth colours that they push toward mental resolutions, "in the same way that first and second lines, because of their coloring, are going to have a survival orientation." This is a comment about colours 1 and 2 generally, in resonance with lines 1 and 2, rather than a label for the Perspective slot. It is the closest thing in the corpus to a source for the contemporary name.

It is worth noting how Ra used the word survival elsewhere, because it is not flattering. Opening the same distraction lecture, he contrasts the seven centered past with the nine centered present: "We are not about survival. We are about awareness." Read against that, the contemporary label points at a mode of attention, the search for what holds and what can be relied on, and not at a life purpose.

## What This Way of Seeing Attends To

The descriptive material for this colour comes from contemporary teaching.

The attention goes to regularity. It is a view that asks whether a thing has behaved this way before, whether the shape being claimed is actually there in the record, and what in the picture can be verified. The stock market analyst is the illustration given: the person poring over trend reports and graphs, reading patterns in order to predict where the thing will go next. The same source is careful that this is a potential rather than a guarantee. It does not mean that a person will be good at it, and it does not mean the talent will be used well. It is a skill to be honed, and it operates as a gift only when the person is living by strategy and authority.

## Distraction, the Nodal Name for Transference

Under conditioning, a colour slips to its harmonic. On the Sun and Earth side this slippage is called transference. On the nodes it has its own name. Ra Uru Hu, speaking about environment and the personality nodes: "it's color and it transfers at the nodal level. We call it distraction."

The harmonic for colour 1 is colour 4, and Ra used exactly this pair when he introduced the mechanic: "If your perspective is going to move from, let's say, a one to a four, what we call this is distraction. This harmonic movement, because your frequency is not correct, this harmonic movement that pulls you away from seeing the way you're supposed to see, this is called distraction." The distraction partner of Survival is therefore [[Perspective: Wanting]]. Ra spells out the content of only one of the three Perspective pairs, possibility and probability; the one to four case is named as the illustration of the mechanic rather than described in detail, so what a distracted Survival view actually looks like is not set out in the corpus.

One naming trap sits on top of this. Ra uses the word wanting for colour 4 of Motivation, saying that the motivation of the four is what it wants. Contemporary teaching calls Motivation 4 "Need" and reserves "Wanting" for Perspective 4. The same word therefore names two different things depending on which arrow is under discussion.

Distraction is also not the thing to chase. Ra's Post Graduate Rave Psychology carries the section headings "More Important to Pay Attention to Environment Than Distraction" and "Distraction Gives Us Balance", which points the remedy back down the chain rather than at the symptom.

## Correct Seeing Depends on Correct Environment

Perspective is the third of the four transformations and the first of the two mental ones, and it cannot be reached out of order. Ra Uru Hu: "if the mind does not have access to correct perspective, then the mind can never, ever, ever, ever, ever be correct. And you cannot have the correct perspective if you're not in the right place." The prerequisite is the [[Environment]] arrow, drawn from the Design Nodes, and before that the dietary regimen.

Set positively: "There is a huge difference in your life when you are environmentally correct because it automatically establishes what color brings to the personality nodes, perspective, view, the way of seeing that is absolutely correct for you and it is your color." And more briefly, "Because if you're in the correct environment, then you're going to see what you're here to see." Post Graduate Rave Psychology puts a section heading on the same idea: "Perspective Begins with the Surrender to Environment."

None of this bypasses the entry point. Variable follows type, [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]]; those are the catalyst, not an optional first step.

## Resonance with Line 1 and Harmony with Line 4

Colour numbers echo profile line numbers. Ra Uru Hu, on the substructure: "the first line and the fourth line have what is called a harmonic relationship. The two and the five, the three and the six." He then carries the rule downward: "If we take this down to the color level, the same thing applies."

So colour 1 resonates with [[line 1]] and harmonises with [[line 4]], which is the same pairing that produces the distraction described above. A person carrying colour 1 on the Personality Nodes alongside a first line in the [[Profiles & Lines|profile]] receives a reinforcement of the same theme, the one Ra called a survival orientation. The corpus does not describe how that combination differs in practice from colour 1 in a chart with any other profile line, so that comparison is left open here.
