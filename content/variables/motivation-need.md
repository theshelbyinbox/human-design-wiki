# Motivation: Need

Need is the fourth of the six Motivations, the colour that sits beneath the Personality Sun and Earth and tints everything the mind builds on top of them. Motivation is not what a person would say drives them if asked. It is the frequency underneath the line, and Ra Uru Hu reached for the theatre to describe it: the actor who reads the script and turns to the director to ask what my motivation is. "When we look at color on the personality side it's called motivation," he said. "Motivation it is the underlying background frequency that that drives the surface."

Of the six Motivations, Need is the one whose name has caused the most trouble. Ra did not call it Need. He called what the fourth colour does wanting, and in contemporary teaching the word Wanting now belongs to a different arrow entirely. Anyone reading widely will meet both usages, often in the same week, and it is worth getting the two apart before going further.

What follows separates what Ra said about the fourth colour from what later teaching has built around the name Need. The two are not in flat contradiction, but Ra's own recorded treatment of this colour is unusually thin.

## Mechanics

Motivation is the COLOUR of the Personality Sun and Earth. It is the top right arrow of the four, the second of the two arrows on the mind side of the chart, and contemporary reference material labels it Mind Style. The arrow points left for Strategic or right for Receptive. Which way it points is not decided by the colour at all but by the TONE sitting one layer below it, so a Need Motivation can be either a strategic mind or a receptive one.

Need is colour 4 in the canonical sequence: Fear, Hope, Desire, Need, Guilt, Innocence. Being fourth places it in the upper half of the six, on the harmonic arm that runs back to colour 1.

Several of the Motivations carry a binary in Ra's material, a pair of opposed expressions the colour moves between. Colour 1 runs communalist to separatist, colour 3 runs follower to leader, colour 5 runs conditioner to one who accepts conditioning. No such pair is recorded for colour 4 anywhere in this library. Sources that offer one for Need are supplying something Ra did not.

Like all of Variable, the arrow depends on an accurate birth time. Small differences can flip its orientation.

## The Naming Trap

Ra's word for the fourth Motivation colour is wanting. Speaking of a colour 1 Motivation that has slipped into its harmonic, he said: "the motivation of the four is what it wants."

Contemporary teaching renamed this colour Need, and assigned the name Wanting to the fourth PERSPECTIVE instead, the bottom right arrow derived from the Personality Nodes. So the same word now names two different mechanics in two different arrows. A reader who encounters Wanting in one source and Need in another may be looking at the same thing, or at two unrelated things, depending entirely on which arrow is under discussion.

The two contemporary descriptions also sound alike. Contemporary teaching describes Perspective 4 as the view that sees the needs of others and of the many, leaning toward activism and toward putting other people's needs ahead of one's own. That is very near the language used for Need Motivation. They remain separate mechanics from separate planetary positions, and a person can have one without the other. See [[Perspective: Wanting]] for that arrow.

## Wanting Without Knowing What It Wants

Ra's fullest surviving description of the fourth colour in this library does not come from a lecture on Need. It comes inside a passage about transference, where he is describing what happens to a colour 1 Motivation that has gone wrong and slid into its harmonic.

"The true motivation is backed by the tone and the sense underneath. It's backed by your ability to really be able to conceptualize and assess things. But the moment that you're in transference, you lose that and what's the four interested in. You know, the motivation of the four is what it wants. It wants something. And because it's not backed by total intelligence, it does not do it very well. It doesn't really know. So, it's motivated to want without knowing what it wants. And then you get a mind that gets very, very, very very confused."

Two things need holding apart here. The naming is general: the fourth colour wants, and that wanting is its defining quality. The impairment is specific to transference: the not knowing, the doing it badly, the confusion all follow from having lost the cognitive backing underneath. Ra is describing the state of a mind that has arrived at the four from outside it, having "lost all your cognitive values" on the way. He does not say in this passage that a correctly operating fourth colour is confused about what it wants. The corpus does not record him describing a correct fourth colour at length at all, which is a real gap rather than an oversight in the reading.

## Assessing the Need

The working description of Need comes almost entirely from contemporary teaching, which frames it as oriented outward to what other people and the surrounding community require. One contemporary account puts it as assessing the needs of others and saying this is what we need, or this is what is needed right now, and here is the next step to get it. Another summarises it as motivated by assessing and meeting the needs of others and the community.

Contemporary teaching connects this to the community theme of the fourth line, which it describes as the tribe, the social line. In this framing, Need is not lack. It is an orientation toward the present requirement in front of the group, and one account describes the effect of getting it right as everything else falling away once the attention rests on the need of the moment.

None of this appears in Ra's recorded material in this library. It is coherent with the harmonic structure and with the fourth line's known themes, but it should be read as later teaching rather than as Ra's.

## Transference with Fear

Transference is the harmonic slippage that conditioning produces in Motivation. Ra: "Transference is a movement in in a harmonic. The one to the four, the two to the five, the three to the six, or the other way around. In a sense, one's motivation is always being pulled away. It's always being pulled away, being pulled into transference by conditioning."

Need and [[Motivation: Fear|Fear]] are therefore a pair, and the traffic runs both ways. A colour 1 Motivation under conditioning transfers into the four, which is the passage quoted above. A colour 4 Motivation under conditioning transfers into the one, into security, foundation building and risk mitigation.

Contemporary teaching treats the transferred state as a signpost rather than a diagnosis. One account from someone with Need Motivation describes the tell as catching herself thinking she has to have something done or it might not happen, or that she had better have it planned far enough in advance to feel safe, and recognising that this never feels right and usually means she is operating from the not self. The mechanics do not offer a way to stop the pull. They offer a way to notice it.

## Resonance and Harmony

Colour relates to line by number. Ra: "if you have a first line and you have a first color, they're in resonance. In other words, how you're going to operate on the surface is going to resonate deeply to the motivation underneath. If you have a first color and you have a fourth line, that's a harmony."

Applied to the fourth colour, Need resonates with line 4 and harmonises with line 1. Resonance means the surface behaviour of a fourth line and the motivation underneath run in the same key. Harmony means a first line is met agreeably rather than dissonantly by a fourth colour beneath it. A colour that meets neither, sitting under a second, third, fifth or sixth line, produces what Ra called dissonance, a process he described as much more uncomfortable. The same 1 and 4 pairing shows up in profile structure, where 1/4 and 4/1 are the transition profiles, classed as harmonies. See [[Lines Overview]] and [[Profiles & Lines]].

## The Prerequisite

Variable is not a shortcut. Ra placed the Personality Sun and Earth at the end of the sequence of transformations, calling it "the final step" and saying that here "is all about conceptualizing." It arrives after correct diet, after correct environment, after correct perspective. Contemporary reference material is blunt about the same point: Variable does not override the foundations of the chart and does not function correctly without Strategy and Authority.

That ordering matters for Need. Knowing the fourth colour sits under your Personality Sun changes nothing on its own, because the colour is not something the mind can operate. It is captured, not chosen. What can change is whether the vehicle carrying it runs correctly, and that is decided by [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]], by [[Deconditioning]] over time, and by the arrows lower in the chain being honoured first. Contemporary reference on the personality side notes that the correct order for Variable is Perspective first and Mind Style second, even though the standard chart display shows them the other way around.
