# Strategy Overview

In [[Human Design System]], Strategy is the type-specific guideline that describes how each energy type is designed to engage with life, decisions, and opportunities. It is derived entirely from [[Energy Types|type]], it never changes, and together with [[Inner Authority Overview|Inner Authority]] it forms the practical core of the system. It answers the first question, before any decision is on the table: how is this particular body designed to meet the world at all.

## Strategy Is a Description, Not a Rule

The most common misreading of Strategy is to treat it as a behavioural instruction, advice about how to act politely or effectively. Strategy is instead a description of what a type's aura is already doing, written in a form a person can cooperate with.

Each of the five types carries a distinct auric frequency: the Generator's open and enveloping field, the Manifestor's closed and repelling one, the Projector's focused and absorbing one, the Reflector's sampling one. The Definitive Book of Human Design describes the aura as doing the talking for us: Generator auras provoke others to ask them so they can respond, Projector auras provoke recognition and the invitation, Manifestor auras challenge or initiate, and Reflector auras provoke others to look to them for their assessment. Strategy is the instruction that matches what the field is already provoking. The same book is explicit that this is mechanics and not belief: Strategy is not a philosophy, it rests on the way the vehicle is genetically designed to operate on every level. [[Aura Types]] covers those mechanics.

> "Your strategy aligns your life to the correct geometry, eliminates resistance and offers the great challenge of awareness to your personality."
> -- The Human Design Dictionary, Jovian Archive

Geometry is the operative word. Strategy does not make a person better; it puts them where what is genuinely theirs can reach them.

## One Strategy Per Type

There are five types and four strategies. Population figures are given as bands, because the sources do not agree on a single number.

- **[[Respond]]**, for the [[Generator]]. Wait for life to present something, then let the body answer. Generators and Manifesting Generators together are 66 to 70 percent of people: Ra Uru Hu's lectures give about 67 percent and approximately two-thirds, the Definitive Book almost 70 percent in the defined-Sacral category, one aura-type reference 66.30 percent. Pure Generators alone run around 37 to 38 percent in contemporary teaching.
- **[[Respond]] and then [[Inform]]**, for the [[Manifesting Generator]]. The only hybrid, covered below. Ra addressed this group as about a third of humanity; contemporary teaching commonly gives around 33 percent.
- **[[Inform]]**, for the [[Manifestor]]. Notify those who will be impacted before acting, so resistance does not close around it. Ra gave about 8 percent in one lecture and about 9 percent in another; an aura-types breakdown gives 9.7 percent. The wiki publishes around 9 percent, honest range roughly 8 to 11.5 percent.
- **[[Wait for the Invitation]]**, for the [[Projector]]. Wait for recognition, then a formal invitation, before entering the significant things. Sources run approximately 20 to 22 percent; Ra's lectures and the Definitive Book sit at about 20.
- **[[Wait the Lunar Cycle]]**, for the [[Reflector]]. Wait a full cycle of the Moon through all 64 gates before a major decision. The wiki publishes approximately 1 to 1.4 percent, following the Definitive Book at approximately 1 percent and Jovian Archive's figure of around 1.3 or 1.4 percent.

That cycle length is a spread, not a disagreement: 28 days in the Definitive Book and in the Newcomer's Guide by Lynda Bunnell and Ruth Brennan, 28.5 and 29.5 in two Jovian Archive Human Design Dictionary entries. Part of the gap is astronomy, and the teaching is identical in all of them. See [[Lunar Authority]].

### The Manifesting Generator Hybrid

The Manifesting Generator is a Generator with a motor connected to the Throat, so the body can act on a response almost instantly. The core strategy is unchanged: Respond, exactly as for any Generator. Informing is a second step added because of that speed, not a second way of starting things. A Manifesting Generator who informs but has not responded is simply initiating. Ra was direct about the cost:

> "And a manifesting generator, instead of waiting to respond, tries to play out the role of the manifestor and meets in resistance an anger that doesn't belong to their form."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

That borrowed anger is why the Manifesting Generator's not-self theme is given as frustration, with anger attached when informing has been skipped.

### The Manifestor Child

Ra taught that the Manifestor is the only type whose strategy has two versions, split by circumstance rather than by chart:

> "only manifestors have two variations of what is, in essence, their strategy. The manifestor child within the house of its parents always needing to ask permission."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

The adult version is informing. Asking permission is not a softer form of it: it is how a Manifestor child discovers that its power does not have to be taken by force. [[Inform]] covers this in full.

### What the Waiting Actually Is

Three of the four strategies contain the word wait, and it carries different weight in each. Ra laid them on one scale:

> "It is about being ready to respond. It is a little more formal, obviously, in the projector in that they are waiting for the invitation. But everything about the reflector is about time."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

A Generator is barely waiting at all, since there is almost always something present to respond to. A Projector waits for something formal, which can take time. A Reflector waits on a clock that belongs to the Moon. None of the three describes inactivity: the waiting concerns the moment of decision, not the rest of a life.

## Strategy Is Not Authority

This is the distinction that most often collapses, and the two tools do different jobs.

Strategy operates at the level of type. Everyone of a given type has the same one, and it governs entry: whether an opportunity is correctly reaching this person at all, and how. Authority operates at the level of individual configuration, set by which centers are defined, and it governs commitment: whether this thing, now correctly in front of the person, is a yes or a no.

So two Generators share a strategy and can still decide in completely different ways. Roughly half of all people carry a defined Solar Plexus -- Ra gave approximately 51 percent -- and therefore an authority that unfolds over time rather than in the moment. One Generator responds and knows immediately. Another responds, feels the same pull, and has to wait out an emotional wave before the answer is trustworthy. The strategy did its job identically for both; only the authority differs.

There are therefore two ways to get this wrong. Initiating without the aura's cue jumps the strategy: the thing was never correctly arriving. Committing in the moment of engagement when the authority needs time jumps the authority: the thing arrived correctly and was answered too fast. The Definitive Book puts the two in sequence, noting that following our Strategy is what connects us to our own Authority.

How each authority works belongs to [[Inner Authority Overview]] and its twelve articles, including [[Emotional Authority]], [[Sacral Authority]] and [[Splenic Authority]]. [[Making Decisions in Practice]] covers the two tools running together.

## What Strategy Replaces

Strategy replaces the mind as the decision-maker. That is the whole of it.

> "It's about decision-making. This is the whole thing to really grasp about strategy and authority: it's about making a decision. It isn't about anything else."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

The mind is not being asked to stop. Ra's position was that it cannot stop and should not be expected to: the mind measures, and that is its proper work. The failure is in mistaking the measurement for a decision.

> "Decision making is not the same as thinking about things. To make a decision is to make a decision. It isn't to ponder it."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

Mental decision-making fails for a mechanical reason rather than a moral one. The mind does not reason from a neutral place. It reasons from the [[Conditioning|conditioned]] openness of the [[Open Centers|open and undefined centers]], which generates the fears and compulsions of the [[Not-Self]]: the need to know, to be loved, to feel secure, to prove strength. Those pressures are not the person's own design; they are what was absorbed from everyone they ever sat beside. A decision built on them is correct for the conditioning and wrong for the body carrying it.

Strategy offers instead a decision anchored in something that cannot be conditioned: the mechanical behaviour of the aura, read through the body. The mind is not demoted so much as relieved. Freed from deciding, it becomes what the Definitive Book calls an outer authority, a valued witness of a life and a genuine resource for other people.

## What the Experiment Asks

Human Design asks for an experiment, not agreement.

> "Human design offers anyone who's interested an experiment. An experiment that you try yourself. The experiment of following the strategy and the authority of your type."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

Practically, that means applying Strategy to real decisions, the small ones especially, and watching what changes. Nothing has to be believed in advance.

The signal is the [[Signature]] against the not-self theme: satisfaction against frustration for Generators and Manifesting Generators, peace against anger for Manifestors, success against bitterness for Projectors, surprise against disappointment for Reflectors. Ra was careful that this is a ratio, not a destination.

> "More satisfaction than frustration. More success than bitterness. More peace than anger. More surprise than disappointment."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

He described it as a seesaw, and the tilt as the signpost that a life is in transformation. Over a longer horizon the experiment becomes [[Deconditioning]], which Ra placed at roughly seven years, the span associated with the body's cellular renewal. Those seven years are an outcome, not an entry fee. The experiment starts with the next decision.
