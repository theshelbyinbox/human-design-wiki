# Variables & PHS

Variable is the layer of Human Design beneath Type, Strategy, Authority and Profile. It appears as four arrows at the top of the BodyGraph, and it describes how a specific body is designed to take in nourishment and information, where that body operates correctly, how the mind is designed to see, and what colours the mind's thinking underneath.

It is read from the substructure. Colour gives the four arrow categories; tone gives cognition and decides which way each arrow points. Because it sits that deep, it depends on an accurate birth time.

Variable is not a shortcut past the basics. Ra Uru Hu treated Strategy and Authority as the catalyst that has to come first, and the whole of Variable as a long process rather than a set of labels.
