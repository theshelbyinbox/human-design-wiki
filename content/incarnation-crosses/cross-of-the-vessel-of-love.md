# Cross of the Vessel of Love

The Cross of the Vessel of Love sits at the midpoint of every quarter, forming the literal center of the wheel. Gate 25 (Innocence) and Gate 46 (Pushing Upward) form the love-of-self axis; Gate 10 (Treading/Behavior) and Gate 15 (Modesty) supply the behavioral expression and range. Carriers are here to love the self so completely that it becomes love for all -- not as sentiment but as behavior, demonstrating through their lived example that the love of self and the love of the universal are the same movement.

**Gates 10, 15, 25, 46**
