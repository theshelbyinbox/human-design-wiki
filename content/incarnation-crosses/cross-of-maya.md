# Cross of Maya

The Cross of Maya moves through completion and transition, carrying the intelligence of cycles. Gate 61 (Inner Truth) and Gate 62 (Detail) form the truth-and-form axis; Gate 32 (Duration) and Gate 42 (Increase) supply the continuity and growth. Carriers understand the duration of things -- how long transformation actually takes, when cycles close, and what inner truth persists beneath the surface of change that most people never perceive.

**Gates 32, 42, 61, 62**
