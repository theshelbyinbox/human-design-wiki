# Cross of Planning

The Cross of Planning was the carrier frequency of the 400-year tribal era -- the organizational intelligence that built community through the bargain between feeling and provision. Gate 37 (The Family) and Gate 40 (Deliverance) form the bargain axis; Gate 9 (Focus) and Gate 16 (Enthusiasm) supply the concentration and skill. Carriers are here to structure collective life and to experience deep satisfaction in seeing a community they helped build function with sustained coherence.

**Gates 9, 16, 37, 40**
