# Cross of the Sleeping Phoenix

The Cross of the Sleeping Phoenix is the cross of individual mutation through chemistry. Gate 55 (Spirit) and Gate 59 (Sexuality) form the mutation axis at the heart of the individual circuit; Gate 20 (Metamorphosis) and Gate 34 (Power) supply the expression and force. Carriers hold a sleeping potential that awakens when crisis or the right chemical field activates it. They are not here to initiate transformation through effort but to be the conduit through which collective mutation flows when its time has come.

**Gates 20, 34, 55, 59**
