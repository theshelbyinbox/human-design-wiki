# Cross of Explanation

The Cross of Explanation carries the frequency of individual breakthrough becoming collectively comprehensible -- the translation of mental mutation into a form others can grasp and use. Gate 49 (Revolution) and Gate 4 (Youthful Folly) drive the pressure to reform; Gate 23 (Splitting Apart) and Gate 43 (Breakthrough) supply the mechanism. Carriers are here to explain the unexplainable, to bridge the gap between individual insight and collective understanding, and to demonstrate that what appears chaotic is part of a deeper pattern.

**Gates 4, 23, 43, 49**
