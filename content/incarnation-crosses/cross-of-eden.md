# Cross of Eden

The Cross of Eden holds the tension between idealized harmony and the reality of collective emotional friction. Gate 11 (Peace) and Gate 12 (Caution) form the conceptual axis of what was and what waits; Gate 6 (Conflict) and Gate 36 (Crisis) supply the friction that forces integration. Carriers live at the threshold between memory of harmony and the crises that force growth, their purpose to metabolize collective emotional experience and transmute crisis into something the group can integrate.
