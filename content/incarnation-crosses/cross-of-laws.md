# Cross of Laws

The Cross of Laws carries the frequency of mutation from raw beginning into principled structure. Gate 50 (The Cauldron/Values) and Gate 3 (Difficulty at the Beginning) form the values-and-origins axis; Gate 56 (The Wanderer) and Gate 60 (Limitation) supply the stimulation and the boundary. Carriers are here to establish the laws, values, and limits that protect collective life -- to hold the value system that the tribe organizes itself around, and to demonstrate that limits are the condition for freedom, not its opposite.

**Gates 3, 50, 56, 60**
