# Cross of the Four Ways

The Cross of the Four Ways closes the wheel, appearing at the between-midpoints of each quarter. Gate 33 (Retreat) and Gate 19 (Approach) form the withdrawal-and-reaching axis; Gate 44 (Coming to Meet) and Gate 24 (Return) supply the alertness and the renewal. Carriers embody the full range of archetypal movement -- approach and retreat, reaching and withdrawing -- their lives a demonstration of the rhythmic pulse through which awareness cycles and connection is made, released, and made again.

**Gates 19, 24, 33, 44**
