# Cross of Penetration

The Cross of Penetration carries the individual initiative that shocks the tribal field into motion. Gate 51 (Arousal/Shock) and Gate 57 (The Gentle/Penetrating) form the initiating axis; Gate 53 (Development) and Gate 54 (The Marrying Maiden/Ambition) supply the developmental drive. Carriers are first movers by nature -- here to penetrate resistance, to initiate what others had not imagined beginning, and to demonstrate through their actions that forward movement is possible.
