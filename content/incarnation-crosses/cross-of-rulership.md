# Cross of Rulership

The Cross of Rulership links the Heart Center's capacity for possession and leadership with the Solar Plexus's grace and the Ajna's clarity. Gate 45 (Gathering Together) and Gate 26 (The Egoist) form the leadership axis; Gate 22 (Openness) and Gate 47 (Realization) supply the emotional grace and mental resolution. Carriers are designed to lead through alchemy -- the art of transforming social power into wisdom the group can actually use.

**Gates 22, 26, 45, 47**
