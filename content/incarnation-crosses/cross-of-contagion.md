# Cross of Contagion

The Cross of Contagion spreads desire and emotional resonance as a collective force. Gate 30 (Recognition of Feelings) and Gate 29 (Perseverance) form the emotional and commitment axis; Gate 8 (Holding Together) and Gate 14 (Possession in Great Measure) supply the power and coherence. Carriers do not persuade -- they commit to what draws them and find that their commitment ignites response in others like fire, organizing groups around shared feeling and purpose.
