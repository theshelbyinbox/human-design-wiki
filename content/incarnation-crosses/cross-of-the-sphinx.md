# Cross of the Sphinx

The Cross of the Sphinx is the directional cross of the BodyGraph, formed by the four gates that open and close each quarter of the wheel: Gate 1 (The Creative), Gate 2 (The Receptive), Gate 7 (The Army), and Gate 13 (Fellowship of Man). Carriers are the navigators of human experience, here to distill the wisdom of the past into orientation for the future. Their gift is not leadership through force but through precision of direction -- the abstract knowing of where humanity has been and where it is going.

**Gates 1, 2, 7, 13**
