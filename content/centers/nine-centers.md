# Nine Centers

The Nine Centers are the geometric shapes that form the core of the [[Human Design System]] BodyGraph. They correspond loosely to the Hindu chakra system but differ in number, function, and arrangement. Human Design holds that humanity shifted from a seven-centered to a nine-centered configuration in 1781, when the heart chakra of the older system split into the [[G Center]] and the [[Heart Center]], and the solar plexus chakra split into the [[Solar Plexus Center]] and the [[Splenic Center]].

## Overview

The nine centers are arranged in a diamond-shaped diagram, and each governs specific themes, biological correlates, and functions. They connect through 36 channels, composed of paired gates drawn from the 64 hexagrams of the I Ching. See [[Channels (36)]] and [[Gates (64)]].

Each center is either defined (colored) or undefined (white) in a person's chart. Defined centers generate consistent energy and represent fixed, reliable qualities. Undefined centers receive and amplify energy from others and the planetary transit field. The configuration of the nine determines energy type and [[Inner Authority]].

## What a Center Is, Mechanically

A center is a hub in the body's energy circuitry where a kind of energy, awareness, or pressure is concentrated and processed. Definition is never a property of one center alone, since a center lights up only when a complete channel joins it to a neighbor.

**Defined centers.** When two gates at both ends of a channel are activated together, they complete that channel and define the two centers it joins. Gates 7 and 31 form the Channel of The Alpha and define the G and the [[Throat Center]]. A defined center is a hub of consistent, reliable energy that works the same way regardless of who is in the room, and it radiates through the aura, conditioning everyone nearby who has that center white. A single gate activation inside a defined center is a hanging gate: it has no channel partner, but because the center is lit it dependably contributes its theme to the definition.

**Undefined centers.** An undefined center is white but carries one or more gate activations. These are dormant gates, alone in their channel, waiting to be sparked by an electromagnetic connection from the gate at the other end, supplied by another person or a transit. The dormant gate filters and directs the incoming energy, making a person more aware of its presence within them.

**Completely open centers.** A completely open center is white with no gate activations at all, so there is nothing to hold onto and no familiar theme with which to catch the energy. Undefined and open are used interchangeably in everyday practice; the load-bearing distinction is between defined and not-defined.

## The Four Functional Groupings

The nine centers sort into four functional families. Two centers belong to more than one family, which is the point: those centers do two jobs at once.

**Pressure centers: [[Head Center]] and [[Root Center]].** These sit at the top and bottom of the BodyGraph and squeeze the whole system between them. The crucial difference is that the Root is a motor and the Head is not. The Head is pressure and nothing more, which is why mental pressure produces anxiety rather than action.

**Motor centers: [[Sacral Center]], Root, [[Solar Plexus Center]], and [[Heart Center]].** These are the four energy sources that power activity: the Sacral as creative life force, the Root as the drive to get things done, the Solar Plexus as motivation through emotional experience, and the Heart as willpower. A motor is what makes something in a chart capable of doing rather than merely knowing.

**Awareness centers: [[Splenic Center]], [[Ajna Center]], and Solar Plexus.** These are the three centers through which conscious life experience is interpreted; the other six operate mechanically, and they mark successive evolutionary phases. The Spleen is body awareness and the oldest, described as roughly four million years old, its keynote reducing to survival recognition. The Ajna is mental awareness, the self-reflective consciousness that emerged around ninety thousand years ago. The Solar Plexus is the newest and still developing, its awareness masked by the power of its motor. All three are built on fear, and twenty of the 64 gates are fear gates. They run on different clocks: the Spleen is quiet and speaks in the now, once; the Ajna is noisy and processes across all time; the Solar Plexus is loud and moves in a wave. The splenic voice is easily drowned by the other two.

**The center of manifestation: the Throat.** The Throat is the bridge from internal to external, where energy becomes language and action, and everything in the chart eventually seeks expression through it. With eleven gates it is the most complex center in the BodyGraph, and whether a motor connects to it is the most consequential structural fact in a chart.

**The center of identity: the G.** The G is neither motor, pressure, nor awareness. It houses the magnetic monopole, which holds the design and personality crystals together and pulls a person through space. It is not an authority and is not part of what makes type, which surprises people who assume the center of self must be the center of decision.

The groupings explain flow: energy is generated by motors, pressed by pressure centers, filtered through the awareness centers, and given a voice at the Throat.

## The Nine Centers

### Head Center (Crown)
Located at the top of the BodyGraph. A pressure center. Governs inspiration, questions, and the drive to make sense of existence. Biologically associated with the pineal gland. The Head generates mental pressure -- the compulsion to think, to understand, to answer. This pressure is not the mind's authority; it is simply the engine of mental activity.

### Ajna Center
Located below the Head, connected to it and to the Throat. An awareness center. Governs conceptualization, mental processing, and the formation of opinions and beliefs. Biologically associated with the pituitary gland and prefrontal cortex. The Ajna takes inspiration from the Head and processes it into concepts. A defined Ajna has a consistent, fixed way of thinking; an undefined Ajna thinks in many different ways depending on conditioning.

### Throat Center
Located in the upper center of the BodyGraph. Governs manifestation and communication. The only center that has no direct equivalent in the traditional chakra system (as a motor/manifestation center). All energy in the BodyGraph eventually seeks expression through the Throat. When a motor center is directly connected to the Throat, the type becomes a [[Manifestor]] or [[Manifesting Generator]].

### G Center (Identity / Self)
Located in the center of the BodyGraph, shaped like a diamond. Governs identity, love, and direction in life. Contains the magnetic monopole, described as the force that holds together the two crystals of consciousness (personality and design) within the body. Biologically associated with the blood and liver. A defined G Center gives a consistent sense of identity and direction; an undefined G Center seeks identity through environment and relationships.

### Heart Center (Ego / Will)
Located to the upper right of the G Center. A motor center. Governs willpower, ego, the material world, promises, and self-worth. Biologically associated with the heart, stomach, gall bladder, and thymus. Only about 30 percent of people have a defined Heart Center. When defined, it provides consistent willpower but also carries the pressure to prove, compete, and achieve in material terms. It is the smallest center, and Ra Uru Hu called the assumption that willpower is available to everyone on demand the big lie.

### Solar Plexus Center
Located to the right of the BodyGraph. Both a motor and an awareness center -- the only center that is both. Governs emotions, feelings, and the wave pattern of emotional experience. Biologically associated with the kidneys, pancreas, lungs, and nervous system. Ra put emotional definition at 51 percent of humanity and the unemotional at 49 percent. The Solar Plexus is described as the center of a future awareness mutation -- the emerging emotional consciousness of humanity, tied to [[Gate 55]] and the close of the current global cycle in 2027. Its rule is that there is no truth in the now.

### Sacral Center
Located in the lower center of the BodyGraph. A motor center. Governs life force, sexuality, fertility, creativity, and work capacity. Biologically associated with the sexual organs and digestive system. Defined only in [[Generator]] and [[Manifesting Generator]] types (approximately 70 percent of people). The Sacral is described as the most powerful motor in the BodyGraph and the source of the generative energy that sustains life on the planet.

### Splenic Center
Located to the left of the BodyGraph. An awareness center. Governs intuition, instinct, immune function, and in-the-now awareness. Biologically associated with the spleen, lymphatic system, and T cells. The oldest awareness center in evolutionary terms, carrying the accumulated survival wisdom of the species. Contains seven fear gates, each associated with a specific survival concern.

### Root Center
Located at the bottom of the BodyGraph. A motor and pressure center. Governs adrenaline, stress, the drive to complete, and physical pressure. Biologically associated with the adrenal glands. Approximately 68 percent of people have a defined Root Center. The Root creates pressure that drives action; the pressure is not inherently bad but becomes problematic when the mind uses it as a reason to rush into incorrect decisions.

Published prevalence figures vary by several percentage points between sources; the numbers above are approximations.

## The 1781 Mutation: From Seven Centers to Nine

Human Design dates the current human form to 1781, the year Sir William Herschel discovered Uranus. Every human born before that date was, in this account, a seven-centered being; every human born since has been nine-centered. The seven-centered being is the one described by the Hindu Brahmans and their chakra system, and it dominated the planet for roughly eighty-five to ninety-five thousand years, building the civilizations, philosophies, and moralities we still live inside. Its defining quality was that it was purely strategic: it fixed on one thing, stored it along a single line of memory, and triumphed over its environment by narrowing attention.

**What split.** The nine-centered configuration is not the old one with two shapes bolted on: two chakras divided. The heart chakra split into the G Center and the Heart or Ego Center, separating identity, love, and direction from will, worth, and the material plane. The solar plexus chakra split into the Solar Plexus and the Splenic Center, separating emotional awareness from instinctual, immune, bodily awareness. This is why the Heart Center sits where the physical heart sits and has nothing to do with love, while the G sits where the traditional heart chakra sits. Some overview material instead describes the Ajna and Solar Plexus as the two new awareness centers; the split account above is the one the primary sources give.

**What mutated.** Alongside the split came a mutation in the visual cortex, which Ra located in the Ajna Center and called a change in the way the vision recording system works. Seven-centered binocular vision was honed to a specific focus, and that focus determined how information was stored. The mutation removed the requirement to focus, opening peripheral or receptive vision. The result is a being that processes in two directions at once: the old strategic left, which narrows and remembers along a line, and the emerging receptive right, which takes everything in.

**A different vehicle.** The seven-centered body ran on a Saturnian cycle of roughly thirty years, its midpoint at fifteen. The nine-centered body is Uranian, built on an eighty-four-year cycle, with maturity beginning between thirty-eight and forty-four.

**What it means for consciousness.** The result is a species living inside a form it does not understand, running an operating manual written for a different one. The nine-centered being is a transitional form, which Ra called Homo sapiens in transitus. The implication runs through the whole system: the mind's decision-making role suited the old form, while the nine-centered body carries its own compass, and the mind's job changed to becoming a source of wisdom for others. The mind has not noticed. See [[Mind vs Body Decision-Making]].

## How Centers Determine Type

Type is a direct readout of the center configuration, turning on two questions asked in order. The first is whether a motor is connected to the Throat. That connection means the person can initiate, because energy reaches speech and action without waiting; it can run through any of the four motors, and it can be indirect. The second is whether the Sacral is defined, which splits the [[Generator]] family from everyone else, because the Sacral is both the response mechanism and the sustainable work engine.

- Defined Sacral (without motor-to-throat connection): Generator
- Defined Sacral with motor-to-throat: Manifesting Generator
- Motor to throat without Sacral: Manifestor
- No Sacral, no motor to throat: Projector
- No defined centers: Reflector

A [[Projector]] has definition somewhere but no Sacral and no motor reaching the Throat, which is why it waits to be recognized and invited. A [[Reflector]] has all nine centers undefined and is not more vulnerable to conditioning than other types; Reflectors are described as the most resilient, protected by an aura that resists and samples rather than absorbs.

## How Centers Determine Inner Authority

[[Inner Authority]] is also read straight off the centers, using a strict hierarchy that runs from the slowest and most overriding mechanism to the fastest. Each rung is available only when everything above it is absent.

- **[[Emotional Authority]]**, first. A defined Solar Plexus is automatically the inner authority regardless of type, because the emotional wave colors every other perception until it settles.
- **[[Sacral Authority]]**, for a defined Sacral with an undefined Solar Plexus. The gut response is immediate and mechanical.
- **[[Splenic Authority]]**, requiring a defined Spleen with both Sacral and Solar Plexus undefined. A Generator therefore cannot have splenic authority, and neither can anyone emotionally defined.
- **[[Ego Authority]]**, when the Heart is defined and none of the above are. It splits into an ego-manifested form, where the voice leads, and a rarer ego-projected form.
- **[[Self-Projected Authority]]**, when the G is defined to the Throat with no motor definition below. Decisions become audible when the person hears themselves speak. Ra put this at roughly two and a half percent of people.
- **[[Mental Authority]]** and environmental authority, where there is no definition below the Throat. These decide by talking a decision through with trusted people who withhold opinions, and by attending to the environment.
- **[[Lunar Authority]]**, for Reflectors, who wait a full lunar cycle before committing to anything significant.

See [[The Authority Hierarchy]], [[Minor Authorities]], and [[No Direct Inner Authority]].

## Defined Centers

A defined center is always on. It does not fluctuate with company, mood, or circumstance. It is a fixed frequency broadcast continuously into the aura, so everyone nearby with that center white is receiving it, amplifying it, and often mistaking it for their own. This is [[Conditioning]] seen from the transmitting end.

Definition is often glossed as strength, and that gloss is wrong. Definition means consistency: a person is reliably, unchangeably a certain way there, and the theme expresses itself whether or not it is welcome. Two people with the same center defined can butt heads precisely because neither can bend. Each defined center also carries a repressive and a hyperactive distortion alongside its healthy expression: a defined Throat either communicates powerfully, bites its tongue, or talks over everyone for fear of not being heard.

**Can a defined center be conditioned?** In the strict sense, no: it cannot be switched off by another aura. But it is not therefore automatically healthy. Definition can live out a consistently low-frequency expression, and because it is consistent the distortion is constant rather than occasional. A defined Heart can feel chronically not enough. The useful distinction is between conditioning, which is acting like someone you are not and belongs to the open centers, and wounding, which is rejecting who you are and belongs to the defined ones. Open centers are cleared by being seen and released; defined centers by being restored to a high expression of themselves.

## Undefined Versus Completely Open

The difference a hanging gate makes is larger than it looks. A dormant gate gives incoming energy somewhere to land, gives it a recognizable theme, and makes the person more consciously aware of it moving through them.

A completely open center has no such handle. The full spectrum of the center's theme pours through at whatever volume the environment supplies, with no fixed way of processing it, which makes it the most extreme position in a chart in both directions. It is the deepest point of conditioning, because a person can spend decades unable to tell what any of it means or where it came from, often concluding that something is simply wrong with them. It is also the deepest potential for wisdom, because such a person comes to know that center's material in its pure state, unshaped by any personal fixation. Ra described his own completely open Sacral in those terms: distorted by conditioning early on, and then, over two decades, the whole mystery of life opening through it.

## Amplification

Amplification is the mechanism that makes openness so powerful and so often misread. When someone with a defined center steps into the aura of someone with that center white, the white center becomes active through the channel connections, but as an amplification of what is being taken in. It is not the same energy at all.

Ra used the analogy of an electric instrument: amplification makes a signal louder for others, and past a certain point it distorts. In most human beings, unaware of the mechanism, it distorts. His example was an emotionally defined son and an unemotional father: the child becomes upset, and the father becomes even more upset, because he is amplifying the child's emotional energy rather than feeling his own. Ra went as far as to say that most violence is rooted in out-of-control amplification. The design of attraction makes this constant rather than occasional: we are drawn to what we are not, so the emotional and the unemotional find each other.

This is why the amplified experience feels more intense than the original. The person with definition runs their ordinary frequency; the person amplifying it receives that frequency boosted, with no internal reference for what a normal level feels like. It is also why openness is so persuasive: an amplified emotion feels more like the truth than anything a defined center quietly produces. Awareness does not stop the amplification; it changes what it means.

## The Not-Self and the Open Centers

The [[Not-Self]] is not a mood or a character flaw. It is the sum of the open centers, operated by the mind. The not-self mind is the spokesperson for the undefined centers: it tells us what to say and do on the basis of energy that is not ours. From birth we sit in the auric fields of parents, siblings, and everyone else, and our open channels, gates, and centers are constantly filled in by their definition. By the age of seven, several layers of familial and societal conditioning have been piled over the true self, and the mind, amplifying the body's fears through that openness, has become hyper-vigilant to how others respond.

Each open center asks its own false question. Noticing the question is the entry point to [[Deconditioning]]:

- **Open Head:** Am I thinking about things that do not matter?
- **Open Ajna:** Am I trying to convince everyone that I am certain?
- **Open Throat:** Am I trying to attract attention?
- **Open G:** Am I looking for love and direction?
- **Open Heart:** Do I think I have something to prove?
- **Open Solar Plexus:** Am I avoiding confrontation and truth?
- **Open Sacral:** Do I know when enough is enough?
- **Open Spleen:** Am I holding on to what isn't good for me?
- **Open Root:** Am I in a hurry to get things done so I can be free of pressure?

## Open Centers as the Site of Wisdom

Ra's most durable metaphor for the BodyGraph is this: what is colored in is the student, and what is white is where you go to school. The open centers are not what a person is supposed to become; they are where the learning happens.

> "It is through the open centers that we achieve wisdom in this life." -- Ra Uru Hu

What turns conditioning into wisdom is not effort or analysis. It is the removal of the open centers from decision-making. As long as the mind is deciding a life on the basis of what an open center amplifies, that center is a source of suffering. Once decisions are handed to [[Strategy]] and Inner Authority, the same center shifts from a conditioning element that controls a person to a window onto the other. A healthy undefined Solar Plexus, for instance, becomes a barometer of the emotional health of others, having felt every emotional weather system without owning any of them. The condition is non-attachment: the moment a person identifies with what they amplify, the same openness produces confusion instead.

## Deconditioning and the Seven-Year Cycle

[[Deconditioning]] is the process of living by Strategy and Inner Authority long enough that the cells of the body, which regenerate over roughly seven years, are no longer organized around decisions made by the not-self mind. Ra was reluctant to name the timeframe early on, on the grounds that nobody caught up in instant gratification would want to wait for it.

What changes is what runs the life. What does not change is the chart. The open centers stay open and the mind keeps talking: a person with an open Spleen will still have a mind that thinks about survival. The not-self is not eliminated, it stops making the decisions. What emerges is an observer with enough awareness to watch what streams through the mind and not act on it.

The process is not linear and not a single pass: later cycles, particularly the Uranus opposition around forty and the Chiron return around fifty, tend to re-open material in the same centers at a deeper level. Conditioning cannot be escaped and is not inherently bad. The aim is never to become impermeable but correctly permeable.

## Reading a Chart's Center Configuration

- **Locate the definition first:** which centers are colored, and which channels produce that coloring. This is the always-on part of the person and what they broadcast.
- **Check the Sacral, then motor-to-Throat.** These give type, and type gives [[Strategy]].
- **Walk the authority hierarchy in order:** Solar Plexus, Sacral, Spleen, Heart, G to Throat, mental and environmental, lunar. The first one present is the answer.
- **Distinguish undefined from completely open in every white center,** noting the hanging gates in the white and the colored ones alike.
- **Look at how definition is distributed.** Gates bridging separate areas of definition are conditioning elements in their own right, though for triple and quadruple splits the undefined centers are usually stronger. See [[Definition Types]].
- **Read the openness relationally,** asking who in this person's life defines their open centers and what they therefore amplify daily. With an open G or Spleen especially, check the environment: the wrong place makes everything else appear broken.

## Common Misunderstandings

**"Defined is better than open."** Every primary source contradicts this. Definition is consistency, not quality; openness is receptivity, not deficiency. The undefined centers are not broken, not empty, and do not need to be fixed. A Reflector with nine open centers is not more damaged than anyone else, and someone with all nine defined is not more evolved.

**"Openness means I have no boundaries."** Openness is a filter, not a sponge. The skill is distinguishing what is yours from what you are amplifying, not preventing the amplification.

**"The mind is the problem and should be quieted."** The mind is not an enemy to be overcome, repressed, or ignored. It is a creative force whose job description changed in 1781. Freed from decision-making it becomes an instrument for thinking, dreaming, and making sense of life. The correction is not to fix the mind but to remove its authority over the life.
