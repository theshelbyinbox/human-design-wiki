# Core Concepts

The foundational ideas of Human Design -- the lens through which every other part of the system makes sense. These are the concepts you return to over and over once you begin running the experiment, because they describe what is actually happening when Strategy and Authority are working (or when they're not).

If you are new to the wiki, this is the place to start. Begin with **Human Design System** for the full picture, then follow the threads into the four concepts that explain why the experiment works the way it does.
