# Emotional Authority

Emotional Authority is the inner decision-making mechanism of anyone born with a defined [[Solar Plexus Center]], and where it exists it outranks everything else in the chart. A sacral response, a splenic hit, a movement of will: each is only information, and none becomes a decision until the emotional wave has had its time. It crosses type completely, so a [[Generator]], a [[Manifesting Generator]], a [[Manifestor]] and a [[Projector]] can all carry it. A [[Reflector]] never does, having no defined centres at all.

> "For half of humanity, there is no truth in the now. Patience and time are the only allies of the Solar Plexus motor."
> -- Jovian Archive Human Design Dictionary

## How Common Emotional Authority Is

This is the most common inner authority, and the library holds several figures rather than one. Ra Uru Hu spoke of just over 51 percent. The Definitive Book of Human Design prints 53 percent in its chapter on the centres and fifty percent in its chapter on authority. Jovian Archive's newcomer material says about 50 percent. Two chart-calculator datasets run lower: a JovianArchive.com set gives 46.97 percent and a HumanDesign.com live page gives 49.8 percent, both drawn from charts people chose to run, which makes them self-selected samples rather than censuses. The honest range is roughly 47 to 53 percent.

## The Solar Plexus as Motor and Emerging Awareness

The Solar Plexus is the only centre the dictionary lists under two headings at once: "Solar Plexus Center - Emotions - Motor Center & Awareness Center". That double nature is the whole mechanical story. It is one of the four motors, alongside the [[Sacral Center]], the [[Root Center]] and the Heart, and its motor output is the wave: a rise and fall from hope to pain and back, endlessly, whether or not anything is happening to you. Beneath the motor an awareness is developing, but it is not online yet. The centre you are asked to trust therefore runs two processes at different maturities. The loud one is chemistry; the quiet one will eventually make the Solar Plexus an awareness centre in the way the Spleen already is.

> "the most powerful authority that we have is useless in the now. Absolutely, completely useless in the now."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

## What No Truth in the Now Actually Means

The phrase is not an instruction to distrust feelings. It is a statement about position. At any instant the motor has placed you somewhere on its arc, and that position determines what you can perceive. The high does not make a thing good and the low does not make it bad; both colour everything the same shade.

> "When you're down in that wave and feeling awful, the dark side of life is all you see."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

A decision made at one point on the wave is a decision about the wave, not about the offer. This is why the Solar Plexus cannot be managed by willpower or by thinking harder. Time is the only variable that changes the reading, because only time moves you somewhere else and lets you see the same thing again from there.

One mechanic is easy to miss: the wave is broadcast, not private. An emotional being does not only live inside a wave, they put one out, and everyone in the aura takes it in.

## The Three Emotional Waves

There are three, not four.

> "There are three different kinds of emotional waves:
> 1. The Ratchet Wave: Tribal; one step forward and one step back until explosion
> 2. The Spike Wave: Individual: generally even but subject to sharp spikes
> 3. The Crash Wave: Collective Abstract: gradual rise and dramatic fall"
> -- Jovian Archive Human Design Dictionary

The same dictionary gives each a keynote elsewhere: the tribal need wave, the individual uncertainty wave and the abstract collective desire wave. Which you carry is set by which Solar Plexus channels are defined.

- **Ratchet, tribal:** [[Channel 19/49 — The Channel of Synthesis]], [[Channel 37/40 — The Channel of Community]] and [[Channel 6/59 — The Channel of Mating (Intimacy)]], all in the [[Tribal Circuit]].
- **Spike, individual:** [[Channel 12/22 — The Channel of Openness]] and [[Channel 39/55 — The Channel of Emoting]], in the [[Individual Circuit]].
- **Crash, collective abstract:** [[Channel 30/41 — The Channel of Recognition]] and [[Channel 35/36 — The Channel of Transitoriness]], in the [[Collective Circuit]].

Sources differ on 6-59. By circuitry it is tribal, and the dictionary's three-way split puts every tribal channel on the ratchet. The Definitive Book does not list it with the tribal wave at all, treating 59-6 separately as the source of all wave mechanics, since the wave is created in the 6th gate before it reaches anything else.

### The Ratchet Wave

Named for the winch that drags a car out of a ditch: pull, hold, pull, hold, tension accumulating with every pass. A remark lands and starts working, and nothing is said. Weeks or months later the partner asks something entirely innocent and the whole accumulation comes out at once, along with a grievance they have long forgotten.

> "It's a slow buildup of tension until there is finally that point where the tension is released."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

The build is not a fault. It is how a tribal emotional being arrives anywhere, and the release at the top is the correct discharge of a motor, not a lapse of composure. Ra was clear that none of this is about controlling your emotions. In his own summary, you wait until it ratchets up to the point where there is either clarity or explosion, and the difference between those two outcomes is the whole practice.

The ratchet misleads its owner by disguising duration as intensity: because the pressure took months to build, the outburst feels proportionate to whatever released it, and never is. The sources conflict on the shape. The dictionary defines the ratchet as one step forward and one step back, while Ra in a Ra.TV lecture calls it two steps forward and one step back.

### The Spike Wave

The individual wave does not look like a wave. In Ra's word it planes. Someone carrying 12-22 or 39-55 can seem the most level person in the room, sitting slightly above or below the midline for long stretches, then move without warning.

> "They don't even necessarily feel like emotional beings until they spike."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

The plane is not indifference and the spike is not an overreaction. Individuality is mutative and operates in a pulse; the spike is where the mutation surfaces. The low here is melancholy, which needs no cause and can settle over a life that is going perfectly well. Ra treated it as raw material: melancholy converts into creativity, and the pressure in the 39-55 stream is released through music.

This wave misleads through the question "what do you want?" On the plane the honest answer is that they do not know, and pressure will not produce one early.

> "You cannot know until you know. You cannot pretend that you know. You can only know when you know."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

It is also the wave most misread from outside: the plane looks like a decision, so silence gets taken for agreement and the spike arrives as a shock.

### The Crash Wave

The collective abstract wave is the one Ra called the most archetypal, because it is the shape of the human experiential way itself.

> "Based on the pressure of desire, it rises and rises and rises and rises and rises and rises and rises. And crashes."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

Desire builds, expectation builds with it, the experience arrives, and it is not what was imagined. The Definitive Book gives the everyday version: someone imagines a whole future after a few dates and finds it is not shared. For this wave clarity arrives at the crash and not before, which is the hardest and most useful part of the teaching: the trough is not the failure of the process, it is the end of it.

The crash misleads by dressing an abstract process in logical clothing: abstract people assume that if they do this, then this, then this, the outcome follows, and it never quite does. The correction is to enter things for the experience itself and leave the expectation off.

### Carrying More Than One Wave

Combination is not a fourth wave. A chart with several Solar Plexus channels carries several shapes at once. Ra warned listeners not to take his separate descriptions as separate people: one person may run all three, or any two, and different experiences may recruit different waves.

## What Clarity Is and Is Not

Clarity is the most misunderstood word in this authority. It is not a peak, it is not the absence of emotion, and it is not truth.

> "Now clarity is not truth."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

What emerges at the end is a settled reading, not a certainty. Ra said an emotional being can never be a hundred percent sure of anything and that seventy-five or eighty percent is a good day: part of you will always know a thing could go badly, having been through the whole spectrum getting here. The Definitive Book describes the target as the point where there is no longer an emotional charge attached to the decision, which is not the same as feeling good. Nor is clarity permanent.

> "The wave keeps moving."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

Ten minutes after a correct decision you may be in a funk about having made it. That is the motor, not a verdict. Emotional clarity is not awareness either: what you wait for could never be the truth, only a way of balancing the whole wave experience into one decision.

## Waiting in Practice

Waiting is not inactivity. Ra defined it as a state, not a pause.

> "Waiting can be translated as a higher state of alertness."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

The practical form is a sentence you say to other people. I need some time. Let me sleep on it. No explanation is owed. Asking for time is itself part of the mechanism: it establishes that you are not available to be decided for.

How long has no universal answer, and the claim that Ra set 24 hours as a minimum is not what the library shows. What Ra did was tell emotional beings to build their own formula, offering a week for a big decision and 24 hours for a small one purely as examples and adding that the exact numbers did not matter. In the Waves of Chaos lecture he says the easiest part of that formula is to sleep on it. The Definitive Book sets sleeping on it as the floor for anything important; a teaching video in the library recommends 24 hours to three days.

The cost is real: you will lose some things by not jumping, and you will have to sit through the low rather than escape it. The compensation is depth, which Ra considered unique to this authority.

> "only emotional authority has depth. No other authority has depth. It is the only one."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

## How Emotional Authority Is Conditioned Away

It is conditioned away by speed. Roughly half the population is unemotional and does decide in the moment, and a culture built on that half treats hesitation as weakness.

> "It's all just anti-emotional propaganda. Slow down."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

The [[Not-Self]] shape of this authority is therefore borrowed timing. Emotional people try to be spontaneous, which is the most dangerous thing they can do, because their timing is everything and they have lost it. Ra said you can always hear it in one sentence: I do not want to miss this, I do not want to miss that. Many emotionally defined people also carry an open [[Splenic Center]], which pushes them further towards wanting it now.

A second pattern is repression. Where a child learned that feelings were unsafe to have, the adult suppresses the wave instead of riding it, and coaching material in the library describes the result as a clean wave turned to muddy water and eventually set hard. The wave still runs; it stops being readable. A third is reason-making: the motor moves, the mind demands an explanation, attaches it to whoever is nearby, then sets about solving the problem it just invented. The Definitive Book adds that society insists we should be happy all the time, so most emotionally defined people spend their lives fighting the down side of their wave.

[[Deconditioning]] here is not emotional management; it is the slow business of letting the wave run before anything is signed.

## Emotional Authority and the Types

The wave is the authority in every case; what changes with type is what you wait with. [[Strategy]] is named in a line here and taught in its own section.

- **Emotional Generator.** Strategy is to [[Respond]], but the sacral answer is no longer the decision. Ra: "A generator's response, for an emotional generator, is just collecting information for the eventual decision that has to be made emotionally." Each response is a sample taken at one point in the wave, so the practical form is to be asked the same question again over days and watch whether the sound holds. Ra's advice was to play hard to get: saying wait is power, not rejection.
- **Emotional Manifesting Generator.** Same sacral mechanism, same wave, same rule about response being a sample. The added difficulty is speed: this type is built to move fast and skip steps, and the wave cannot be accelerated.
- **Emotional Manifestor.** Strategy is to [[Inform]], and the informing is what changes. With the Solar Plexus wired to the [[Throat Center]] the wave goes out whether or not anyone is ready for it, which is why blurting at the wrong point is the classic distortion. The Definitive Book notes that emotionally defined Manifestors who do not wait meet more resistance, and that the waiting is usefully spent working out who the action will affect. Often, by the end of the wave, the urge has gone, which was the answer.
- **Emotional Projector.** Strategy is to [[Wait for the Invitation]], and the invitation need not be answered on the spot. Ra gave emotional Projectors a script and a test: when someone invites you, you can only say, give me some time. What they do with that request is itself information.

> "If they can't give you time, they don't belong in your life. If they can give you time, it means they respect you."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

## Relationships, Work and Children

In relationships the wave is shared weather. Emotionally open partners amplify it and often mistake it for their own feeling. The commonest error is taking a high for a decision: Ra's example is the bar, the person across the room, and the morning after at the other end of the wave. For 59-6 the Definitive Book advises building friendship with a lover or a business partner first, because the wave needs its cycle. Where the ratchet is running, much tension can be released by touch rather than confrontation.

At work the cost of ignoring the wave is contracts. A yes given on a high is paid for later; a no given in a low loses something correct. Make asking for time a professional habit rather than an apology; being unavailable for an instant answer tends to make an emotional person more attractive to deal with.

With children, the guidance is to let the feeling stand without demanding a reason, to teach that an emotion arriving with no memory or thought attached is worth honouring, and to respect their discernment wherever safety is not at stake. A parent with an open Solar Plexus finds boundaries hardest to hold with an emotionally defined child, because holding the line means provoking the wave.

## The Solar Plexus and the 2027 Mutation

The Solar Plexus is described in Human Design as a centre in the middle of a long mutation rather than a finished mechanism.

> "In 2027, according to Rave Cosmology, the Solar Plexus will experience a mutative event through the agency of the fifty-fifth and forty-ninth Gates genetically expressed as the Codon Histidin."
> -- Jovian Archive Human Design Dictionary

The Definitive Book places the start several thousand years ago, between the birth of Buddha and the birth of Jesus, and says it will be cosmically supported from 2027. Ra located the beginning in the 55th gate, in the [[Channel 39/55 — The Channel of Emoting]], and said its effect would be to turn the motor off, leaving the awareness developing underneath. Nothing in the library says the mutation removes Emotional Authority from anyone already alive, and all of it is stated as Rave cosmology rather than observed mechanics.
