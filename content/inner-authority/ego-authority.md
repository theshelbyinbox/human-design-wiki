# Ego Authority

Ego Authority, also called Heart Authority, is the decision-making mechanism of the small group of people whose defined [[Heart Center]] is the only motor left standing in an otherwise open chart. It comes in two forms, Ego-Manifested and Ego-Projected, together a little over one person in every hundred. The Heart is the centre of willpower, the material plane, self-esteem and the bargain. When it is the authority, the decision is not felt in the body and not awaited over time. It is heard, out loud, in what the person says.

## The Precondition: Three Silent Centres

Ego Authority is one of the minor authorities, and every minor authority rests on the same precondition: an undefined [[Solar Plexus Center]], an undefined [[Sacral Center]] and an undefined [[Splenic Center]]. Ra Uru Hu described the group as about four out of every hundred people, identified by exactly those three open centres.

The reason is the order of precedence set out in [[The Authority Hierarchy]]. A defined Solar Plexus always takes authority, because there is no truth in the now for an emotional being; failing that a defined Sacral takes it, and failing that a defined Spleen. The Heart is a motor but not an awareness centre, and it never outranks any of the three. Only when all three are silent does it become the voice of the body's truth.

Ra named a further consequence: these people have an undefined [[Root Center]] too, since every channel leaving the Root terminates in the Sacral, the Spleen or the Solar Plexus. So this is a body open almost everywhere. No emotional wave, no sacral response, no splenic hit, no root pressure. What it has is will, running through a great deal of openness.

## The Heart as a Motor

Of the four motors in the BodyGraph, the Root, the Sacral, the Solar Plexus and the Heart, the Heart is the smallest. It carries only four gates, which makes it the second least gate-oriented centre after the [[Head Center]], and each gate is tied to an organ: gate 21 to the heart muscle, gate 40 to the stomach, gate 26 to the thymus and the T-cells, gate 51 to the gall bladder. This is why misusing will is a physical matter and not only a psychological one. Crucially, the motor does not run continuously.

> "It is the smallest Center in the Bodygraph and operates in pulses, not as a constant force."
> -- Genetic Matrix

It works in bursts and then needs to stop, unlike the Sacral, which builds and sustains.

It is the hub of the [[Ego Circuit]], the only circuit whose streams flow into a centre other than the Throat, and its currency is the bargain: I will do this for you, and in return I want that. It carries no awareness at all.

> "There is no awareness in the Ego and never will be."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

That is not a criticism. It is the reason this authority works by voice rather than insight. The Heart does not know anything. It wants, or it does not want, and the wanting has to be heard before it can be known.

The centre has exactly four channels, and naming them is the whole of the mechanics. [[Channel 21/45 — The Channel of Money (The Materialist)]] runs from the Heart to the [[Throat Center]]. [[Channel 25/51 — The Channel of Initiation]] runs from the Heart to the [[G Center]]. [[Channel 26/44 — The Channel of Surrender]] runs from the Heart to the Spleen, and [[Channel 37/40 — The Channel of Community]] runs from the Heart to the Solar Plexus. There is no channel 21-51.

The last two can never carry Ego Authority, and the precondition is the reason: the 26-44 defines the Spleen, which would make the person splenic, and the 37-40 defines the Solar Plexus, which would make them emotional. So only 21-45 and 25-51 are left, and which of the two a person has decides everything about how it is lived.

## Why the Signal Is Heard, Not Felt

Every other body authority has a signal: the Solar Plexus a wave, the Sacral a sound, the Spleen an instantaneous hit. The Heart has none. Its outlet is the voice.

> "You cannot have ego-manifested authority unless the heart center is directly connected to the throat. The expression of this authority is an expression that will ultimately be verbalized, coming out of the throat center and expressing the decision verbally."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

In practice the decision is not available before the sentence is spoken. It arrives in the sentence. Someone asks whether you will take the job or sign the contract, and whatever comes out of your mouth is the answer. Not the answer you intended. The one that came out.

This is hard to trust, because the thing carrying the truth is over in a second and is often embarrassing. The person hears themselves say something blunt and wants to take it back. That impulse is the [[Not-Self]] mind reclaiming the decision, and its commonest form is after-the-fact rehearsal: I should have said this, I could have said that, next time I will say the other. Ra was unsparing about it. That replay has nothing to do with who you are.

> "Don't try to write the script. If you try to write the script, you're going to be lost."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

So the discipline is not to generate better answers. It is to notice what you said and abide by it, including when it was ungracious and including when it was a no. The no is as much the authority as the yes; Ra observed that when the ego is defined it is natural both to deny and to offer one's willpower.

## Ego-Manifested: The Heart Reaches the Throat

Ego-Manifested Authority is the configuration in which the Heart's definition reaches the Throat. There are two routes. The direct one is 21-45. The indirect one is 25-51 from the Heart into the G, then the G into the Throat through one of the four G-to-Throat channels, 1-8, 7-31, 10-20 or 13-33.

Either way a motor is connected to the Throat, and that is what makes a [[Manifestor]]. It is also why this form is called manifested. Because a Manifestor's obligation is to [[Inform]], and informing is done out loud, strategy and authority share one instrument. The voice that informs is the voice that decides.

> "Trust in your voice. It's the key for you."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

Lived well, the commitments are unusually reliable, because they were only made where the will was engaged. Lived badly, the person says one thing, revises it mentally into something more acceptable, acts on the revision, and cannot finish, because the willpower was never behind the revision.

## Ego-Projected: The Heart Reaches Only the G

Ego-Projected Authority is the configuration in which the Heart connects to the G through 25-51 and to nothing else, and the G does not carry on to the Throat. No motor reaches the Throat, and that absence is what makes this a [[Projector]] rather than a Manifestor.

> "It's called ego projected authority because the heart center is a motor, and it is more powerful in its nature than the G center."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

The result is a concentrated identity, a powerful sense of I, sitting in a sea of openness. And it has no direct voice. Ra was explicit that these beings do not have access to the Throat and do not have a voice in and of itself, which is the most important difference between the two forms. Ego-Manifested speaks and follows what it said. Ego-Projected cannot start that speaking. It has to be invited, and only then does the will find something to answer.

This is why [[Wait for the Invitation]] is not a soft recommendation here but the whole mechanism, and why the correct preparation is mastery of something worth being recognised for. When the decision comes, it is correct for it to sound selfish. The Definitive Book of Human Design puts the question plainly: what do I want, and what is in it for me. The Heart is a motor, so the answer is simply whether the will is there. The comparative survey of Ego-Manifested, Ego-Projected and Self-Projected side by side, including the bitterness of never being invited, belongs to [[Minor Authorities]].

## How Rare This Is

The sources give a range rather than a single figure, and differ by kind.

- **Ra Uru Hu, spoken.** Ego-Manifested is just under one percent, just under one in a hundred. Ego-Projected is less than one in two hundred. The minor authorities together are about four in every hundred.
- **Jovian Archive statistics.** Ego Manifested 0.80 percent and Ego Projected 0.45 percent, inside a seven-slice set summing to 100 percent. Jovian states this comes from charts run through their free calculators, so it is a large self-selected sample, not a census.
- **A second chart-statistics dashboard in the library**, built from over four hundred thousand charts, gives Ego Manifested 0.8 percent and Ego Projected 0.4 percent.

Taken together, Ego-Manifested sits between 0.8 and just under 1 percent, and Ego-Projected between 0.4 and 0.5 percent. On any set, Ego-Projected is the rarest authority with a direct inner signal.

## Work, Rest and the Misuse of Willpower

Because the Heart pulses, the correct rhythm is work and then rest, not work sustained. The Definitive Book of Human Design is direct: a defined Heart must have a balance between work and rest, and it works so that it can also have time to play. It adds that these people do best as their own boss, because that lets them follow their own sense of when to stop.

The characteristic failure is to treat the Heart as though it were a Sacral. A target is set, the will is committed, and the pushing continues long after the pulse has ended, because the goal was announced and the ego does not want to be seen to fail. That is burnout by design, and the bill arrives in the heart muscle, the stomach, the thymus and the gall bladder.

## Commitments, Promises and Relationships

A defined Heart stays healthy by making promises and keeping them, which is how others come to trust it, so it should only make promises it can and will keep. For someone with Ego Authority that is not an ethical rule laid over the mechanics. It is the mechanics. The promise the voice made spontaneously has the motor behind it; the promise the mind made to be liked does not, and it fails for want of fuel, not character.

In relationships this reads as bluntness. Someone operating correctly says what they want, declines what they do not, and expects the exchange to be fair, because the bargain is the native language of the Ego Circuit. Being pushed to soften that is how the authority is lost.

## The Open Heart by Contrast

The undefined Heart is the other side of this article, and one of the most conditioned centres in the chart.

> "Just over 60% of humanity have an undefined, open heart center."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

The Definitive Book of Human Design gives the complementary figure, 37 percent of the population with the Heart defined. Either way, most people have no consistent access to willpower at all, while living in a world that treats it as universal.

> "Despite propaganda concerning its universality, willpower is an attribute of a defined Heart Center. The not-self strategy of the undefined Heart/Ego center is a life of trying to prove your worthiness."
> -- The Human Design Dictionary, Jovian Archive

Ra called the open Heart's not-self one of the most devastating strategies on the planet, the one that says I am not worthy, so I have to prove myself. Its practical shape is over-promising: saying yes to be seen as a good person, taking on unwanted work to demonstrate value, then labouring beyond what the heart muscle can support. Ra's standing advice to anyone with an undefined Heart was that they have nothing to prove in this life. That [[Deconditioning]] work is the mirror image of the Ego Authority person's, who must instead stop apologising for wanting.

## Common Misreadings

- **Reading it as permission to be selfish.** It is a mechanism, not a licence. The selfishness is in the question, not the conduct.
- **Confusing the mind's "I want" with the Heart's.** Desire thought about in advance is usually [[Conditioning]]. The authority is in the unrehearsed sentence.
- **Treating Ego-Projected as a talking authority.** Without a Heart-to-Throat connection there is no voice of its own, and the decision waits on an invitation.
- **Assuming any defined Heart means Ego Authority.** A defined Solar Plexus, Sacral or Spleen anywhere in the chart removes it.
