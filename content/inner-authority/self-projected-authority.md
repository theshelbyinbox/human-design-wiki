# Self-Projected Authority

Self-Projected Authority is the inner decision-making mechanism of a [[Projector]] whose [[G Center]] is defined and connected to the [[Throat Center]], with no definition in the [[Solar Plexus Center]], [[Sacral Center]], [[Splenic Center]] or [[Heart Center]]. Jovian Archive states that it is exclusive to Projectors, and that deciding here means speaking your truth and hearing your own voice.

It is the one authority whose signal is not a sensation but a sound. No gut response, no intuitive hit, no emotional wave. The body produces speech, and the truth arrives as the person hears themselves speak. Ra Uru Hu taught it as one of the [[Minor Authorities]], and of those it is the most common.

## The Configuration

Every minor authority starts from the same three exclusions: no defined Solar Plexus, no defined Sacral, no defined Spleen. Self-Projected adds a fourth. The Heart must also be undefined.

> "And the thing to recognize again, no defined solar plex, no defined sacral, no defined spleen, no defined ego, just simply the G-centre defined to the throat and or above. There you have your self-projected authority."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

The fourth exclusion is what separates this authority from Ego-Projected Authority, where the Heart reaches the G through [[Channel 25/51]] and, being a motor where the G is not, dominates it. Remove the Heart and the G stands alone. [[Ego Authority]] and [[Minor Authorities]] carry that comparison in full.

Ra's phrase "and or above" matters too. Mental definition does not disqualify this authority.

> "Now, self-projected authority is the G-centre defined to the throat. And when the G-centre is defined to the throat, and the Ajna centre can be connected to the throat, as a matter of fact, you can have head, Ajna, throat, G. This is still self-projected authority."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

Read that mechanically. The G has no channel to the Ajna or the Head; its only upward connection is to the Throat. Ra is describing a chart where the Ajna runs to the Throat and the Head to the Ajna, alongside the G-to-Throat definition. Mental definition above the Throat changes nothing. It is also the line separating this authority from [[Mental Authority]] and [[No Direct Inner Authority]], since a Mental Projector has nothing defined below the Throat at all.

Ra called it about two and a half percent of the population, two to three out of every hundred, and not so rare. The library's chart statistics agree: a JovianArchive.com set gives Self Projected 2.84 percent, a second live-chart dashboard gives G authority 2.9 percent. Both are self-selected calculator samples, not censuses. [[Inner Authority Overview]] gives roughly 2 to 3 percent, [[Minor Authorities]] approximately 2.5 percent.

## The Four Channels to the Throat

The G reaches the Throat through exactly four channels, and any one alone produces this authority.

- **[[Channel 1/8]], the Channel of Inspiration.** Gate 1 in the G, Gate 8 in the Throat.
- **[[Channel 7/31]], the Channel of the Alpha.** Gate 7 in the G, Gate 31 in the Throat.
- **[[Channel 10/20]], the Channel of Awakening.** Gate 10 in the G, Gate 20 in the Throat.
- **[[Channel 13/33]], the Channel of the Prodigal.** Gate 13 in the G, Gate 33 in the Throat.

These are the BodyGraph's four role-gate channels. Ra listed them by their G-side gates, the 10, the 7, the 1 and the 13, and held that whichever carries the identity up, the authority is the same. What differs is the role the voice carries.

> "They all carry with them roles of identity. Those roles of identity are deep expressions of who you are relative to the other and relative to circumstance."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

## Why Identity Has to Be Spoken

The G Centre is the seat of identity, love and direction, and it is neither a motor nor an awareness centre. It makes no energy and generates no awareness of its own. That is why this authority has to be audible.

A defined Sacral answers in sound, a defined Spleen gives a hit of awareness, a defined Solar Plexus runs a wave across time. The G does none of this. It holds an identity in place but has no instrument to report on it and no motor to push it into the world. Its one route out is the Throat, so the identity becomes knowable only once said aloud. Ra's rule: in any authority connected to the Throat, whatever you need to know you will discover in what it is that you say.

> "They are one of the few beings whose truth is always expressed through the core of their identity. It means you have to listen to it. If you don't listen to it, if you don't hear it, there is no truth for you. And if there is no truth for you, there is no success for you."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

## The Mechanism: What You Hear Yourself Say

The signal is the spontaneous utterance. Not the sentence that was planned, and not the one that survived editing.

> "It means that when you were invited, listen, listen to what you say. When somebody asks you something, listen to what you say. Hear it. Don't try to figure out what you should say or could say or might say."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

The difference is mechanical, not stylistic. A rehearsed sentence is assembled by the mind, and here the mind sits inside an almost entirely open chart, so its material is largely conditioning. The unrehearsed sentence leaves before the mind can reach it, and so leaves carrying the G. Ra's warning was blunt: write the script and you will be lost.

The test is not whether the statement sounds reasonable; plenty of conditioned sentences do. It is what happens in the speaker on hearing it, which Jovian Archive calls feeling the resonance of one's own words. A person with this authority often does not know what they want until they have said it. That is not indecision. It is the mechanism working.

## The Listener

Because the signal does not exist until spoken, this authority structurally depends on other people being available. Not for their judgement. For their ears.

> "You don’t need advice. You simply need a sounding board, a good friend, or someone you trust to listen while you talk through your options."
> -- Karen Curry Parker, The Encyclopedia of Human Design

A correct listener stays present and out of the way. They ask open questions, and where it helps reflect back what they actually heard with no reading of their own attached, because a person with this authority cannot always hear themselves clearly. That is the Parenting by Design guidance for parents of self-projected children, and it generalises.

A correct listener does not advise, counsel, argue or supply a preference, and the reason is mechanical rather than polite. Once advice enters the room the speaker is responding to the advice, so what leaves their mouth is a reaction to someone else's position, which is the very conditioning this authority exists to escape. The Unshakeable with Human Design material is emphatic: you do not ask someone else's opinion on whether a thing is right for you.

Sources split on whether a second person is strictly required. Ra does not make the listener a mechanical component; he locates the authority entirely in the speaker's own utterance at the moment of invitation. Business by Design goes further.

> "Speaking things out loud, alone, helps you hear your truth and direction clearly."
> -- Business by Design, The Soul Strategy Method

Both agree on the essential point: the words have to leave the body and be heard.

## The Open Body

This is the configuration's distinguishing feature, and the part most often skipped. The identity is defined; almost nothing else is. There are no motors at all: no Root, no Sacral, no Solar Plexus, no Heart. Nothing generates sustained energy, and everything around the G is open, taking in and amplifying whatever is in the field. Ra described the openness the minor authorities share this way:

> "You have all those areas that are open and those areas that are open bring all kinds of conditioning forces into your life, never knowing what enough is enough, trying to hold on to things, always being in a hurry, avoiding confrontation and truth, all of these things abound."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

Each pressure is someone else's energy amplified and mistaken for one's own: a Sacral that never knows when enough is enough, a Heart under pressure to prove worth, a Solar Plexus that avoids confrontation, a Spleen that holds on. None of it is the identity, and all of it is louder, because the identity makes no noise until it is spoken. Ra's word for the result was lost. A Self-Projected Projector has to tell an amplified signal from their own, and the voice is their only reliable discriminator. See [[Conditioning]].

## The Invitation Comes First

The Projector strategy is [[Wait for the Invitation]], covered in [[Strategy]]. What is specific here is how tightly strategy and authority are welded: the utterance that carries truth is made in response to an invitation, so without one the authority has nothing to work on.

> "Without the invitation, there's nothing to say. Without the invitation, there's nothing to hear. Without the invitation, there can be no decision."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

This is why talking a decision through before any invitation exists produces little. There is no real proposition for the identity to speak to, so what comes out is speculation.

## Practical Forms

Only a few practices are supported across the library, all variations on one:

- **Talk the decision through out loud with someone whose only job is to listen.** A friend, partner or colleague with no stake in the outcome.
- **Keep a standing arrangement rather than improvising one.** The library describes a trusted person who is simply available, not one recruited in a crisis.
- **Speak it aloud alone where no listener is available.** Supported by Business by Design above, though not by Ra.

The Definitive Book of Human Design points this authority toward what makes a person feel like themselves, and gives three questions for the talking: will this make me happy, will this give me self expression, am I heading in the right direction.

## Common Misreadings

- **Mistaking it for asking around for advice.** Collecting ten opinions is not self-projected decision-making; it is an open chart shopping for a position to absorb. The library describes people with this authority going wrong exactly this way, canvassing opinions and reading around for an answer that only ever existed in their identity.
- **Mistaking a mental conclusion for the spoken truth.** A person can reason their way to a conclusion, say it aloud, and take the saying as confirmation. Saying a decided thing is delivery, not authority. The signal is the sentence that arrives, not the one approved in advance.

## What It Costs to Ignore the Voice

The identity is the only thing in this chart reliably theirs, and its one expression is the voice, so ignoring it leaves a person nothing of their own to steer by. The result is the Projector not-self theme of bitterness, reached by a specific route: a life of decisions taken from amplified conditioning, none ever theirs. Ra's correction was to trust what you say, hear it, respect it, and abide by it.

> "Avoid the bitterness, trust your identity, trust its voice, but only, only in invitation."
> -- Ra Uru Hu
