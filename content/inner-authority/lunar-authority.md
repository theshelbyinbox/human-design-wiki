# Lunar Authority

Lunar Authority belongs to [[Reflector]]s and to no one else. It is the only inner authority in Human Design not located anywhere in the body. A Reflector has [[No Definition|no definition]] at all, all nine centers open, so there is no sacral sound, no emotional wave of their own, no splenic hit, no willpower, nothing fixed to consult. What they have instead is the Moon, and the authority is the time it takes the Moon to travel the wheel.

> "You know, you look at a reflector, there is only strategy. That is the authority. Time, time, nothing else."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

In every other design, strategy and authority are two separate things. For the Reflector they collapse into one. [[Wait the Lunar Cycle|Waiting the lunar cycle]] is the strategy and it is also the authority.

## Why No Definition Means No Fixed Authority

Definition is what produces both type and authority in every other chart. It is the hard wiring, and it is what strategy and authority are read from. A Reflector has none of it, and Ra described that whiteness as no definition, no consistent life force and no consistent hard wiring.

That is easily misread as a deficit. Openness in a defined chart is the counterweight to whatever is fixed; in a Reflector there is nothing fixed for it to counterweight, and Ra was explicit that the two kinds of openness are not the same thing. Consistency must come from outside the body.

> "But everything about the reflector is about time. Everything. Because, in fact, a reflector on any given day is only an aspect of who they are."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

## The Moon as the Agent of Authority

A Reflector's chart is not empty. It is full of hanging gates, single gate activations with nothing at the other end of the channel. The Definitive Book of Human Design calls each a receptor seeking its harmonic gate on the far side. The Moon supplies those gates, faster than any other body in the wheel.

> "The Moon is the fastest moving object of the wheel. The Moon is going to go through all 64 gates in approximately a month."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

The Moon does pass through all sixty-four gates; the library states this directly in several places, including the Definitive Book's glossary. But it does not create definition in all sixty-four. Definition appears only where the Moon lands on a gate harmonic to one of that Reflector's own hanging gates.

> "All the activations of the Reflector are facing inactive harmonic Gates. The Moon in its rapid cycle will activate and for that half day define each and every harmonic Gate."
> -- Jovian Archive Human Design Dictionary

Each hookup is brief. The Definitive Book notes a definition made this way may last only half a day, and the Dictionary puts the Moon in a gate for slightly under twelve hours. The month is not one long mood but a fast sequence of short, specific configurations.

## How Long the Cycle Is, and When It Starts

The library does not give one number.

- **Twenty-eight days.** The Definitive Book of Human Design, in its Reflector strategy heading, its Lunar Authority entry and its glossary. The Newcomer's Guide by Lynda Bunnell and Ruth Brennan also uses 28.
- **Twenty-eight to twenty-nine days.** Ra Uru Hu, in the No Direct Inner Authority lecture.
- **28.5 days, and 29.5 days.** The Jovian Archive Human Design Dictionary, in two different entries.

Part of the spread is astronomy rather than disagreement: the Definitive Book notes the Moon takes roughly 28 days to circle the Earth but about 30 days from one full moon to the next. The teaching is identical in all of them. Roughly a month, and specifically the Reflector's own repeating pattern rather than a tally of days.

As for when the month begins, Ra's answer in the RaZen talk on Reflectors is that the cycle starts the moment the stimulation arrives, when something comes toward the Reflector that will demand a decision. It is not the calendar's new moon, and Reflectors cannot start it alone; the Definitive Book is explicit that they must be initiated for the big decisions of life.

## Becoming a Different Configuration Through the Month

As the Moon moves, the Reflector is not simply in a different mood. They are wired differently.

> "The magic of being a reflector is that you're here to sample. As you sample through that cycle, you collect all kinds of fascinating data. At one moment, mental. At the next, it may be ego. At the next, your identity."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

For half a day the Moon completes a channel into the [[Solar Plexus Center]] and there is a wave to ride. Later it completes one into the [[Splenic Center]] and decisions feel instantaneous. Later still it hooks up the [[Sacral Center]] and there is response energy that was not there before. The example cycle in the Definitive Book shows one Reflector registering across a single week as a Projector, then a Manifestor, then a Generator, then a Reflector again. This is sampling, not becoming.

It is also what separates a Reflector's temporary definition from ordinary transit conditioning. For a defined person a bridging transit intrudes on their consistency. For a Reflector it is the consistency.

> "Each one not simply being a conditioned temporary definition, but in the case of a reflector, it is their definition."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

## The Cycle Is a Sample, Not a Delay

The common misreading is that the Reflector is stalling, and that the month is dead time before a decision that could have been made on day one. It is the opposite: the month is where the decision is made. Nothing is postponed, and a partial cycle is a partial sample, not a smaller version of the answer.

> "You don't know after a week. You don't know after two weeks. You don't know after three weeks. You don't. Because it's not complete yet. It's only when you go through the complete cycle."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

What is gathered is experience, not analysis; Ra held that a Reflector's decision is never associated with conceptualizing, so the Reflector who spends the month thinking harder has substituted the mind for the mechanism. The Definitive Book adds a note easy to miss: if the cycle has run and clarity has not arrived, keep waiting. The cycle is a minimum, not a deadline.

## Mapping Your Own Cycle

Because the pattern repeats identically every month for life, it can be learned, and Ra named this as the first thing to do.

> "The first thing to do as a reflector is to map the wheel. See the sequence of it. Follow it around the wheel and you will begin to see who you are."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

In practice: list every hanging gate, find the harmonic gate at the other end of each channel, mark those positions on the wheel, and use an ephemeris to see when the Moon will sit on each. The result is a personal calendar of temporary definitions, repeating monthly.

The other half is keeping the record. Noting day by day where the Moon is and what the day was actually like builds the correspondence between transit and experience. Over enough cycles a Reflector learns which days in their own month are consistently clear, which are social, which are flat, and which are the days a decision tends to settle on. The Definitive Book calls this a unique sense of consistency, comparable to but not the same as definition.

## Talking Through the Cycle

Reflectors are told to discuss a pending decision with several different people across the month, and the purpose is not advice. The Definitive Book is emphatic that these others are confidantes trusted to listen and help the Reflector articulate their own thoughts, not to influence the decision or make it.

What they provide is more sampling. Ra described the Reflector aura itself as a sampling mechanism: rub up against a Reflector and they sample you and compare. Each person carries a different definition, so each conversation is a different auric environment to set beside the others, and many people across a month is a broader sample than one person asked repeatedly. The parallel with the Mental Projector's sounding board is in [[No Direct Inner Authority]].

## Environment and Community

For most types environment is a factor. For a Reflector it is decisive, and the reason is mechanical. Every Reflector has an open [[G Center]], without exception.

> "That undefined G-center is always pushing towards some kind of situation resolution. Where is the right place to be?"
> -- Ra Uru Hu

Because the sampling aura takes in whatever is around it, the environment does not merely colour a Reflector's experience of the cycle, it supplies much of the content of it. A cycle run in the wrong place returns data about the wrong place. Ra held that wherever they incarnate, Reflectors will always be the samplers of their community.

The Definitive Book puts finding the right environment within the right community among the most important decisions a Reflector faces, and says their correct place is in the heart of a community where they can freely sample what is around them. Its test is plain: if the environment feels good the Reflector feels good, and if it is unhealthy they will not.

## Demanding the Time

None of this is available to a Reflector who cannot hold the line. Lunar Authority has to be asked for out loud, in a world that treats a month as an unreasonable answer, and Ra was blunt that a Reflector who does not demand respect for what they are will never get it.

> "You're a cycle. You are a cycle being. You're very, very different from the rest. You don't have a whole picture until you go through your cycles."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

His position on the cost is the one taken across the [[Inner Authority]] material: the opportunities that will not wait were never the right opportunities.

> "And it's an interesting thing about beings. That if you are correct in your demand for respect, you will get it. They will wait. And those who do not wait, you did not lose anything."
> -- Ra Uru Hu

## Disappointment, Health and the Rushed Reflector

The Reflector's [[Not-Self]] theme is disappointment and the [[Signature]] is surprise. Disappointment here is not a mood about outcomes. It is the readout of a life lived on other people's timing, where decisions were made inside the moment instead of across the cycle and so never had a chance of being correct.

The library also links this to the body, and it is worth stating only as far as the sources go. The Definitive Book says a Reflector who does not honour the lunar cycle may suffer disappointment and dis-ease; that most Reflectors have been rushed all their lives and their health begins to break down from pushing against the natural flow of life, the resistance having an impact on their bodies. That is the whole of the claim the corpus supports: resistance and its cost, not a diagnosis. With nothing defined there is no fixed physical baseline and no consistent energy to spend, which is why rest and time alone to discharge what has been sampled are not indulgences here.

## How Rare This Is

The figures in the library disagree, so they are given here as a band.

- **Approximately 1 percent.** The Definitive Book of Human Design, in the Reflector chapter and in the glossary.
- **Approximately 1 to 2 percent.** The Jovian Archive Human Design Dictionary.
- **Around 1.3 or 1.4 percent.** Ra Uru Hu's own spoken figure, which he also puts as roughly one in every hundred people.

The JovianArchive.com statistics set for Inner Authority gives no Reflector figure at all. Its smallest slice, 3.58 percent, is labelled "None" and covers Reflectors and Mental Projectors together without splitting them. It is drawn from charts run through Jovian's free calculators, so it is a large self-selected sample rather than a census.

## Where This Sits Among the Authorities

Lunar Authority is one of two configurations with no direct in-the-moment body signal; the other is the Mental Projector, whose process runs through conversation rather than time. Both are compared in [[No Direct Inner Authority]]. [[Ego Authority|Ego]] and [[Self-Projected Authority|Self-Projected]] are in [[Minor Authorities]]; the full ordering is in [[The Authority Hierarchy]].

A Reflector is not a being without authority. A Reflector is a being whose authority is not in them, but in time.

> "For you, there is only truth after a cycle of the moon. This is your key."
> -- Ra Uru Hu
