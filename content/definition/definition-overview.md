# Definition Overview

Definition is what is consistent in a person. When two centers are joined by a completed channel, with both of its gates activated, those centers are defined, and the energy between them runs the same way every day for a lifetime. Everything else in the BodyGraph is open to influence. Definition is the part that is not.

The question this section answers is not which centers are defined but how the defined parts sit in relation to each other. Are they all joined in one piece, or separated into two, three or four islands with gaps between them? That arrangement is a person's definition type, and it shapes how quickly they process, what they look for in other people, and where conditioning gets in.

## The Five Types

The Definitive Book of Human Design lists five ways definition is expressed in a BodyGraph, with the population figures it gives:

- **[[No Definition]]**, a Reflector with no centers defined, approximately 2 percent
- **[[Single Definition]]**, all defined channels and centers connected in one continuous flow, approximately 41 percent
- **[[Split Definition]]**, two separate areas of definition not connected to each other, approximately 46 percent
- **[[Triple Split Definition]]**, three separate areas, approximately 11 percent
- **[[Quadruple Split Definition]]**, four separate areas, approximately 0.5 percent

The Jovian dictionary gives the same five and states the principle plainly. "There are four different kinds of Definition: Single Definition -- A continuous connection. Split Definition -- Two areas of definition separate from each other. Triple-Split Definition -- Three separated areas. Quadruple-Split Definition -- Four separate areas. Finally, there are those without definition."

## What The Numbers Actually Are

Three of the five figures are stable across sources. Single at about 41 percent, Split at about 46 percent and Triple Split at about 11 percent are given the same way by the Definitive Book, by Genetic Matrix and by the reference material in the library.

Two are not stable, and both are worth stating as ranges rather than as facts.

**Quadruple Split.** The Definitive Book says approximately 0.5 percent. Ra Uru Hu's own lecture on the subject, and Jovian Archive with it, put it at 0.63 percent, which Ra glossed as "one out of every 200 people, something in that neighborhood." Genetic Matrix says about 0.6 percent. The reference material in the library summarises the spread as "somewhere between 0.57% and 1% of the global population." A figure of 1 percent is the outer edge of that range, not the middle of it.

**No Definition.** The Definitive Book's list of definition types says approximately 2 percent, while the glossary in the same book says Reflectors are 1 percent of the population. Ra's lecture says about 1.4 percent. Since No Definition and Reflector are the same population, these are three different answers to one question, and the honest reading is somewhere between 1 and 2 percent.

A note on naming. The Definitive Book calls the two-area type **Simple-Split Definition**. Contemporary teaching, and this wiki, use Split Definition for the same thing. Both names appear in the library and they mean the same arrangement.

## What A Split Actually Is

The mechanic is a gap, not a weakness. As the Definitive Book puts it, "A split in a design means all defined channels and centers are not continuously connected within the BodyGraph; a gate or channel remains open between two, three or four areas of definition."

So a split is defined by what is missing. Somewhere between two islands of definition there is a gate, or a channel, that would join them if it were activated. It is not, so the two areas run independently.

That missing piece is what makes splits socially live. When another person carries the gate that closes the gap, the two areas connect for as long as that person is present. That is [[Bridging]], and it is the most conditioning-heavy mechanic in the whole system, because it feels like completion.

## Processing Speed

The practical consequence of separation is timing. Information has further to travel, and more gaps to cross, before all of a person's definition has had a say.

Contemporary teaching sets it out as a simple order: Single is fastest, Split slower, Triple Split slower still, Quadruple Split slowest. The same source is careful about what that does and does not mean. "This is not about intelligence. It is about the time required for information to move across your internal circuitry." Emotional Authority adds its own wave on top of any definition type, so a Split with Emotional Authority is waiting on two different clocks.

## Where Conditioning Gets In

Every chart takes conditioning through its undefined centers. Definition type decides whether there is a second route in as well.

Contemporary teaching draws the split this way. Single Definition and Triple Split tend to be conditioned mainly through their open and undefined centers. Split Definition and Quadruple Split are additionally sensitive to bridging gates, because a person who closes the gap changes how the whole design runs while they are there.

That second route is the one worth understanding, because it does not feel like conditioning. It feels like relief.

## Definition Is Not A Ranking

Nothing in the five types is better or worse than another. Genetic Matrix states it directly: "Definition is not about intelligence or capability. It describes how energy integrates internally. A Quadruple Split is not less capable than a Single Definition, but it often needs more time for separate areas of definition to synthesize experience before action."

Definition also does not change. The natal arrangement is fixed for life. What changes is whether a gap is currently bridged, by a transit or by a person, and that is temporary by nature.

## How Definition Is Read

Definition is derived, never chosen. Count the separate groups of connected defined centers in a BodyGraph, and the number of groups is the type. One group is Single, two is Split, three is Triple Split, four is Quadruple Split, none is No Definition.

Definition sits alongside [[Energy Types|Type]], [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]] rather than above them. It describes the shape of what is consistent, and it explains a great deal about relationships and timing, but it is not a decision-making tool. The decision-making still runs through Strategy and Authority.
