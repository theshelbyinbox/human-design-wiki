# Split Definition

Split Definition is a BodyGraph in which the defined centers fall into two separate groups that do not connect to each other. The Definitive Book of Human Design lists it as "SIMPLE-SPLIT DEFINITION - two separate areas of definition that are not connected to each other (approximately 46 percent of the population)." Simple-Split Definition is the classical name. Contemporary teaching says Split Definition for the same arrangement, and this wiki uses the shorter name.

At approximately 46 percent, Split is the most common of the five definition types, ahead of [[Single Definition]] at approximately 41 percent and [[Triple Split Definition]] at approximately 11 percent. That matters before any of the mechanics do. A split is not a defect or a rarity. It is what most people are walking around in. Ra Uru Hu put the consequence bluntly: "Split definitions are the plurality on this planet. It means they have an enormous influence over us culturally and in terms of our civilizations."

## Two Areas And A Gap

Definition exists where a channel is fully activated, both of its gates lit, joining two centers. A split is what happens when that joining does not reach across the whole chart. As the Definitive Book states it, "A split in a design means all defined channels and centers are not continuously connected within the BodyGraph; a gate or channel remains open between two, three or four areas of definition." So a split is defined by an absence. Somewhere between the two islands sits a gate, or a whole channel, that would join them if it were activated. The two areas each run perfectly well. They simply do not talk to each other.

The piece on the near side of that gap has its own name. The Jovian glossary calls it a hanging gate: "A gate alone in a channel (either dormant in an undefined center, or active in a defined center), that is open to receiving the harmonic gate via an electromagnetic connection through a transit or the aura of another." A hanging gate on the edge of a split is an open socket. Something is designed to plug into it, and that something is always outside the person.

## What The Missing Piece Does To The Mind

The gap is not neutral. The Definitive Book is precise about what it produces. "When you have a split in your design, the quality and characteristics of the 'missing' gate or channel that would bridge the split is a motivating factor in your life. You will pursue the qualities of the missing gate or channel as though it represents something that is incomplete or wrong with your life, or as if this is a part of you that needs to be completed or repaired."

The worked example in the book is the one Ra used in his own lecture. A person has [[Gate 48 — Depth / The Well|Gate 48]] in the Splenic Center but not [[Gate 16 — Skills (Enthusiasm)|Gate 16]] in the Throat, so the [[Channel 16/48 — The Channel of The Wavelength (Talent)|16/48]] never completes and the split stays open. "Gate 48 is the Gate of Depth; Gate 16 is the Gate of Skills. For this person's depth to express itself through the Throat Center, she needs Gate 16. She may never feel she is skillful enough, and will go out into the world looking for ways to develop her skills."

Notice the reversal. The depth is hers, defined for life. The skill is not, and the skill is the only thing she can see. "In reality, it is her not-self that is trying to fill the mis-perceived void. She wants to literally become that missing piece, and ends up making mental decisions to attempt to do just that." That is the split's characteristic error: a lifelong campaign to personally become the one thing the design was never going to supply.

## Small Split And Large Split

Splits are not all the same width, and the width changes the psychology completely.

A small split needs one gate to close. A hanging gate sits on one side, its harmonic partner is missing on the other, and a single activation anywhere in the world completes the circuit. A chart may carry several such gates around the same gap, giving several separate one-gate routes across it. Classical teaching calls this the simple split.

A large split needs more: a full channel, several gates, in some charts as many as three channels. The Definitive Book calls this the wide split and describes a different flavour of not-self. "In a wide split, which means it takes an entire channel or multiple gates to bridge the different areas of definition, one will experience the split as being the other person's problem, or from a not-self perspective, the other person is the cause of the problem. This can engender blame and victimhood that are not true."

Ra taught the same contrast as a flip of blame. With a one-gate bridge, the person concludes that they are the one lacking. The moment the bridge takes more than a gate, it is never them who has the problem, and the broader the split, the more convinced they become that everybody else is at fault. Both readings land in the same place. The wide split does carry a gift alongside the blame: "There is a potential for wisdom in a wide split if one uses it as an opportunity to become an unattached, objective observer of others, and does not get locked into the blame game."

A note on names. Contemporary teaching says small split and large split, and uses wide split for large. Classical teaching says simple split and wide split. The mechanic is the same. One wrinkle: the Definitive Book uses Simple-Split Definition for the whole two-area type, while Ra in lecture used simple split more narrowly, for the charts that close with a single gate.

## The Pull Toward People Who Bridge

Because the missing piece is a gate, and gates are carried by other people, the split is inherently social. The Jovian glossary defines the mechanic in two sentences. "Bridge: Any activation, whether from a transit or a person, that links a Split Definition in a person's chart. Bridges have a deep conditioning influence."

When someone carrying the missing gate walks into the aura, the circuit closes, and for as long as that person is present the design runs as though it were single. Ra described this as an electromagnetic connection, one person holding one gate of a channel and the other holding its partner, and he was clear that it produces attraction and repulsion in the same motion. The attraction is automatic, because the other has what you do not. The repulsion follows, because you resent having to get it from them.

The Definitive Book allows the romance of it without excusing the trap. "In this respect, saying 'you complete me' is both profound and true. We will only have healthy and effective relationships, however, when they are entered into correctly through Strategy and Authority." Bridges also come from transits, and Ra noted that the outer planets and the nodes bring bridging conditioning lasting years rather than an evening. The full mechanic is covered in [[Bridging]].

## Why This Is The Heaviest Conditioning In The System

Every chart takes conditioning through its open centers. Splits take it through the gaps as well, and for a two-area split the gaps come first. The Definitive Book sets out a hierarchy: "For a Simple-Split Definition, the bridging gate(s) that connect the two definitions is the most powerful conditioning element in your design, followed by the centers." Ra drew the same line across the five types. Single Definition and Triple Split are dominated by their centers. Split Definition and [[Quadruple Split Definition|Quadruple Split]] are dominated by their bridges. For a split, the single most conditioning force in the life is not an open center at all. It is a gate the person does not have.

What makes bridging the heaviest conditioning in the system is how it feels. Conditioning through an open center announces itself as pressure, and a person can learn to recognise the discomfort. A bridge does the opposite. It arrives as relief. The scattered thing settles, the internal argument stops, and the person feels for the first time like one continuous being. Nothing in that raises an alarm, because completion is the last sensation anybody thinks to be suspicious of.

Ra's warning is the sentence to keep. "The fact that somebody creates a bridge for you as a split definition does not mean they are for you. There are so many different human beings who can fulfill that. There are planets in the transit program that can fulfill that. The question is, can you discern who is the right other?"

He named the failure that follows. "One of the real dilemmas of the split definition is dependency." Getting stuck with an aura that completes the mechanic while the being attached to it is not correct. His instruction was plain: step outside of your split often. Even a correct relationship is not one to be locked inside twenty-four hours a day.

## Timing

Separation costs time. Information lands in one area, is processed there, and has nowhere to go until something joins the two. Ra taught this as a straight progression across the definition types.

"Whenever you're dealing with a split definition you have to recognize that the moment that you move from single definition to split definition to triple split definition to quadruple split definition, everything slows down. You cannot expect a triple split to operate at the same speed as a single definition. A single definition integrates information completely the moment it receives it. A split definition does not. In other words, it can receive information but those two sets of information as they operate through their design don't have a relationship to each other. You're always waiting for the bridge."

The Definitive Book says it practically. "Those with Split Definitions require more time to fully take in, process and digest information, simply because the separate pieces of their definition are not connected." Until the areas connect, splits may feel incomplete and find clear decisions hard to reach. This is not a statement about intelligence. It is a statement about distance.

## Public Places

The one piece of practical guidance the Definitive Book gives splits specifically is unusually concrete. When a decision is pending, go where strangers are. "The best way to bridge a split, when making a difficult or important decision, is to make use of a public place where you can still be alone, such as a bookstore, coffee shop or walking in a shopping mall. These public melting pots of designs will help provide neutral auras, and neutral conditioning, to assist you in the assimilation process."

A crowd supplies bridging without supplying an agenda. A partner bridges the same gate every day. A bookshop bridges a different one every few minutes.

## Living It

The book is direct about how splits go wrong when they stop waiting. "Initiating when inappropriate, lack of patience, impulsive action or premature speech are the biggest problems for splits when they don't take time to follow their Strategy and Authority." On the temptation of hunting for the person who completes you: "Seeking wholeness with a specific partner by initiating, rather than waiting, is a not-self activity." The alternative is not to give up on connection but to stop manufacturing it. "If you have a split, you are designed to have others naturally bridge your split(s) as you move through your life day by day. Let your Strategy and Authority do the work for you."

Ra reduced the subject to discernment. Everything about a split is about the other, and the only real tool is the ability to tell which other belongs in the aura. "As a split definition, if you want to be correct in this life, redefine your relationships. There is no other way." Deal with relationships through [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]], and the ones that were never correct fall away on their own. He also located where the pressure of the gaps surfaces: the areas between the splits are where the [[Not-Self]] theme of the type shows up, anger in the Manifestor, frustration in the Generator, bitterness in the Projector.

Two things hold this together. This is the majority design, and Ra called the split definition the force that locks us into the pair-bonding game, bringing the genetic imperative straight to the surface as a demand for the other. And the split is not a wound to be healed. The gap is permanent, it is correct, and it is the reason the person is built for relationship at all. What changes is never the chart. It is whether the people standing in the gap were chosen by the mind or arrived through Strategy and Authority.
