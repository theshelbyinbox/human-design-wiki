# No Definition

No Definition is the arrangement in which no two centers in a BodyGraph are joined by a completed channel. Nothing is hooked up to anything. The Definitive Book of Human Design lists it first among the five definition types: "NO DEFINITION - a Reflector with no centers defined (approximately 2 percent of the population)."

It is the only definition type that lands on exactly one [[Energy Types|Type]]. Everyone with no definition is a [[Reflector]], and every Reflector has no definition. The population figure is the least settled number in this section, and the honest answer is a range: roughly 1 to 2 percent.

## Three Answers To One Question

Because No Definition and Reflector describe the same people from two angles, every figure given for one is a figure for the other. The library gives at least three, and two of them sit in the same book.

- The Definitive Book's list of definition types says **approximately 2 percent**
- The glossary at the back of that same book says Reflectors are **1 percent** of the population
- Ra Uru Hu's lecture on definition says **about 1.4 percent**, and in his lecture on the Reflector he put it slightly wider: "as a percentage, reflectors hover around 1.3 or 1.4% of the human population"

The Jovian dictionary skips the point estimate and states the spread as the answer: "In the general scheme of things, Reflectors are really different. They have no definition and comprise approximately 1-2% of the human population."

That is the position taken here, because choosing one figure would pretend a disagreement had been settled. For scale, the other four run: [[Single Definition]] about 41 percent, [[Split Definition]] about 46 percent, [[Triple Split Definition]] about 11 percent, and [[Quadruple Split Definition]] between 0.5 and 0.63 percent depending on the source. No Definition is rare, but on those numbers it is not the rarest arrangement in the BodyGraph.

## The Mechanic

Definition is made by completed channels, not by activated gates. As the Definitive Book puts it, "when two gates at both ends of a channel come together they form a definition." One end alone is a hanging gate, and a hanging gate defines nothing. No Definition is what happens when that pairing never occurs anywhere in the chart.

Ra Uru Hu taught definition as the thing everything else is read from. "It is through that definition that we get the hard wiring. We get to be able to see those things that are consistent and therefore things that can be reliable. And in turn, through those configurations, we get to the strategy and authority." Looking at a chart with none of it, he described what is left: "There is no definition. There is no consistent life force in that sense. There is no consistent hard wiring."

Every other definition type has somewhere inside it that runs the same way every day for a lifetime. This one has none, so there is no internal constant to check against and no inner reading that reports the same result tomorrow as it did today. Ra put the consequence in one sentence worth sitting with: "a reflector on any given day is only an aspect of who they are."

That is not an empty chart. The usual activations are all there; they simply never pair up, so each is a hanging gate in an undefined center, and they are the only fixed activations in the design. One of the books in the library says so directly: "the gates in their charts are very important, because those are the only consistent energies found there." Reading a chart like this means reading the gates and the profile, not shrugging at nine white centers. See [[Nine Centers]] for how an undefined center and a completely open one differ.

## The Whole Design Samples

With nothing fixed to run on, the design runs on what is around it. Definition is normally what a person transmits and openness is what receives; here nothing is transmitting and everything is receiving. The Definitive Book's glossary makes the sampling part of the definition of the Type itself: "One of the four Types, no definition; connected to the Moon and the Lunar cycle; Reflectors are 1 percent of the population; have the capacity to sense who is living authentically, and who has been conditioned by the transit field; aura samples the environment."

The Newcomer's Guide gives the same mechanic from the inside: "Your only source of definition is from your connection with others or by the transits ... In a sense, you sample and reflect back information that you take in."

## Openness Here Is Not The Same Openness

It would be easy to read no definition as maximum conditioning, and Ra blocked that reading. For everyone with definition, openness is the counterpoint to what is hardwired, and that contrast is where the not-self gets its grip. "But the openness in a reflector is not the same. It is not a counterpoint to what is hardwired at all." The Newcomer's Guide arrives at the same place from the other direction, naming open centers as the greatest conditioning force for the not-self here "but not to the same extent as the others ... you're more resistant to the influence of other people than the other types."

[[Conditioning]] does its lasting damage by making an open center feel like an identity. Where there is definition there is a fixed self available to be contradicted and overwritten; where there is none, borrowed material has nothing to attach to permanently and it passes through. That is what makes this openness a sampling instrument rather than a wound, and why [[Deconditioning]] in this design is less about clearing out a false identity than about not mistaking today's reading for a permanent one. Ra's oldest metaphor for the BodyGraph applies here without remainder: "What you have colored in is the student. And what is white is where you go to school."

## Environment And Community Carry More Weight Here

Every design is affected by where it is and who it is with. In this one, that is not an influence on the design. It is the design's only source of definition, which turns the question of place from a preference into a mechanic. The reference material in the library puts it first among the things that matter: "A Reflector's environment is paramount. Being in the right, healthy, aligned environment and surrounded by the right people (who are also healthy, aligned, and correct for them!) is key."

Community works the same way, and the material is specific that it should be a wide one. Contemporary teaching observes that these are beings not designed to be locked into small groups, and that being in the right place is critical for them. Variety here is not restlessness; it is how a design with no internal constant gets a broad enough sample to be reading the world rather than one household. None of which makes other people the authority. It makes them the material.

## Why The Strategy Is A Lunar Cycle

The link from no definition to waiting a lunar cycle is direct rather than decorative. Ra taught it as the consequence of having nothing fixed: "But everything about the reflector is about time. Everything." And on authority: "the authority for a reflector is actually time. It's time." A decision cannot be made in the moment, because there is no consistent inner reading in the moment to make it with, and it cannot run for an unbounded stretch either. It is locked to one specific cycle.

The mechanics of that cycle are what make it the right length. The Jovian dictionary lays them out: "Its cycle is 29.5 days. The Moon spends slightly less than twelve hours in a Gate. All the activations of the Reflector are facing inactive harmonic Gates. The Moon in its rapid cycle will activate and for that half day define each and every harmonic Gate."

So the transiting [[Moon]] supplies, one at a time and for about half a day each, the missing halves of the channels this chart never completes. Over one full pass, every piece of definition the design is capable of holding is switched on and off again. Waiting the cycle is not patience for its own sake; it is the only way to consult the whole design, because the whole design is never present at once. Ra named the strategy in the same breath as the mechanic: "The strategy of a reflector is to wait for the cycle of the moon." Where the other types are solar, this one is lunar. [[Wait the Lunar Cycle]] covers how the waiting is done, and [[Lunar Authority]] covers it as an authority.

## No Definition Is Not The Same Word As Reflector

They are the same people seen from two angles, and the angles are worth keeping apart. No Definition is a definition type, and it answers the question this section asks of every chart: how do the defined areas sit in relation to each other? The answer here is that there are none, so there are no splits, no bridges and no processing order to describe. [[Bridging]], the mechanic that dominates [[Split Definition]] and [[Quadruple Split Definition]], has no equivalent, because bridging joins two existing areas of definition and there are none to join. What a transit or another person supplies is not a bridge across a gap. It is the definition itself, for as long as it lasts.

[[Reflector]] is a Type, and it answers different questions about aura, strategy and signature. The material on the aura's resistance, on surprise as the signature, on disappointment as the [[Not-Self]] theme, and on the lived texture of the design belongs there. The two collapse into one another because definition is what Type is read from in the first place; here there is nothing to derive it from, and the absence is itself the answer. Definition is still fixed at birth and still no ranking. What changes daily is only which parts are switched on by the transit field or by whoever is in the room, which is the design working correctly.
