# Single Definition

Single Definition is the arrangement in which everything defined in a BodyGraph is joined to everything else that is defined. There is no gap anywhere in the chain. The Definitive Book of Human Design lists it as one of the five definitions and gives the figure plainly: "SINGLE DEFINITION - all defined channels and defined centers in the design are connected in one continuous flow (approximately 41 percent of the population)." The Jovian dictionary states the same mechanic in four words. "Single Definition - A continuous connection."

Approximately 41 percent, and that number is stable wherever it appears. Ra Uru Hu rounded it the same way in his own lecture on the subject: "About four out of every ten beings have single definition." It is the second most common definition after [[Split Definition]], and between them the two account for the large majority of charts.

## What Counts As Single

The word single describes the number of pieces, not the amount of definition. It says nothing about how much of the BodyGraph is coloured in, and Ra was careful about that. "You can see somebody, for example, that only has two centers connected to each other and have seven other centers that are open. They're a single definition. You can have those that have three and four and five and six and so on, centers that are all connected to each other. This is single definition." The Jovian dictionary sets the floor at two: "Single definition is the continuous connection of two or more Centers." The Definitive Book describes the same thing from the energy side, as a chart "where the energy flows continuously through defined channels and centers without a break, connecting all defined centers in the chart."

What decides the type is an absence, not a quantity. The Definitive Book defines the alternative by what is missing: "A split in a design means all defined channels and centers are not continuously connected within the BodyGraph; a gate or channel remains open between two, three or four areas of definition." Single Definition is the case where there is no such open gate to find. Nothing internal is waiting to be closed.

## One Continuous Circuit

The consequence of having no gap is that the whole of a person's fixed nature runs as one system rather than as separate departments.

Ra described what it means "to have everything connected, whether it's two centers or any combination of centers together, it means that inherently the being has everything working together." The effect of that, in his words, has a particular texture. "It gives them a certain solidity. It gives them a certain sense of wholeness within themselves."

The Definitive Book says the same thing in flatter language, and adds the qualification that matters most. "Single Definition people are at some level self contained because their definition forms a single, continuous, constant, ever-present and reliable energy. These designs can have a very singular focus, and don't have the same need to reflect on different aspects of themselves that the splits do. Single Definition designs do not need others to assist them in the assimilation of information, or to feel a sense of wholeness. They digest information rapidly, unless of course they are emotionally defined."

Self-contained is the accurate word. Ra's contrast was with the Split, of whom he said "these beings are always looking to find the bridge in order to discover their wholeness." A Single Definition is not looking. As he put it, "Their dilemma is not seeking within themself some kind of bridge to bring them wholeness. Their dilemma is dealing with deeply powerful conditioning."

## Speed

Since there are no gaps to cross, information does not have to wait anywhere inside the design.

Ra stated the comparison directly when teaching the splits: "A single definition integrates information completely the moment it receives it." Contemporary teaching agrees, and is careful to frame it as timing rather than capacity, describing Single Definition as more immediate in its internal processing while the split types generally need more time for information to integrate across separate areas.

There is one standing exception, and the Definitive Book builds it into the sentence: Single Definition designs digest information rapidly, "unless of course they are emotionally defined." A defined [[Solar Plexus Center]] lays its own wave over any definition type, and no amount of internal connectedness overrides waiting for emotional clarity. [[Emotional Authority]] still sets the clock.

The everyday cost of the speed is other people. Contemporary teaching flags it as a pattern in group settings and teaching work: a Single Definition assumes everyone else processes at the same rate, and gets impatient with those who need to hear a thing twice. The world can feel as though it is moving slowly. That is not the world being slow.

## Where The Conditioning Gets In

This is the part of the topic Ra spent the most time on, and it is the reason he taught Single Definition as its own subject at all.

Because a Single Definition has no gap for another person to close, [[Bridging]] is not their conditioning route. The pressure comes in through the openness instead, and Ra described its scale in the strongest terms he used anywhere on the subject. Speaking to anyone with this definition, he said that "every center in your design that's white, every one of them, every one of them that is not colored in, each and every one of these centers is a dominant conditioning field in your life. Dominant."

He then walked it through centre by centre. A Single Definition with an undefined [[Heart Center]] meets a request they do not want to accept, and the mind supplies the reason to accept it anyway, in order to look like a good friend, a good lover, a good child. One with an undefined [[G Center]] spends a life "always looking around and over your shoulder, thinking there's something else." One with an undefined [[Sacral Center]] does not know when enough is enough, and keeps going until exhaustion.

His framing for all of this is worth keeping. "Those activations that you have on the design side, on the personality side, this is you, the student. Those centers that are open, this is where you go to school." Openness is not a defect. It is receptivity, and over a life lived correctly it is where the wisdom comes from. He spoke about his own chart in those terms: "I have an open emotional system, I have an open sacral, I have a totally open sacral, no activations. I understand both sides of that, and I'm a single definition."

But that is the outcome of decades of correctness, not the default. The default runs the other way, and Ra put it bluntly, that "for those beings that have single definition, their whole life is about a conflict with their mind." The irony he kept returning to is that the consistent, reliable part of the design is not the part causing the trouble. "It's not your definition that's doing that to you. Your definition is sitting there waiting for you to trust it because that's you."

## Standing Alone

Ra's strongest positive claim about this definition concerns self-sufficiency, and he made it without hedging: "the single definitions, they are here by design to stand alone, alone, they can always stand alone, always." He attached a condition to it in the next breath. "As a matter of fact, only when they feel good in their standing alone, are they ready, you know, to embrace whatever their design pulls them towards."

That is the order. Being at ease in one's own company comes first, and connection follows from it rather than substituting for it. The payoff he described is access to something already present rather than something acquired. "There is this extraordinary ability once you're there, once you've got it. You know, to really be able to live out that uniqueness, it's not about needing somebody else to make a bridge for you."

## Self-Containment Is Not Superiority

Nothing about being unsplit is an advantage over being split, and the mechanic invites that misreading, so it is worth stating directly. Contemporary teaching puts it flatly: definition is not about intelligence or capability, only about how energy integrates internally. A design that takes longer to synthesise is not a lesser design. The split types are the ones carrying the human pull toward pair bonding and company, and the Single Definition's independence is a different assignment rather than a better one.

The failure mode runs in the opposite direction from the splits. Self-containment that goes unexamined reads to other people as not needing them at all. Contemporary teaching describes the Single Definition as a self-contained unit whose energy is not looking outward for anything, and notes that the same quality is what makes them want to work alone and makes group settings frustrating. From the outside it can land as inflexibility.

So being able to stand alone is not the same as being right, and processing quickly is not the same as deciding well. Both still run through [[Strategy]] and [[Inner Authority]], exactly as they do for every other definition.

## Living It

Ra's practical instruction for Single Definition is one move, repeated. In his words, "if you have a single definition, the most important thing that you can do is look at your open centers." Learn the not-self keynote attached to each one, and watch for it in the moment it starts making its argument. That is the whole of the specific work.

The rest is the same work everyone does. "If you have a single definition, there is one way around the dilemma of mind and conditioning: strategy and authority." Definition is not a decision-making tool and was never meant to be one. It is the description of what will not move, and Ra treated that as its entire value: "the only thing that's going to be consistent in your life is your definition."

What sits on the other side of [[Deconditioning]] is not a new design. It is the same design, finally running itself. Ra described it as getting "to see the wonder of what it is to live out being a single definition. Being whole. And being able to fulfill your purpose, your differentiated purpose on this plane."
