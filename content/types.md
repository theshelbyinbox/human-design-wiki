# Energy Types

Type is the most fundamental layer of a Human Design chart. It is determined by which [[Centers]] are defined and how that definition connects to the Throat Center. Type sets the aura, which is how a person's energy meets the world, and it determines the [[Strategy]] that person is designed to live by, along with their [[Signature]] and [[Not-Self]] themes.

There are five types. There are only four auras, because Generators and Manifesting Generators share one. That distinction is worth holding onto, because the aura is the part other people actually meet.

Your type does not change. Knowing it and living it correctly is the foundation everything else is built on.

## How Common Each Type Is

Sources genuinely disagree here, and none of the figures are census data. They are estimates drawn from chart samples, lectures and reference works, so the honest answer is a range rather than a number.

One aura reference document derived from a Ra.TV presentation gives a single internally consistent set that totals one hundred percent: 66.30 percent Generator and Manifesting Generator combined, 22.5 percent Projector, 9.7 percent Manifestor and 1.4 percent Reflector.

Ra Uru Hu's own figures in lectures are rounder and not always the same from one talk to the next. For the sacral types together he said "approximately two-thirds of the human population" in one place, "about 67 percent of the human population" in another, and "about 70% of humanity" in a third. For Manifestors he gave both "about 8% of humanity" and about nine percent. For Reflectors he gave "around 1.3 or 1.4%". Contemporary teaching material puts the combined sacral figure at "about 66 to 70% of the population combined".

Taken together the workable bands are roughly 66 to 70 percent for Generators and Manifesting Generators, roughly 20 to 22 percent for Projectors, roughly 8 to 11 percent for Manifestors, and roughly 1 to 1.4 percent for Reflectors. The one figure that matters socially is the pairing: Generators and Projectors together are close to ninety percent of the species, so most human contact is an enveloping aura meeting a focused one.

## Pages in This Section

The five types:

- [[Generator]] : defined Sacral with no motor to the Throat. Responds. Satisfaction is the signature, frustration the not-self.
- [[Manifesting Generator]] : defined Sacral plus a motor to the Throat. Responds and then informs. Faster, and able to skip a step.
- [[Manifestor]] : a motor to the Throat without a defined Sacral. Informs. Peace is the signature, anger the not-self.
- [[Projector]] : no defined Sacral and no motor to the Throat. Waits for the invitation. Success is the signature, bitterness the not-self.
- [[Reflector]] : no defined centers at all. Waits a lunar cycle. Surprise is the signature, disappointment the not-self.
